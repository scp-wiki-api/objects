**Item #:** SCP-9232

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9232 is to be kept within a modest one bedroom 19th century styled chamber at Site-19 upon request by SCP-9232. Should SCP-9232 be removed from his Site-19 chamber, SCP-9293 must be given a thorough explanation for why and be told when he will return. Everyday by at least 9 A.M, SCP-9232 must be given the daily paper to prevent further harassment of staff.

 If in good behavior, SCP-9232 is permitted to receive low-level classified documents from the current Presidential Administration pertaining to the Department of State affairs. SCP-9232 upon request, should receive “3 fine meals a day” that are routinely switched to prevent “the staleness of a repetitive cuisine”. SCP-9232 is to be occasionally monitored, with staff being encouraged to check in on the camera in his room at least once every 3 hours.

**Description:** SCP-9232 is 27th Secretary of State, William M. Evarts. SCP-9232 is biologically an adult male aged 70-80 years, however his true age is closer to that of 200 as reported his birth certificate and historical documents. It is currently unknown how SCP-9232 does not age, and further testing will be done by Site-19 staff. The height of SCP-9232 is approximately 5.7 inches tall, with his weight being 125 pounds. SCP-9232 has shown no indication of revealing how he obtained his so called “miracle immortality” and has refused any further questioning from site staff. SCP-9232 has however occasionally provides staff with information about his past pertaining to the Johnson and Hayes Administration, along with experiences of his time in 19th century Great Britain. SCP-9232 has been described as an ill-tempered old man who possesses high intellect skills.

SCP-9232 enjoys talking about his experiences in late 19th century American politics, notably having extreme distain when talking about lawyer Charles O’Conor and Republican Roscoe Conkling, the gentleman who are the source for a substantial amount of his rants to staff. The only notable exception to SCP-9232 usual policy of not using physical force was when he flung his walking cane at the security camera situated in his bedroom due to apparent “fucking ceiling dampness”.

**————————————————————————————————————————————**

**History:** SCP-9232 was discovered by the ████████ Police Department in August of 2001 in the township of ██████. SCP-9232 was arrested for harassment of an officer and refusal of arrest. Upon interrogation, the ████████ Police Department found absolutely zero background information of SCP-9232, turned the NCIC for assistance in prosecution. With there being no databases available for SCP-9232, the prosecution process became unusually suspicious which caught the attention of FBI. Upon examination of SCP-9232 facial features, it became a theory to investigators that this individual was possibly a relative of 27th U.S. Secretary of State, William M. Evarts. Upon blood testing and finger printing of SCP-9232 and William M. Evarts, the discovery was made that the two shared not only consistent blood profiling, but identical fingerprints. Upon this discovery, the SCP foundation stepped in and took control over SCP-9232. 

It has been made clear to SCP-9232 that if he is willing to reveal more about his immortality, more documents and freedoms will be permitted to him. It is not recommended to not use modern 21st century slang around SCP-9232 as he will get noticeably upset and typically respond with some form of verbal abuse. SCP-9232 has requested to not be shown or interact with any SCP on Site-19. Upon being shown an image of SCP-█████, SCP-9232 referred to it as “a freakish gumdrop” with “an eye akin to a Cyclops from a Homeric tale”. 

**Addendum:** SCP-9232 is assigned to Dr. Van Persie for all questioning regarding his “miracle immortality” and requests. Further questioning will continue with SCP-9232 by Dr. Van Persie in the future.
