**Item #:** SCP-9241: Pigeon man.

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** once SCP-9241 has been summoned, he is unable to leave until a deal has been made with him. Therefore, if SCP-9241 were to be kept inside of a regular containment cell, with no interaction, he can be contained. However, it can still be summoned, therefore leading to SCP-9241's escape.

**Description:** SCP-9241 is a white male with an anthropomorphic pigeon head. Standing 6'4 in height and weighing 147lbs. Despite their head, they are able to speak coherent English, in a southern american accent. SCP-9241 is able to teleport at will, leaving behind a dead pigeon when it teleports. SCP-9241 is able to be summoned by sacrificing a certain number of pigeons and chanting "columba" repeatedly. The number of pigeons depends on the summon cycle, which was learnt from SCP-9241 himself. The summon cycle takes the form of the first half of a sine wave, scaled by 10, rounded to the nearest interger, therefore the cycle is: 0, 5, 9, 10, 9, 5, 0.

SCP-9241 is relatively calm and composed, and attempts to sell items to anyone near. These items are normally useless, however on some occasions he has sold incredibly powerful items, and has been able to sell other SCPs (when this happens, the SCP in question is instantly transported to his suitcase.) The cost of these items, are either time off the buyer's life, or a body part of the buyer. The amount of time/importance of the body part ranges from how incredible the item being sold is. Furthermore, SCP-9241 has the ability to know everyone's name just by looking at them.

Insulting SCP-9241 heavily enrages him, causing him to produce a flashing white light, which temporarily blinds anyone watching, and transforms the person insulting SCP-9241 into SCP-9241-1. SCP-9241-1 is a regular sized pigeon, with the ability to fly, however is able to speak English, and possesses all the same memories as they did before the transformation. There continues to be no known way to transform SCP-9241-1 back into a human.

**Addendum A:** A log of an interaction between SCP-9228 and a D-Class.

<details>
<summary>+ unfold</summary>

> SCP-9241: Hello, how are you today, ██████?

> D-Class: How do you know my name?

> SCP-9241: Lucky guess, now, I'm assuming you're here to do business?

> D-Class: Actually I was told to *not* do that...

> **SCP-9241 sighed**

> SCP-9241: Why don't you just take a look at what I'm offering?

> D-Class: ...Alright

> **SCP-9241 opened his suitcase, revealing a monopoly "Get Out Of Jail Free" card**

> SCP-9241: It only costs 15 years off your life, we got a deal?

> **The D-Class looked towards the one way glass**

> SCP-9241: Don't look at them, look at me. Do we have a deal?

> **The D-Class looked back at SCP-9241**

> D-Class: Fine.

> guards attempted to enter to stop the deal, but they were too late. A white light illuminated the room. Once the light dispersed, only a dead pigeon remained on the floor. Following the events, SCP-9241 was quickly resummoned, however the D-Class was never found.

</details>

**Addendum B:** A log of an interview between Dr. ████ and SCP-9241, the focus being of how SCP-9241 obtained their anomous nature.

<details>
<summary>+ unfold</summary>

> Dr. ████: Hello SCP-9241! How are we feeling today?

> SCP-9241: Good, Dr. ████.

> Dr. ████: I plan on asking you some questions, are you ready to answer

> SCP-9241: Go ahead.

> Dr. ████: Good, were you born with a pigeon's head? 

> SCP-9241: No, of course not. I used to be human, infact I used to be a researcher at this very site, brings back good memories, well except of course when I got ripped in half.

> Dr. ████: Can you tell me more?

> SCP-9241: Of course I can, my name used to be Dr. ██████████, I worked at site ██, I was killed by an SCP, but I had a family that didn't know my work, so they covered it up as murder and sold my body to whatever lunatic wanted it.

> Dr. ████: And which lunatic was that?

> SCP-9241: some cultists, they practised resurrection using animal remains, they believed that the souls of the dead animals may be able to be reused into a dead human, then its just basic resuscitation. All of their previous cult gatherings had failed, but for some reason this one worked. Now I'm this.

> Dr. ████: So that's why you have the stupid pigeon head?

> SCP-9241: Stupid? How dare you! 

> A blinding white flash illuminated the cell, transforming Dr. ████ into SCP-9241-1. Following the events, Dr. ████ was terminated.

</details>
