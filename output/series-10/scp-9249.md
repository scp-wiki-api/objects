**SCP-9249** - Faces.

**Author:** 

**[My Author Page](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/kef-s-personnel-file)**

[*https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/kef-s-personnel-file My Author Page]

**Item #:** SCP-9249

**Object Class:** Contained

**Special Containment Procedures:** The meadow containing SCP-9249 is to be surrounded by a perimeter of privacy fencing. A nearby observation post is to be manned by at least one SCP personnel at all times. Any incursion into the meadow must be approved by SCP-9249 HMCL supervisor on duty; otherwise, any trespassers are to be apprehended and turned away.

Those who have been subject to SCP-9249 are to be catalogued and undergo routine physical and psychological evaluation.

**Description:** Brought to SCP attention and successfully contained in 1958; however, historical documents indicate its existence long before. SCP-9249 is a series of motile stone monoliths bound to a meadow near Ypres, Belgium. Each instance is engraved with a face, typified by elongated, warped features--sometimes pitched at odd angles or with multiple lineaments layered atop one another. Each stone stands at around 2 meters tall.This figure does not account for the portion of each instance underground, which is hypothesized to be 4-5 times the height of the aboveground portion. The number of SCP-9249 instances is dynamic and changes on a regular basis; however, the figure typically stands anywhere from 3 to 30 individual monoliths.

SCP-9249 instances are capable of locomotion, though at an imperceptibly gradual rate. No trace or path is left behind. An instance's life-cycle will include materializing, roaming the confines of the meadow with no obvious destination or pattern for 5-10 days, and eventually dematerializing.The means by which SCP-9249 monoliths materialize and dematerialize is unknown and has yet to be observed or captured on tape. The engraved faces invariably depict elderly, bearded men, with expressions consistently described as "pained", "disgusted", or otherwise despondent.

Any individual within several meters of an SCP-9249 monolith will cause the particular instance to rotate and orient itself towards them. Upon several minutes of prolonged "eye" contact, an individual will fall under the paranormal effects of SCP-9249.

While subject to SCP-9249, afflicted individuals gain a statistically significant increase in favorable outcomes across unrelated events. These effects last for a time period correlated to the life-cycle of the SCP-9249 monolith which induced said effects. Examples of these effects include work promotions, coming across large sums of money, finding love, avoiding near-death incidents, the inexplicable resolution of health impediments, and more. Afterwards, these statistical paranormalities will subside and return to normal levels.

After affliction, subjects describe frequent dreams involving suffocation and being buried alive. Subsequently, these subjects will experience progressively degenerative vision, ultimately resulting in total blindness after 1-2 months.

**Appendix I:** A team of researchers attempted the excavation of a newly materialized SCP-9249 instance in an effort to further experiment and document its morphology. Upon the attempted uprooting of the selected instance, the team documented the following:

> Date: August 24, 1970

> Filed by: P. Zawaideh

> ~~-~~-

> Attempted excavation began by gently clamping and pulling upwards using a claw excavator; however the underground portion was much deeper than hypothesized and excavator arm was not long enough. Underground portion is significantly more than 10 meters in depth.

> 

> Newly visible underground portion of instance has base structure similar to that of tangled tree roots or veins. Entire surface area covered in thousands of secondary faces; sizes vary. Secondary faces near termini of braches appear gradually more organic in origin rather than engraved(?)--hard to tell, coated in caked mud.

> 

> Secondary faces all have similar facial expressions to those of typical SCP-9249 instances. Upon reobserving primary facial engraving, its expression had changed. Primary face now appears relieved; blissful.

> 

> While observing, excavator suffered a hydraulic malfunction and collapsed, crushing a team member. Soon after, another team member on-site experienced a heart-attack and went into cardiac/respiratory arrest, later dying in hospital. Word arrived shortly thereafter that a security compromise had occurred at Site-19. Decision was made to immediately rebury SCP-9249 instance; however, it had dematerialized whilst not being observed.

> 

> Follow-up mental/physical evals of SCP-9249 patients saw that multiple had regained vision. These individuals also report new dreams of paradise, weightlessness, and time spent with loved ones. No further activity has been observed.
