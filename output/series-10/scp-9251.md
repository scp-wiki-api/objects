**Item #:** SCP-9251

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:** Item SCP-9251 is to be kept in a room with floodlights that are on unless in testing. In the case Item SCP-9251 breaches containment use extremely bright lights to stop it from using its alanomalies effects. Testing is permitted with the use of d-class personnel.

**Description:** Item SCP-9251 appears to be a normal looking goose. Item SCP-9251 has the anomalies effects of every power humans have wanted for themselves. It has limited control over these powers and it is disoriented by bright lights. It is sometimes aggressive and sometimes is kind and caring but it hates structure and enjoys chaos. With a random reaction to most non anomalies effects.
