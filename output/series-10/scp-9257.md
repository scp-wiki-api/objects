**Item #:** SCP-9257

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:**

The surface land surrounding the instances of SCP-9257 has been acquired by the Foundation under an agricultural commercial cover. These areas have been divided into two concentric containment perimeters:

*   **Outer Perimeter:** A primary exclusion barrier established within a 25-meter radius around each cavity, designed to prevent unauthorized civilian intrusion.

*   **Inner Perimeter:** A scientific research and monitoring facility constructed around the anomalies at a distance of 3 meters from the epicenter, tasked with analyzing the constant flow of electromagnetic energy and bluish luminescence expelled from underground.

Direct access to the interior of the portals is strictly restricted to personnel with Level 3 authorization.

**Description:**

SCP-9257 is the collective designation for a network of vertical cylindrical shafts of unknown origin, located in a remote region of ████████. Each shaft descends vertically, connecting with the other instances through fixed underground tunnels. The walls of SCP-9257 exhibit conventional structural instability, with constant collapses of sedimentary material (common soil and clay) recorded into the interior of the anomaly.

The bottom of the cavities emits a massive and continuous discharge of bright blue electromagnetic energy. The system displays an invariability of the energy flow: the blue luminescence constantly passes through any obstruction or collapsed soil mass without suffering any loss of potency or alterations in its trajectory vector. Any solid matter introduced into the flow is instantly accelerated along the tunnel and expelled through another random instance of the network. This energetic emission is lethal to exposed biological organisms that do not possess adequate radiological protection equipment.

**Addendum 9257-1: Experimental Trial Log**

* **Experiment 9257-01:**

  * **Test Subject/Item:** Automated robotic exploration probe (Unit-01), equipped with electromagnetic shielding and thermal sensors.

  * **Methodology:** Unit-01 was suspended via a reinforced alloy cable and lowered vertically into the luminous core of SCP-9257's main cavity.

  * **Telemetry and Results:** Upon reaching a depth of 45 meters, Unit-01 was suctioned by a kinetic acceleration force exceeding the physical capabilities of the cable, causing it to snap. The video signal was instantaneously cut off due to intense ionizing radiation. After 4.3 seconds, surface sensors detected the ejection of Unit-01 through a secondary node located in Lake ████████. The probe's chassis suffered critical structural damage from impact at a calculated speed of 240 km/h. However, recovered data confirmed that the interior of the tunnel operates as a zero-gravity environment during the energy flow.

* **Experiment 9258-02:**

  * **Test Subject/Item:** Subject D-4492 (male, 32 years old, equipped with a radiological protection suit and lead-mesh electromagnetic shielding) and Subject D-4493 (male, 29 years old, without protective equipment, dressed in standard Foundation uniform).

  * **Methodology:** Both subjects were independently secured using automated traction harnesses and positioned at the edge of SCP-9257's main cavity. The protocol dictated the simultaneous insertion of both individuals' lower extremities into the blue luminescence flow to contrast the effectiveness of biological shielding against quantum radiation.

  * **Telemetry and Results:** Upon making contact with the light flow, D-4492's biometric sensors recorded stable radiation levels within the suit's safety limits. Conversely, D-4493's monitors reported an instantaneous molecular alteration in the exposed epidermal tissue, suggesting a process of painless cellular transmutation or disintegration. Before the research team could initiate extraction, the portal's kinetic acceleration force exceeded the tension limit of the physical harnesses, snapping the retention cables simultaneously and dragging both individuals into the system. Biometric signals ceased after 3.2 seconds of transit.

  Subsequently, Subject D-4492 was successfully located and recovered after being ejected at Node-██. Despite the protection, his suit sustained minor radiation damage, and the subject presented first-degree thermal burns on his lower extremities. Meanwhile, Subject D-4493 remains unaccounted for; his absolute disintegration due to direct radiation exposure during transit is theorized. Following this trial, the scientific department postulates that ionizing radiation levels increase exponentially within the tunnels due to the anomalous weightlessness conditions experienced during transit.
