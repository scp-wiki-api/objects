**Item #:** SCP-9258 

**Object Class:** Euclid 

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9258 is to be contained within a specialized primary containment vault suspended inside a secondary high illumination envelope at Site-19.

Due to SCP-9258's severe photosensitivity, high-intensity floodlight arrays maintaining a minimum 

continuous output of 10,000 lux must be directed from the secondary envelope inward into the primary vault. This continuous illumination serves as a passive physical barrier, suppressing SCP-9258's ability to use its anomalous abilities or attempting to breach containment, but in the case of a power outage, the backup generators inside the observation room are to be activated immediately as SCP-9258 is always active and monitoring its environment. Personnel entering the secondary containment envelope or conducting authorised testing are required to wear Grade-4 polarized eyewear during their time near SCP-9258, as direct unprotected eye contact with the anomaly is strictly prohibited.

**Description:** SCP-9258 is a humanoid entity appearing as a male adolescent approximately 17 years of age, lacking pigmentation, standing 1.75 meters in height and weighing 50 kilograms. The entity possesses shoulder-length black hair, black sclera with green pupils, a bifid tongue, and retractable fingernails extended to approximately 2.5 centimeters. SCP-9258 is clad in 19th-century Victorian attire consisting of black paisley waistcoat, a double-breasted formal vest, and dark trousers, and remains barefoot.

SCP-9258 Continuously emits an olfactory  anomaly resembling necrotic flesh saturated with ophidian venom.

When hostile or distressed, SCP-9258 enters an altered physical state. During this transformation, the entity's hair expands into a structure resembling the hood of a king cobra (*Ophiophagus hannah*), its pupils become slit-like and red, and a secondary vocalization registers simultaneously its primary voice. 

SCP-9258 possesses several anomalous properties, including localized telekinesis, phase-shifting (invisibility), instantaneous spatial displacement (teleportation), and visual hallucination induction.

Additionally, the entity can manifest an autonomous serpentine organism, specifically a dark-skinned cobra king  (designated SCP-9258-A) from its oral cavity. Upon entering a human subject's oral cavity, SCP-9258-A enables direct neural host possession by SCP-9258.
