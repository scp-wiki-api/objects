**Item #:** SCP-9265

**Object Class:** Thaumiel

**Special Containment Procedures:** Through careful testing, a method has been found to retrieve any being or item currently stored within the room. Burning a log from a standard Juniperus Virginiana (Eastern Juniper) and sprinkling the red-colored ash onto the base of the tree will cause it to gently expel whatever is inside of the small room by opening a small tunnel into the room and lifting the contents out carefully with its roots. Attempts to transplant The Dreaming Tree into a chamber inside Site ████████ have been met with difficulties, as its roots began to plunge themselves deep into the Earth when staff attempted to dig them up.

**Description:**  The Dreaming Tree is a Juniperus Virginiana found in ███████ ███ ████. The Dreaming Tree appears in the center of an empty field with no other trees within 5 meters. Beings who see The Dreaming Tree are compelled to sleep underneath it, and those who do report nightmares of a forest covered in crimson ash. They cannot awaken without an outside force. However, the Thaumiel-class effects appear when the being is not awoken. In these cases, The Dreaming Tree will reach its roots above ground and slowly pull the sleeping subject into the soil underneath it. Careful excavation revealed that underneath the tree was a small room about 7 foot by 7 foot square cube made of living roots. Any being pulled underneath the tree is placed inside of this room without the need for air, food, or water.
