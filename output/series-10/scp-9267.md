**Special Containment Procedure:** The Telecommunications Monitoring Office will actively observe mass social media platforms for instances of SCP-9267. Outbreaks of SCP-9267 are to be reported immediately. 

Standard misinformation and personnel containment is to be deployed at verified instances of SCP-9267-1. Rapid response team Sigma-1 from the Department of Memetics will research, analyze, and develop a memetic vaccine package that will be immediately pushed onto all relevant social media platforms with the aid of the Telecommunications Monitoring Office.

**Description:** SCP-9267 is a fatal neurodegenerative memetic disease that is transmitted through social media platforms. Instances of those affected by SCP-9267 are designated SCP-9267-1. The symptoms of SCP-9267 begin within days of contraction, starting mild before rapidly increasing in lethality:

| **Timeline** | **Effect** |
| --- | --- |
| 0-2 days | Mild headaches |
| 2-4 days | Severe headaches and mild to strong fevers (38 to 39.9°C) |
| 4-5 days | Migraines and severe fevers (>40°C) |
| 5-6 days | Strokes and partial loss of function in the frontal and temporal lobes |
| 6-7 days | Total rotting and deterioration of the brain |
As of yet, there is no known means to treat SCP-9267-1 instances through standard medical procedures.

The first confirmed instance was discovered in 19██ on an internet chat room. Since then, documented instances of SCP-9267 has appeared across a variety of social media platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. SCP-9267 becomes endemic to a social media platform once an SCP-9267-1 instance has visited it. The spread of SCP-9267 is characterized by periods of slow irregular transmission and stochastic outbreaks amongst major social media platforms, not dissimilar to influenza outbreaks. 

The Department of Memetics, since first discovery of SCP-9267, has developed a streamlined method of discovery, research, and treatment of SCP-9267. A dedicated rapid response team, designated Sigma-1, under the Department's purview collectively has ████ researchers across all major continents ready to address any outbreaks. The team is tasked with identifying SCP-9267 instances whilst public discovery risk is low and inoculating their local population with a culturally relevant and viral memetic vaccine package. Such a package is spread via the same social media platforms where the SCP-9267 instance has been identified.

**Addendum-9267-A:** A History of Responses to SCP-9267 outbreaks

Although the vast majority of internet memes are generated by civilians, many of the most viral internet memes have been active efforts by the Foundation in combating SCP-9267. The following is a selected collection of responses that Sigma-1 has had for SCP-9267 over time. Successes and failures are included; with historical notes from Dr. Helena Tetrin, global coordinator of the Sigma-1, and other personnel on the team.

# Operational Review Board Investigation-9267-1

### Approved for general release

An Operational Review Board was formed to investigate a large volume of internal complaints levied against the operations of Sigma-1.

> **Interviewed:** Dr. Helena Tetrin

> 

> **Interviewers:** ORB-1, ORB-2, ORB-3, ORB-4; members kept anonymous under provision 3.4 of ORB policies.

> 

> **Foreword:** TBD

> **<Begin Log, 2/15/2025>**

> 

> **ORB-1:** Good morning, can you state your name and role for the record please?

> 

> **Tetrin:** Good morning. I am Dr. Helena Tetrin, global coordinator of Sigma-1, a rapid response team under the Department of Memetics tasked with containing SCP-9267.

> 

> **ORB-1:** Thank you. We have called you in today because of complaints received regarding the operations of Sigma-1.

> 

> **ORB-2:** Over five hundred thousand complaints worldwide in the past two years.

> 

> **ORB-1:** Yes... we have never received such a high volume of complaints in such a short period of time. Do you care to explain?

> 

> **Tetrin:** Of course. It is Sigma-1's responsibility to combat the painful and, frankly, undignified deaths caused by 9267. We have found that the most effective means is by spreading highly viral mediums containing a specially designed memetic package.

> 

> **ORB-1:** Yes, we believe the mediums that your team spreads is precisely the cause of the complaints.

> 

> **ORB-2:** They're [EXPLETIVE REDACTED] annoying. 

> 

> **Tetrin:** They are necessary, ma'am. 

> 

> **ORB-1:** Well, nearly all of the complaints share a similar sentiment that ORB-2 has expressed. Personnel with young children have reported irritation, headaches, and sometimes lack of sleep due to the efforts coming out of Sigma-1. Let's see if I have an example here...

> 

> [Sound of many papers shuffling]

> 

> **ORB-1:** Okay. Trala- Tralalalo- How do you read this?

> 

> **ORB-2:** [Sigh] Tralalero Tralala.

> 

> **ORB-1:** Tralalaro Tralala [sic], yes. Was there truly a need to push this as hard as you did? 

> 

> **Tetrin:** I understand the complaints, sir, but SCP-9267 has only grown in severity and strength since our initial discovery and attempts to contain it. We have to push these packages *extremely* aggressively to ensure enough people are inoculated. We are currently researching a more permanent containment method for SCP-9267.

> 

> **ORB-1:** But this outbreak has been contained?

> 

> **Tetrin:** Correct, the outbreak identified on December 11th, 2024 has been contained.

> 

> **ORB-4:** Okay, so can't you now just get rid of this? This, uh, Trelelaro Trelala [sic]? Or at least stop it from being so popular anymore?

> 

> **Tetrin:** Unfortunately, sir, with most of Sigma-1's efforts they cannot be so easily stopped. These packages are designed to be placed with the most viral memetic mediums. The best way that the Department of Memetics has for preventing the spread of a highly memetic idea is to create one even more memetically compelling. 

> 

> **ORB-2:** [Groan]

> 

> **ORB-1:** We understand the position you are in, Dr. Tetrin, and it's not an easy one. The ORB will conduct an independent review of the operations of Sigma-1 and your testimony here will be accounted for in any final decisions we make.
