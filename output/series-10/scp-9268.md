**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9268 must not be observed, neither directly nor by live camera feed. All direct documentation of SCP-9268, such as video recordings and photographic images, is to be stored in a reinforced secure locker in Site-500 Area-02 located at ██████. Access to these materials is authorized for Level 5 clearance only.

If any direct documentation of SCP-9268, such as video recordings and photographic images, gets observed by a wider scale of personnel that are below the necessary clearance level, PROTOCOL: AMBER is to be immediately executed. Interacting with personnel affected by the agent is prohibited. MTF Eta-10 ("See No Evil") and MTF Eta-11 ("Savage Beasts") are to be immediately dispatched if PROTOCOL: AMBER is executed.

All containment efforts have been impaired due to SCP-9268 posing immediate and or often irreversible danger to personnel. Personnel with schizophrenia or other conditions that have visual hallucinations are prohibited from accessing SCP-9268. No testing is to be performed by any personnel at any time.

**Description:** SCP-9268 is consistently described by individuals affected by SCP-9268 as the skull of an Odocoileus virginianus.white-tailed deer

Additional information about SCP-9268's physical appearance has not been given at this time. ##bf0606|**You have it right there. :(**##

SCP-9268's primary anomalous property is a cognitohazard that urges to anyone who makes visual, auditory or informational contact with it to take their own life.See Case File: Liability

SCP-9268 can also urge anyone to take another person's life by suicide or any other means, which includes committing acts of violence.See Case File: Code Gray

After initial exposure to SCP-9268, the effects of the cognitohazard can take between a few seconds and a couple minutes to fully manifest inside the victim.

##000000|**Initial Exposure List:**##

##000000| · Decreased morality & empathy##

##000000| · Increased hallucinations *(schizophrenia affected subjects are exclusively affected)*##

##000000| · Increased fear & dissociation##

It is theorized that after initial exposure, any of the behaviors above that any human within line of sight of SCP-9268 experiences include nausea, increased suicidal behavior, and an urge to self-harm. Affected subjects presenting these behaviors are henceforth designated SCP-9268-1.

Due to this, complete removal of SCP-9268's cognitohazard from a subject necessitates termination. ##bf0606|**They have tried amnestics,**## ##bf0606|**it didn't work very well hehe :D.**##

Any affected subjects upon arrival must be found and terminated immediately.
