Object Class: Euclid

Containment procedure: SCP 9276 must not be removed from its original box at all costs.

Access Level: Class D personnel may be subjected to testing with the console for study of the absorption into the hardware.

Description: Atari Video Computer System manufactured in 1980, 4 wooden knobs with various game settings, standard controller outlet, RF jack, synthetic wood vinyl on the front, painted in the margins with orange paint and grey paint respectively.

ADDENDUM 9276-1: DISCOVERY

FOUND AT: 13157 ██████ ln, Campbell, Santa Clara, CA, USA

SCP-9276 Recovered during ongoing investigation of a missing persons case, Dez ██████. Current testimony provided that the house was still, people home albeit quiet. On an unsuspecting summer night, Dez was suspected to have run away. Investigation on 08/██/84 found in his room an atari-2600 still on from the night of his disappearance. Investigators photograph the still glowing screen, the console still powered as well as the room surrounding them. Investigators feel the console for warmth, but feel nothing. Strange from a console that had been on for days by this point. 

INCIDENT 9276-ALPHA

DATE : 08/██/84

As a small investigation team consisting of officers Leo "████" chambers, Nate ██████, ██████ Coburn, and Autumn E. ████████ aims to unplug the console to send it to evidence to analyze the clock data to see the last usage, but even after all cables are removed between the television and the console, the console remains powered, still posting an image to the screen. As soon as the team begins discussing the anomalous nature of the state of the power, officers begin disappearing one by one nearly out of thin air- as if they were pixelating into pure obscenity. Only one member of the team escaped; ██████ █████. Accounts from surviving victim include that there was a strong, bounding feeling towards the console- and as she watched her cohorts slowly gamify, she was lucky enough to make it up the stairwell and far enough from the anomaly that she didn't become victim as well. She also stated that the game slot seemed to have been cemented shut - only able to play one game, Night Driver.
