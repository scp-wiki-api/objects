**Item #:** SCP-9821

**Object class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9821 samples are to be kept in a 2x2x2 meter cube, made of [REDACTED] alloy. No less than two (2) level 3 personnel are to be stationed inside of SCP-9821's containment cell at any given time. All personnel stationed must undergo a standard quarentine screening after departure from the cell.

**Description:** SCP-9821 is highly infectious form of [REDACTED]. Resembling the common strain of the disease under a microscope, SCP-9821 causes a variety of symptoms in it's infectees, including, but not limited to:

* Fever

* Nausea

* Vomiting

* Conditions similat to early SCP-610 infection

* Auditory hallucinations

* Visual hallucinations

* Olfactory hallucinations

* Death

* Reanimation

* Extreme lethargy

Infectees (hereafter refered ro as SCP-9821-1) exposed to SCP-9821 quickly undergo three stages of infection, only the third of which is visible as anomalous to civilian medical professionals.

**Stage 1:**  begins to show symptoms similar to the 2020 strain of [REDACTED]. Fever, nausea, dehydration, dry eyes, etc. SCP-9821-1 instances typically enter this stage within three hours of infection

**Stage 2:** SCP-9821-1 begins to manifest symptoms similar to early-stage SCP-610 infection. Instances begin to show extreme reclusiveness, with the exception of other SCP-9821-1 instances. Instances that are grouped together will often be seen together, regardless of time of infection. This stage is usually entered around 4 days after initial infection.
