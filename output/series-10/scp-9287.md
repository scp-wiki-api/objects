**Item #:** SCP-9287

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9287 is contained within a standard humanoid containment chamber at Site-06-03.

Interactions with SCP-9287 are restricted to personal witch Level 3 or higher security clearance.

Only researchers which security clearance level 5 and 05-members are allowed to speak about the SCP foundation with SCP-9287. Any other personal members are not allowed to discuss the SCP foundation, foundation operations or classified information which SCP-9287. any researcher violating this rules will be given amnesties.

**Description:** SCP-9287 is an approximately 30 years old humanoid male, 1.81 meters tall with blond hair, blue eyes. SCP-9287 shows an arrogant personality and often reminds others that it is better than them.

SCP-9287s anomalous effect shows itself whenever it interacts with one or more humanoid individuals. The anomaly only works with humanoid individuals.

SCP-9287 will always have slightly better stats then the subjects it is interacting with.

These include but are not limited to: strength, speed, reflexes, intelligence, knowledge, vision hearing, instinct, luck and many more.

Testing has shown that SCP-9287s capabilities cannot go below the average of an adult human being, even if interacting with individuals with below average stats. 

The only things that don´t change are SCP-9287s personality and external appearance.

**Addendum:** 

**Test 02.11.████**

**Subjects**

Dr. Shatner and SCP-9287

**Procedure:** 

Dr. Shatner played a series of board and card games against SCP-9287 including chess, checkers, poker and black jack.

**Result:**

SCP-9287 won every game. As said by SCP-9287 it has "never lost in a fair game".

**Test 07.23.████**

**Subjects**

SCP-9287 and [SCP-181](SCP-181)

**Procedure:**

SCP-9287 and SCP-181 were instructed to play five rounds of Heads or Tails. The purpose of this test was to find out if SCP-9287 could even have better luck than SCP-181s anomalous luck.

**Result:**

SCP-181 won all five rounds. This was the first recorded time of SCP-9287 loosing a fair game.

Immediatly after the final round SCP-9287 became visibly angry and repeatedly stating that the results were "impossible" and insisting that "SCP-181 was cheating". The test was canceled after SCP-9287 tried ataking SCP-181 and security had to intervene.

**Test 11.07.████**

**Subjects:**

Dr. ██████ and SCP-9287

**Procedure:**

Dr. ██████ intentionally started a conversation about the SCP foundation with SCP-9287, to find out if SCP-9287 could even now better about something he could never find out the normal way.

**Result:** 

SCP-9287 started telling Dr. ██████ secret information about the SCP foundation that were above DR. ██████s security clearance level. The test was then stoped imidiatly and Dr. ██████ was then given Class-B amnesties.

It was decided shortly after the incident to forbid any mentioning off the SCP foundation infant of SCP-9287 to personal below clearance level 5
