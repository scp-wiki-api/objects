**Item #:** SCP-9296

**Clearance Level:** 0

**Containment Class:** Euclid

**Secondary Class:** None

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9296 is to be kept in a standard humanoid containment chamber at Site-██. Due to SCP-9296's physical capabilities and combat experience, contact with it is limited to authorized personnel.

SCP-9296 is not permitted to possess firearms, ammunition, or other weapons while in containment. Its personal equipment is to be kept separately and may only be accessed by authorized personnel.

SCP-9296 is not permitted to leave its containment chamber without authorization from Site Security. Any requests made by SCP-9296 regarding access to Foundation facilities, equipment, or personnel are to be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Personnel assigned to SCP-9296 are advised not to discuss ongoing Foundation operations with it unless authorized to do so.

**Description:**

SCP-9296 is a male humanoid possessing physical strength and endurance above normal human limits. It was recovered at Site-██ following a raid by the Chaos Insurgency, severely injured and wearing damaged tactical armor of unknown origin. SCP-9296 was initially believed to be a surviving Foundation operative, although it failed to recognize Foundation personnel, locations, or equipment present at the Site.

During its first interviews, SCP-9296 claimed to be a member of a Mobile Task Force, although no records of the unit it described could be found. It stated that immediately before its recovery, it had been fighting on a battlefield in another reality and was involved in an operation against forces responsible for attacking a Foundation Site. SCP-9296 has been unable to explain how it arrived in the current reality, only stating that it appeared "too early."

Despite its circumstances, SCP-9296 has generally cooperated with Foundation personnel and displays behavior consistent with its claimed experience as an MTF operative. Further information regarding its past and the circumstances surrounding its arrival is currently being investigated.

It has a patch depicting a collapsing building, that is attached to his armor.

# Addendum 9296.1 — Initial Interview

<details>
<summary>OPEN INTERVIEW LOG</summary>

Interviewed: SCP-9296

Interviewer: Researcher Pielucha

Date: 13/08/2026

Pielucha: So, you said you're from 2033?

SCP-9296: Yeah.

Pielucha: You understand that it's 2026, right?

SCP-9296: That's what you keep telling me.

Pielucha: And you don't remember coming here?

SCP-9296: No.

Pielucha: Then what do you remember?

SCP-9296: A city.

Pielucha: Which city?

SCP-9296: I don't know.

Pielucha: You were a member of Beta-21?

SCP-9296: Lieutenant.

Pielucha: There's no record of Beta-21.

SCP-9296: There should be.

Pielucha: There isn't.

SCP-9296: Then something went wrong.

Pielucha: How do you know so much about the Foundation?

SCP-9296: You taught me.

Pielucha: Who did?

SCP-9296: You did.

Pielucha: I've never met you before.

SCP-9296 looks directly at Pielucha.

SCP-9296: Not yet.

</details>

Note from researcher: SCP-9296 refused to answer further questions about the year 2033. When asked if it believed it had travelled through time, it replied, "I don't know. I just know I wasn't supposed to be here yet."

# **Addendum 9296.2: Discovery**

<details>
<summary>OPEN INTERVIEW LOG</summary>

SCP-9296 was found severely injured inside Site-██ following an attack by the Chaos Insurgency. The Site had been partially occupied by Insurgency forces before Foundation personnel regained control of the area.

When questioned about his presence at the Site, SCP-9296 stated that he had been sent there to stop the Insurgency. it claimed that the operation was supposed to take place in 2033, seven years after his discovery.

**Researcher:** You said the operation was supposed to happen in 2033. Why?

**SCP-9296:** Because that's when I was supposed to be there.

**Researcher:** Then why did you arrive in 2026?

**SCP-9296:** I don't know.

</details>

# Addendum 9296.3 - Strength Assessment

<details>
<summary>OPEN STRENGTH ASSESSMENT</summary>

Following the events of Report SCP-9296-007, Foundation personnel conducted additional testing to determine the extent of SCP-9296's increased physical capabilities.

Testing confirmed that SCP-9296's physical strength can temporarily increase by approximately 2.35 times its normal level when experiencing heightened emotional distress.

The effect appears to occur involuntarily and has not yet been successfully reproduced under controlled conditions.

Further testing has been suspended until more information regarding the phenomenon can be obtained.

Researcher's Note: SCP-9296 remains cooperative with testing, but deliberately provoking an emotional response is not considered an acceptable method of experimentation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>OPEN REPORT SCP-9296-001</summary>

Subject: SCP-9296

Location: Site-██

Date: 13/08/2026

SCP-9296 was transported to Site-██ after being recovered from the remains of a Foundation Site following a Chaos Insurgency raid. Upon arrival, it was placed in a temporary containment chamber and remained under observation.

Pielucha: Do you know where you are?

SCP-9296: A Foundation facility.

Pielucha: How?

SCP-9296: I've been in one before.

Pielucha: Not this one.

SCP-9296: No.

Pielucha: Do you know why you're here?

SCP-9296: Not really.

Pielucha: You were found at the Site after the attack. Do you remember what happened?

SCP-9296: I remember fighting.

Pielucha: Fighting who?

SCP-9296: Chaos.

Pielucha: The Chaos Insurgency?

SCP-9296: Yes.

Pielucha: And after that?

SCP-9296: Nothing I can explain.

Researcher's Note: SCP-9296 remained cooperative throughout the interview. It appeared more confused by the circumstances surrounding its arrival than concerned about being contained.

</details>

Subject: SCP-9296

Location: Site-██

Date: 14/08/2026

SCP-9296 was interviewed again after personnel were unable to identify the Mobile Task Force designation it had provided during its initial interview.

Pielucha: You said you were part of Beta-21.

SCP-9296: Correct.

Pielucha: What was your unit's job?

SCP-9296: Reconnaissance and direct action.

Pielucha: Against who?

SCP-9296: Depends on the operation.

Pielucha: And your last operation?

SCP-9296: We were moving through a city.

Pielucha: Which city?

SCP-9296: I don't know its name.

Pielucha: You don't know the name of the city you were fighting in?

SCP-9296: We didn't use names.

Pielucha: What happened next?

SCP-9296: We came under fire. After that, I remember the building collapsing.

Pielucha: The building you were in?

SCP-9296: No. The whole area.

Pielucha: And then you appeared at Site-██?

SCP-9296: No. Then I woke up here.

Pielucha: You don't remember being transported?

SCP-9296: No.

Researcher's Note: SCP-9296's account remains consistent with its previous statements. No evidence has been found supporting the existence of Beta-21 in this reality.

</details>

<details>
<summary>OPEN REPORT SCP-9296-003</summary>

Date: 15/08/2026

SCP-9296 encountered SCP-555-1 during a containment breach. SCP-555-1 attempted to stop SCP-9296 after noticing that it had left its containment room without authorization. Neither entity was willing to back down, although no physical confrontation occurred at this point.

Several minutes later, a Class-D personnel member attempted to escape the evacuation area. SCP-9296 caught the individual and ordered them to stop.

SCP-9296: Get on the ground.

Class-D: I'm not going back in there.

SCP-9296: You surrendered.

Class-D: I changed my mind.

SCP-9296: Doesn't work like that.

SCP-9296 attempted to kill the Class-D. SCP-555-1 intervened before it could do so.

SCP-555-1: He surrendered.

SCP-9296: And?

SCP-555-1: You let him live.

SCP-9296: That's not how it works.

SCP-555-1: It is here.

SCP-9296 remains silent for several seconds.

SCP-9296: You're making a mistake.

SCP-555-1: Maybe.

The two subsequently engaged in a physical confrontation. SCP-9296's armor sustained significant damage before security personnel separated them.

Later that day, Researcher Pielucha asked SCP-9296 why it had attacked SCP-555-1.

Pielucha: You know he was trying to stop you from killing someone.

SCP-9296: I know.

Pielucha: And you're not angry with him?

SCP-9296 pauses.

SCP-9296: No.

Pielucha: Why?

SCP-9296: He did what he thought was right.

Pielucha: You respect him?

*SCP-9296 looks away.*

SCP-9296: I accept him.

</details>

Subject: SCP-9296

Location: Site-██

SCP-9296: Because I'm not there anymore.

SCP-9296: I don't know.

<details>
<summary>OPEN REPORT SCP-9296-005</summary>

Date: 16/08/2026

Following a security breach at Site-██, SCP-9296 was temporarily removed from its containment chamber due to damage sustained by the containment wing.

During the evacuation, SCP-9296 requested permission to assist Foundation personnel in securing the Site.

SCP-9296: Let me help.

Pielucha: You're not cleared for that.

SCP-9296: People are still inside.

Pielucha: Security teams are already handling it.

SCP-9296: Then let me work with them.

Pielucha: You expect me to hand you a weapon after what happened yesterday?

SCP-9296: No.

SCP-9296 pauses.

SCP-9296: I expect you to let me help.

After a brief discussion with Site Security, SCP-9296 was permitted to assist with the evacuation under direct supervision. No firearm was issued to SCP-9296.

SCP-9296 assisted Foundation personnel in locating several staff members trapped inside the damaged section of the Site. It returned to its containment chamber without resistance once the evacuation was completed.

Researcher's Note: SCP-9296 demonstrated a clear willingness to assist Foundation personnel despite having no obligation to do so. Its request for access to the Site appeared motivated by concern for personnel rather than an attempt to escape containment.

</details>

<details>
<summary>OPEN REPORT SCP-9296-006</summary>

Date: 17/08/2026

Following the events of the previous report, SCP-9296 remained in containment and was subjected to additional psychological evaluation. During the evaluation, Researcher Pielucha questioned SCP-9296 about its previous military service.

Pielucha: You said you were a lieutenant.

SCP-9296: Yes.

Pielucha: How long were you with Beta-21?

SCP-9296: A few years.

Pielucha: And what happened to the rest of your unit?

SCP-9296 remains silent.

Pielucha: You don't have to answer.

SCP-9296: They're dead.

Pielucha: All of them?

SCP-9296: Most.

Pielucha: What happened?

SCP-9296: The operation went wrong.

Pielucha: The one you mentioned before?

SCP-9296: Yes.

Pielucha: Was that when you came here?

SCP-9296: No.

Pielucha: Then what do you remember?

SCP-9296 looks at the floor for several seconds.

SCP-9296: I was still fighting.

Pielucha: And then?

SCP-9296: I don't remember.

Researcher's Note: SCP-9296's account of Beta-21 remains unverifiable. Its responses regarding the unit's casualties appeared genuine. Further questioning on the subject is not recommended without a clear reason to do so.

<details>
<summary>OPEN REPORT SCP-9296-007</summary>

Date: 18/08/2026

Following the recovery of SCP-9296, Foundation personnel arranged a controlled interview between SCP-9296 and a captured member of the Chaos Insurgency who had participated in the raid on Site-██. SCP-9296 remained inside its containment chamber throughout the interview.

Chaos Insurgency Member: So you're the one they found after we brought the Site down.

SCP-9296: That's what I've been told.

Chaos Insurgency Member: Funny. We didn't see anyone like you when we went in.

SCP-9296: I wasn't there when you entered.

Chaos Insurgency Member: Then you showed up out of nowhere?

SCP-9296: Apparently.

Chaos Insurgency Member: And they just locked you up?

SCP-9296: Yes.

Chaos Insurgency Member: Doesn't sound like much of a soldier.

SCP-9296 remains silent.

Chaos Insurgency Member: What? Nothing to say?

SCP-9296: I was instructed to answer questions, not respond to insults.

Chaos Insurgency Member: Right. Still pretending you're some kind of professional.

SCP-9296: I'm not pretending.

Chaos Insurgency Member: You know what I think? You're just another guy who got caught in the wrong place at the wrong time.

SCP-9296: You don't know me.

Chaos Insurgency Member: I know enough. We destroyed that Site, and you survived. That's it.

SCP-9296's expression changes slightly.

SCP-9296: You were responsible for the attack?

Chaos Insurgency Member: Part of it.

SCP-9296: How many people were inside?

Chaos Insurgency Member: Doesn't matter.

SCP-9296: How many?

Chaos Insurgency Member: What are you going to do about it?

The prisoner smiles.

Chaos Insurgency Member: You're locked in a box, remember?

SCP-9296 grips the edge of the table.

Senny: Gold.

Chaos Insurgency Member: Go on. Get angry.

SCP-9296: I suggest you stop.

Chaos Insurgency Member: Or what?

SCP-9296 bends the metal edge of the table with one hand.

The prisoner stops smiling.

Chaos Insurgency Member: ...What the hell?

Senny: SCP-9296, release the table.

SCP-9296 releases it and takes a step back.

SCP-9296: My apologies.

Senny: Are you feeling alright?

SCP-9296: Yes.

Senny: Your hands are shaking.

SCP-9296 looks down at them.

SCP-9296: I'm aware.

The interview was terminated immediately afterward.

A subsequent physical assessment showed no injuries to SCP-9296's hands. Comparison with previous strength tests indicated that SCP-9296's physical output had temporarily increased to approximately 2.35 times its previously recorded level.

The increase occurred during a period of heightened emotional distress. No comparable increase had been observed during previous testing.

Researcher's Note: SCP-9296 remained verbally controlled throughout the incident despite displaying significant agitation. The subject's ability to maintain its composure while experiencing a substantial increase in physical strength warrants further investigation.

Further testing is prohibited until the circumstances surrounding the phenomenon are better understood.

</details>

<details>
<summary>OPEN REPORT SCP-9296-008</summary>

Date: 19/08/2026

Following the incident during the previous interview, Foundation personnel conducted a controlled physical assessment of SCP-9296 to determine the cause of the temporary increase in its physical capabilities.

SCP-9296 was informed that the test would involve several physical tasks and agreed to participate.

Senny: We're going to repeat some of the tests you've already done.

SCP-9296: Alright.

Senny: We're also going to monitor your heart rate and stress levels.

SCP-9296: Understood.

Senny: If anything feels wrong, tell us.

SCP-9296: I will.

SCP-9296 completes the first series of tests without any significant changes.

Senny: How are you feeling?

SCP-9296: Fine.

Senny: Any anger? Stress?

SCP-9296: No.

Senny: Good. Let's continue.

Several minutes later, SCP-9296 is shown footage of the damage caused during the Chaos Insurgency raid.

Senny: Tell me what you're thinking.

SCP-9296 watches the footage in silence.

SCP-9296: People died there.

Senny: Does that make you angry?

SCP-9296: Yes.

Senny: How angry?

SCP-9296: Enough.

Monitoring equipment records a sudden increase in SCP-9296's heart rate.

Senny: Stop the test.

SCP-9296: I'm fine.

Senny: I said stop.

SCP-9296 takes a breath and steps away from the screen.

Senny: Did you feel stronger?

SCP-9296 pauses.

SCP-9296: Yes.

Senny: How much?

SCP-9296: I don't know.

Senny: We measured it.

Senny looks at the results.

Senny: Approximately 2.35 times your normal output.

SCP-9296 remains silent.

Senny: Can you control it?

SCP-9296: Not yet.

Testing was terminated after SCP-9296's stress levels continued to rise. No physical injuries were sustained during the procedure.

The results support the hypothesis that SCP-9296's increased physical capabilities are directly connected to heightened emotional states, although the exact mechanism remains unknown.

Researcher's Note: SCP-9296 appeared uncomfortable with the results and requested that further testing be conducted only when necessary. The request was accepted.

</details>

<summary>OPEN REPORT SCP-9296-009</summary>

Date: 20/08/2026

Following the physical assessment conducted on SCP-9296, Researcher Senny conducted a routine interview to evaluate SCP-9296's mental state. No physical testing was performed during the interview.

Senny: How are you feeling?

SCP-9296: Fine.

Senny: You sure?

SCP-9296: Yes.

Senny: Yesterday's test bothered you.

SCP-9296: It did.

Senny: Because of what you found out?

SCP-9296: Because I couldn't control it.

Senny: You weren't expecting it?

SCP-9296: No.

Senny: Has anything like that happened before?

SCP-9296 pauses.

SCP-9296: I don't know.

Senny: You don't remember?

SCP-9296: Not clearly.

Senny: That's probably frustrating.

SCP-9296: It is.

Senny remains silent for a moment.

Senny: You know you don't have to answer every question I ask.

SCP-9296: I know.

Senny: Then why do you?

SCP-9296: Because you're doing your job.

Senny: And?

SCP-9296: And I respect that.

Senny pauses.

Senny: That's more than I expected.

SCP-9296: Don't make it a habit.

Senny laughs quietly.

Senny: Fair enough.

SCP-9296 remains silent for several seconds.

SCP-9296: Senny.

Senny: Yes?

SCP-9296: Thank you.

Senny: For what?

SCP-9296: Not treating me like I'm already a threat.

Senny: You aren't making that easy.

SCP-9296: I'm aware.

Senny: But you're trying.

SCP-9296: Yes.

The interview concluded without further incident.

Researcher's Note: SCP-9296 continues to demonstrate a cooperative attitude toward Foundation personnel. Its interaction with Researcher Senny suggests that the subject is beginning to develop a degree of trust toward specific personnel.

</details>
