**Item #:** SCP-9297

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9297 is to be contained in a 12 meter x 12 meter room that is 45 meters underground, accessible only by a single elevator. The elevator door in the containment room is to be camouflaged to match the wall. No objects that can be construed as a bed are to be introduced to the room. The walls have been treated with rituals that contain it.

**Description:** SCP-9297 instances appear to be a shadow figure with a variable number of arms ranging from 2-8. The single instance contained by the SCP Foundation has 4 arms. All known instances of SCP-9297 only appear in [redacted], CA. Testing on non-contained instances is carried out in a residential home in a suburb of [redacted]. 

SCP-9297 is a psychic predator that hunts by phasing into an item that is then slept on by a human, typically a bed, which is then designated SCP-9297-A. Once the bed has been infested an instance of SCP-9297-B manifests. When a victim sleeps in SCP-9297-A and enters a REM cycle the victim will experience sleep paralysis in 100% of cases. SCP-9297-B is very similar to a phenomenon referred to as a “sleep paralysis demon”. Unlike the natural sleep paralysis demon, SCP-9297-B is observable by anyone. SCP-9297-B is incorporeal creature that can take on the appearance of almost anything except a natural living organism. SCP-9297-B reacts to the victim as though they were a solid object. Upon attempting to physically interact with SCP-9297-B, all other objects pass through it, personnel who touched it reported an odd sensation.

Upon manifestation, SCP-9297-B will attempt to execute the victim. The method of execution varies by which instance of SCP-9297 has appeared. 

After the SCP-9297-B demanifests, the instance of SCP-9297 leaves SCP-9297-A and then phases through a wall and demanifests, remanifesting elsewhere to continue the process.
