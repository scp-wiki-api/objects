**Item #:** SCP-9348

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9348 is to be kept in a standard humanoid containment chamber decorated and modified to resemble an academic learning space centered around the mathematics subject. Personnel who come into contact with SCP-9348 must be amnesticized. Personnel are not permitted to stay in SCP-9348's containment chamber for more than 10 minutes.

**Description:** SCP-9348 is a humanoid male measuring approximately at 1.7 meters in height and a medium skeletal frame. Subject often wears formal attire and rectangular glasses. SCP-9348's hair is gray around the sides and black on top.

Subject is reluctant to share any identification, simply referring to himself as "M" or "The Biggest M". Upon being questioned about the meaning of M, SCP-9348 grows incredibly angry towards the questioner (Now designated SCP-9348-1). Subject will stand stoically and point directly at SCP-9348-1, where shortly after SCP-9348-1 will combust into flames which will last for about 1 - 3 minutes. The fire emitting from SCP-9348-1 will quickly begin to burn the subject. Most instances of SCP-9348-1 are quick to experience cessation of life function, but it's still possible for instances to survive the combustion.

If any biologic lifeforms come into contact with SCP-9348 for more than 10 minutes, subjects (now designated SCP-9348-2) are prone to self-inflicted death. Usually by self-inflicted combustion. However, this can be prevented if SCP-9348-2 is administered amnestics. The effect of SCP-9348 is seemingly caused by having a knowledge of the subject.

SCP-9348 is prone to engage in extensive conversations with themselves for prolonged periods of time, usually lasting from 15 minutes to 8 hours. If this event is occurring, no personnel are allowed to come into contact with the subject until they have finished their conversations. Upon attempts to record these conversations, it was discovered that SCP-9348 has a deep passion for academic subjects, primarily mathematics. These conversations replicate that of an academic educator lecturing a class.

**Discovery:** SCP-9348 was discovered during the investigation of a series of unidentified disappearances in the suburb of Randwick in New South Wales, Australia. He was found standing roughly 5 meters away from a pile of burning corpses. Upon being approached, Foundation personnel combusted due to SCP-9348's ability to do such. Personnel unaffected apprehended the subject, where he was quickly taken to the nearest Containment Site for temporary holding due to pending procedures.
