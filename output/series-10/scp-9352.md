**Item #:** SCP-9352

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:**

SCP-9352 is contained in a standard humanoid containment chamber measuring 5m x 5m x 5m at Site-198. The chamber includes a standard bed and bedding, a wooden table, a wooden chair, four high-definition cameras, and a steel delivery tube. The tube has a diameter of approximately 12 cm, uses a hydraulic system to deliver food, and a negative pressure system to remove waste.

The chamber walls consist of an outer layer of 40 cm reinforced concrete, a middle layer of 200 mm martensitic age-hardened steel coated with thermally sprayed aluminum oxide ceramic, and an inner layer of 50 mm boron-tungsten composite armor.When the subject is in humanoid form with a stable expression, any damage to the chamber walls must be repaired immediately.

**Testing Protocols:**

- **Testing Frequency:** Every Monday

- **Testing Conditions:** Written approval from a Level-3 researcher

- **Testing Method:** Communication attempts with D-Class personnel accompanied by two security personnel

- **Termination Conditions:** The experiment is to be stopped if the subject shows limb deformation, height increase, jaw extension, arachnid features, or any behavior inconsistent with human traits.

- **Resumption Conditions:** Testing can be resumed with written approval from a Level-3 researcher once the subject is calm, maintains a normal facial expression, returns to a normal humanoid form, and remains stable for 72 hours.

**Emergency Response Protocols:**

1. If the subject shows aggressive intent or takes an arachnid form, security personnel are to trip the subject, leave the containment chamber within two minutes, and switch to camera observation.

2. If the subject attempts to escape, the Mobile Task Force and security personnel are to intercept using force as necessary, but no weapons that could cause lethal harm are allowed.

    **Description:**

SCP-9352, the Undying, is a humanoid creature. According to information gathered by the Media Department, the subject once studied at 9352 University. The nickname 'the Undying' came about because the subject called themselves a certain classmate's mother, and when asked how to address them, this nickname stuck. Later, the subject apparently fell off the school rooftop while admiring the view. Local police found no body or any evidence indicating death. Police were aware of the nickname, though it wasn't widely used, and the subject did not appear to mind it. No prior history of suicidal ideation was documented, so it was assumed to be an accidental fall.

Later, the subject may have discovered an immortality ability. The Foundation has captured reports of similar creatures all over the world and tried to contain it multiple times but failed. On ██/██/20██, the subject was found near an unnamed village in the U.S., transforming from arachnid form into humanoid form and cooperating with containment. Based on witness-released information and videos as well as footage and descriptions from Foundation tracking agents, it is initially judged that in arachnid form, the subject has a jaw opening more than six times that of a normal human and can change forms at will. Currently, two forms are known: arachnid form and humanoid form.

The arachnid form of the entity is extremely violent. It can break most known objects that are highly rigid, with almost zero stretchability or toughness, and that barely deform elastically or plastically under stress. It can also consume most objects and shows extremely high immunity to harm. This form is known to have no consciousness and cannot be communicated with.

In its humanoid form, it appears as a normal human. Visually, the entity is about 180 cm tall, has a buzz cut, muscular build, and appears to be an East Asian male of Chinese descent. In its normal state, it shows rational behavior and can engage in basic communication. It wears a black, long-sleeved, hoodless jacket made of unknown material. While contained in humanoid form, the entity's cognitive function fluctuates unpredictably between high and low, with no discernible pattern or regular cycle. Its interest in food also comes and goes unpredictably, with no consistent pattern.

According to footage from witnesses and Mobile Task Force capture attempts, when the subject sustains a clearly lethal injury — such as severance of the head — it immediately loses vital signs and collapses. The lifeform that delivered the fatal attack immediately loses focus; its body twists, becomes disoriented, and proliferates, exhibiting extremely inhuman traits and becoming an irrational, uncommunicative individual. This individual is extremely powerful and indiscriminately attacks nearby lifeforms.

The attacker enters an irrational state (designated SCP-9352-A), and the attacked individual (designated SCP-9352-B) experiences extreme pain and may attack others. Those subsequently attacked by SCP-9352-B (designated SCP-9352-C) undergo rapid necrosis accompanied by extreme pain. When necrosis reaches its peak, the victim revives, only to continue decaying again, repeating in cycles.

The cycle does not self-terminate unless the affected individual commits suicide or is administered the developed antidote serum. Survivors treated with the serum sustain severe psychological trauma unless provided with long-term psychological counseling or Class-A amnestics.

After five minutes, SCP-9352-A gradually ceases movement, the hypertrophic limbs recede, and the subject reverts from SCP-9352-A back to SCP-9352, effectively reviving. At this point, SCP-9352-B becomes dormant and obeys the subject's commands.

If multiple individuals jointly inflict a fatal wound, they all initially become SCP-9352-A, but only one ultimately reverts to SCP-9352; the others either disappear or become SCP-9352-B. The determining factor appears to correlate with how the individuals perceive the SCP-9352 designation. Research indicates SCP-9352-B retains approximately 10% of SCP-9352's strength, yet still possesses immortality. Unless SCP-9352-B's mental control is lifted, it will permanently retain immortality and remain under command. The uncontrolled individual claims no memory of their actions; therefore, personnel must never kill or attempt to kill the subject, particularly not on multiple occasions.

Multiple experiments and eyewitness footage confirm that the subject exhibits extreme anger upon hearing 'SCP-9352' or 'the Undying.' The subject will switch to an arachnid form or use its humanoid form to kill any living being that utters 'SCP-9352' or 'the Undying,' or to destroy any non-living object emitting audio containing 'SCP-9352' or 'the Undying.' If a living being utters 'SCP-9352' or 'the Undying,' personnel should immediately attempt to trip the subject or lift one of its legs to bring it down, then escape within two minutes. If more than two minutes pass, the subject will stand up and pursue. If the living being is far enough away, the subject will revert to humanoid form. It is currently unknown why the entity stands up so slowly.

Although the arachnid form seems hard to approach, in reality, as long as you move fast enough, you can quickly get close and trip it. The subject seems to have very poor recognition of fast-moving objects, or its intelligence drops in arachnid form. You can also try pulling off its jacket and throwing it back to distract the subject and make an escape. Currently, it's unknown why the subject behaves in ways that seem so unintelligent.

Multiple studies have shown that the subject has a short temper and is sensitive. When D-Class personnel try to communicate with it, it often becomes inexplicably angry, switches to arachnid form, and causes extensive damage to the walls of its containment room. Once it calms down, multiple D-Class personnel are sent to repair the walls. Those who repair the walls should avoid interacting with the subject.

A Level-3 researcher suggested replacing the original iron bed with a more comfortable thick mattress for the subject and extensively applying a calming blue tone in the containment room to help the subject get enough sleep and reduce instances of irritability, along with the research results:

**SCP-9352 Experiment Records:**

## SCP-9352 Experiment Records

**When D-Class personnel mention 'SCP-9352' or 'the Undying':**

Trials: 10  Rampages: 10  Rampage Rate: 100%

**When D-Class personnel mention matters related to SCP-9352, such as its past or origins:**

Trials: 10  Rampages: 3  Rampage Rate: 30%

**When D-Class personnel play soothing music to induce sleep:**

Trials: 10  Rampages: 0  Rampage Rate: 0%

**When D-Class personnel wait for the subject to wake naturally after good sleep, then mention matters related to SCP-9352, such as its past or origins:**

Trials: 10  Rampages: 1  Rampage Rate: 10%

So, a Level-3 researcher deduced that sleep and a comfortable environment could reduce the subject's aggression. The Site-198 Containment Committee held a meeting to review the report, and the evaluation was ultimately approved. The Level-4 Site Director and project lead then looked over the review results, considering feasibility and cost, and finally approved implementation.

On ██/██/20██, after being in containment for 2 years and 7 months, the subject suddenly transformed into an arachnid form without any warning, smashing through the containment room walls and escaping. All security and MTF intercepts failed. Afterward, sightings of the subject occurred frequently around the world, with countless fatalities, causing widespread panic. Some daring civilians even followed the subject and recorded videos, which were discovered by the Foundation Media Department. The footage showed the subject walking into a corner, and the corner emitted a loud thudding as if something had fallen. However, the corner turned out to be a dead end with nothing that could have caused such a sound. Following this video, no new sightings were reported.

Two months later, the Media Department suddenly discovered a sighting report from an unknown IP address. The report sent a video and a picture. The picture seemed to be taken in an underground parking lot, suspected to be Backrooms Level 1. The main subject appeared to be SCP-9352 attacking a Backrooms entity called Smiling Nightmare in an arachnid form. The video loaded extremely slowly. Once fully loaded, it recorded from another person's perspective: someone in a yellow hazmat suit was taking photos of the suspected SCP-9352 attacking Smiling Nightmare. Then a few more people in identical hazmat suits arrived. Due to compression, the audio was unclear, but it was roughly possible to tell they seemed to be talking about the SCP Foundation, SCP-9352, and a novel. Afterward, they led the suspected SCP-9352 entity into a small room. Both parties were talking about something. At 3 minutes and 28 seconds, someone muttered a fuzzy "SCP-9352 can't die" comment, causing the suspected SCP-9352 entity to immediately transform into its arachnid form and start slaughtering everyone present. The originally gray room was stained red with blood, with broken bodies and hazmat suits scattered everywhere. The camera recorded the entire attack. After finishing, SCP-9352 rammed through the wall and left the room, disappearing without a trace. It wasn't until 28 hours, 36 minutes and 16 seconds later that people in hazmat suits came in, took a few photos, and took the camera. The video ended. No sightings occurred for the following eight years.

Since the Foundation did not know how to enter this unknown area, all that could be done was to intensify the search — there was no other way.。
