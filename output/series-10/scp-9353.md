**Item #:** 9353

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9353 is in no way a threat to human life and as such its containment procedure falls more under the label of being stored away on a shelf. Due to 9353s anomalous properties it is typically held within a soundproof bag, designed by facility engineers, when not being used.

**Description:** SCP-9353 is a small thin device, about five by two and only about a centimeter thick, and is completely smooth and featureless. At all times, no matter what, and seemingly with no need for power or any way for it to lose any it plays the song, "Genius of Love" by Tom Tom Club. It does this at a volume which never seems to loud but never quiet enough to where you cant hear the words to the song. 

**Additional Notes:** 9353 is occasionally used for prolonged enhanced interrogation on uncooperative D class personnel.
