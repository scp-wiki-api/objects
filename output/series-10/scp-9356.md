Item #: SCP-9356

Object class: Euclid

**Special Containment procedures:**

SCP-9356 is to be kept in a 8x8 soundproof titanium containment cell, with a 1x1 reinforced glass containment vessel set atop a 2x2x4 titanium viewing platform. Classified made sound-absorbing foam is placed on the inside walls, ceiling and floor of SCP-9356's Containment Cell.

**Description:**

SCP-9356 appears to be a standard bottle. Upon drinking any liquid from SCP-9356, SCP-9356-2 become infected and start to scream at a high frequency and causes deafness. All Infected Instances are to be terminated by order of the 05.
