**item:** SCP-9362-“Spam”

**Object class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:**

SCP-9362 is to be kept in a 10x 10x 10x 10 cell. Shelf’s are to be constantly viewed by scientists conducting tests on SCP-9362. Shelf’s are to be filled with 25 bowls of candy, 12 stuffed dolls and 12 porcelain dolls resembling children. if any of the 5 shelf’s go empty, class-D inmates are to restock shelf’s. 

**Description:** 

SCP-9362 is a hand-sculpted meerkat that possesses human-like behaviour.

Multiple Children refer to call SCP-9362 “spam,” perhaps these children are connected in some way? SCP-9362 still needs food and hydration to survive.  SCP-9362 is sentient and aggressive, mainly towards adults but specifically cherishes children. SCP-9362’s known origin dates back to 1776 when an anomalous artist sculpted SCP-9362 to teleport to abused children households to kill abusive parents using the same abuse methods used on children. SCP-9362 will then proceed to quickly kill the child so they feel no harm. SCP-9362 states that it is just “helping children ascend peacefully to the afterlife,”

If destroyed, SCP-9362 will reform out of site making termination impossible.
