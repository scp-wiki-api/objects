**Item #:** SCP-9374

**Object Class:** ~~Safe~~ Apollyon

**Special Containment Procedures:** There is no way to currently prevent SCP-9375, as any more attempts to prevent disasters it may result in worse incidents than we already have. Currently it is stored in a temporary object holding cell in the Keter containment wing of Site-██.

**Description:** SCP-9374 is a box made out of an unknown material with a phrase engraved on one of its sides. This phrase changes every 1 - 2 years after the main event happens, often being violent disasters, or disasters so large they can be considered anomalous. These words can be “ROTATION” or “DISCOVERY”, and events related to these phrases seem to occur. For example, on May 2011 the word “FIRE” would appear ob the front side of the box, and approximates 3 weeks later, a explosion from a nearby mine caused a massive, out-of-control wildfire. This fire would go on to spread straight towards Site-41, reaching the on-site generators, plunging the site into darkness and causing a site-wide containment breach. 

SCP-9374 is now contained on Site-██ after the warheads of Site-41 detonated.
