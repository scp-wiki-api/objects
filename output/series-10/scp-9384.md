**Item #:** SCP-9384

###990001|**Level 3/9384**##

**Object Class:** Keter

###990001|**Classified**##

---

**Assigned Site**

**Site Director**

**Research Head**

**Assigned Task Force**

Site-48

 Linda Lutz 

Nathan Nouriani

 E-33 "Ford Pintos" 

---

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9384 is currently operating within San Joaquin County, California, USA. A method of capture is currently under revision.

Standard media protocols are in effect to suppress knowledge of SCP-9384 from the public, with all mainstream internet browsers updated to prohibit access to SCP-9384-A. Mobile Task Force Epsilon-33 ("Ford Pintos") is responsible for persuading civilian clients of SCP-9384 to discontinue their contracts with the entity. 

**Description:** SCP-9384 is a humanoid entity possessing teleportation abilities, with the given name "Johnny Mendoza". Currently, SCP-9384 uses its ability to work as a freelance commercial truck and bus driver under the branding "Johnny-On-The-Spot". 

Commercial delivery businesses can solicit SCP-9384 through its website, designated SCP-9384-A. The website will contact the solicitor over email Attempts to trace messages to obtain SCP-9384's location have failed. Its email address remains the only known method of contacting SCP-9384, as it does not possess a phone number. , allowing them to arrange contracts, negotiate payment, and obtain a date and time for SCP-9384's services.

At the negotiated time, SCP-9384 will manifest in the driver's seat of a commercial vehicle pre-selected by the client. The entity will then drive the vehicle to its arranged destination in a non-anomalous manner. Following successful arrival, SCP-9384 will demanifest. Vehicles driven by SCP-9384 do not retain anomalous properties.

SCP-9384 is believed to possess a secondary anomalous effect, as civilians interacting with the entity, including its clients, do not recognize or acknowledge its ability to teleport into and out of vehicles. SCP-9384 also does not appear to require sustenance, sleep, or rest breaks, with daily work hours of between 21 to 23 hours a day, excessively exceeding federal hours of service regulations.

Initial contact with SCP-9384 through SCP-9384-A induced the following email response.

**To:** admin@speedycourierproviders.com

**From:** johnny@johnnyonthespot.com

**Subject:** Re: Hiring Opportunity

---

Dear Sir/Madam, 

Our promise to all clients is to be there when we need to be, at the beginning and the end of our journey. We guarantee that you will not be disappointed in our strong work ethic, perseverance, punctuality, and eye for safety. 

Our inbox always remains open for more delivery opportunities. Whether you need to replace a driver on short notice or want to replace an underperforming driver, we are always willing to welcome one more client into our family. Our short-notice and penalty free cancellation policy is yet another plus. 

To prevent misuse of our services, we will require additional client verification, including Employer Identification Number, commercial vehicle insurance provider, and the name of your supervising employee that we will be monitored by during each shift. Payment rates and deposit method must also be finalized before the acceptance of a delivery contract. 

Sincerely,

Johnny Mendoza

---

**Addendum 01:** Following approval from the Site-48 Director, Researcher Nathan Nouriani arranged a daily delivery schedule for SCP-9384 through the Foundation front company Speedy Courier Providers. Mobile Task Force Epsilon-33 ("Ford Pintos") was assigned to monitor SCP-9384 during these deliveries, escorting it and its assigned delivery truck in plainclothes and in unmarked vehicles.

Researcher Nouriani chose to ride with SCP-9384 on its maiden voyage under Foundation employment.

= **Transcript 9384-1**

**Personnel Involved:** Site-48 Researcher Nathan Nouriani

**Subject:** SCP-9384

**Location:** Speedy Courier Providers warehouse, San Joaquin, California, USA

**Date:** April 3, 2015

---

= **<Begin Log>**

---

*Researcher Nouriani is sitting in the passenger seat of the Ford E-350 box truck assigned to SCP-9384. He adjusts the recording device on his tie.*

*As he looks downwards, SCP-9384 manifests in the driver's seat. Researcher Nouriani looks up in surprise.*

**Researcher Nouriani:** Oh, what the-

**SCP-9384:** Oh shit, is this one of those- um... 

**Researcher Nouriani:** Whoa whoa, don't worry! I'm not here to rob you or anything. I'm the supervisor from Speedy Couriers, I was the one sending the emails and everything.

*SCP-9384 takes a deep breath.*

**SCP-9384:** Ah okay, so I'm not gonna be... alrighty then. 

**Researcher Nouriani:** Are you Johnny-on-the-spot?

**SCP-9384:** The one and only. 

*SCP-9384 grabs the ignition keys from the center cup holder, inserts them into the ignition, and starts the truck's engine. It reaches around for the seatbelt.*

**Researcher Nouriani:** So Johnny, how long have you been driving trucks? 

*SCP-9384 clicks its seatbelt into the buckle, then grabs the shift knob.*

**SCP-9384:** Three months or so. 

*SCP-9384 begins to navigate the truck out of the parking lot.*

**Researcher Nouriani:** You've made quite the name for yourself over three months.

**SCP-9384:** Well... um... that's the power of name recognition. And... doing a great job, and whatever. 

**Researcher Nouriani:** Very well. 

= **[LOG TRUNCATED FOR BREVITY]**

*The truck is traveling southbound on Interstate 499.*

*SCP-9384 appears to yawn audibly, covering its mouth with its hand.*

**Researcher Nouriani:** Hey, are you alright? Looked like you were about to fall asleep.

**SCP-9384:** I mean... with the hours I've been working, can you blame me? They need to start telling the clients to leave caffeine pills in the cup holder. 

**Researcher Nouriani:** Who's "they"? Your boss?

**SCP-9384:** Huh? Oh, um... I, uh... I'm talking about the guy who arranges all my deliveries... secretary! Right, my secretary! I... have one of those. 

**Researcher Nouriani:** I was under the assumption it was just you.

*SCP-9384 yawns again.*

**SCP-9384:** Nah man, who'd wanna make themselves work for so long?

**Researcher Nouriani:** You can't just take a day off, or quit, or anything like that?

**SCP-9384:** That just ain't how things work, man. 

*The truck drifts into the left-adjacent lane, causing the Epsilon-33 agent driving next to the truck to honk his horn.*

**SCP-9384:** The FUCK?!

*SCP-9384 swerves the truck back into its lane, jolting Researcher Nouriani around in his seat.*

**SCP-9384:** Did you- did you see that? That [Mitsubishi](SCP-4350) just tried to cut me off!

**Researcher Nouriani:** Did it? 

**SCP-9384:** I swear to Christ, people in this city can't fucking drive. Everywhere I go, I gotta share the roads with people who got their license out of a box of Cracker Jacks. You know what I mean?

**Researcher Nouriani:** Um... sure? 

**SCP-9384:** Look, I wanna... I wanna apologize. Shouldn't have yelled aloud like that, but you know how it is, right?

**Researcher Nouriani:** Are you... no offense, but are you sure you should be driving right now? We can always find someone else to hire. 

**SCP-9384:** No no no, don't worry man, that was just a one-time thing. You know how it is. Don't ever tell me you've never been that mad before.

*Researcher Nouriani does not respond.*

**SCP-9384:** You know how it is. 

= **[LOG TRUNCATED FOR BREVITY]**

*The truck is nearing its destination. SCP-9384 yawns again. Researcher Nouriani does not respond.*

*SCP-9384 stops the truck at a stop sign, then makes a right turn. As it does so, the truck scrapes against a parked sedan, activating its alarm. Researcher Nouriani turns around in his seat to look at the commotion outside.*

**Researcher Nouriani:** Did you just- you just hit that car!

**SCP-9384:** Alright, almost there. Where do I park the truck again?

**Researcher Nouriani:** You scraped that parked car back there!

**SCP-9384:** Huh? What are you talking about, man?

**Researcher Nouriani:** The parked car... that you hit... while turning right.

**SCP-9384:** Look man, where am I supposed to park this truck? 

*Researcher Nouriani takes a deep breath.*

**Researcher Nouriani:** Bay seven. 

*SCP-9384 stops the truck in front of the loading dock.*

**SCP-9384:** Alright, we're here! 

*SCP-9384 turns off the ignition, rubs its hands together, and looks upwards. Researcher Nouriani looks out the window.*

**Researcher Nouriani:** Hey, uh... you're supposed to back the truck into the loading-

*Researcher Nouriani turns to look at SCP-9384, only to discover that it has already disappeared. The seatbelt remains buckled in, with nothing to hold in place.*

**Researcher Nouriani:** Bay...

---

= **<End Log>**

Attempts to identify SCP-9384's alleged "secretary" were unsuccessful. 

---

**Addendum 02:** Further investigation into SCP-9384's history revealed a poor safety record, with the entity named as the at-fault party for multiple vehicle collisions. Notably, these incidents occurred in multiple states outside of California, suggesting that SCP-9384's teleportation range is not as limited as previously assumed.

A meeting was scheduled to discuss containment possibilities for SCP-9384.

= **Transcript 9384-2**

**Personnel Involved:** 

* Site-48 Director Linda Lutz

* Hazardous Material Containment Liaison Benjamin Bolton

* Lead Researcher Nathan Nouriani

* Researcher Detella Kovac

**Location:** Site-48

**Date:** April 6, 2015

---

= **<Begin Log>**

---

**Director Lutz:** Next on the agenda, we've got... SCP-9384, "Johnny-on-the-spot". Any updates, Nouriani?

**Researcher Nouriani:** I rode along with it last Friday, it nearly wiped out Agent Spago, it scraped a parked car, and it didn't even bother to park the truck properly. 

**Director Lutz:** I meant updates about its history, but I do congratulate you on surviving.

**Researcher Nouriani:** Right, um... Kovac, your presentation?

*Researcher Kovac opens up her laptop.*

**Researcher Kovac:** I've been looking into "Johnny-on-the-spot" as a company, and found advertisements dating back to 2011, far past the three months that it initially told us. Same logo and website, but localized in cities around the United States that are big logistics hubs.

*Researcher Kovac types on her laptop, pulling up a slideshow on the meeting room's projector screen.*

**Researcher Kovac:** All those ads would disappear after 9384 causes a crash, such as this [bus accident in Chicago](SCP-7025) that killed an undercover Foundation agent. I've read online reviews from trucking company managers complaining about it suddenly no-showing the day after it crashes. 

*Researcher Kovac switches through the slides.*

**Researcher Kovac:** And therein lies its pattern of behavior. It moves to a new area, posts ads about it, gets hired at multiple delivery companies, overworks itself, crashes, and then disappears from the area, taking down its ads and abandoning all of its planned deliveries. Then it reappears in a different state, posts more ads, and starts the cycle all over again.

**Director Lutz:** Now the question is *how* the subject was allowed to wander around getting hired at all these trucking companies if its driving record is *that* poor. 

**Researcher Kovac:** That leads to my next point: 9384 frequently changes its surname after becoming inactive following a crash. The Chicago bus crash report named it as "Johnny Thompson", another truck crash in Utah as "Johnny Velasquez", a third in Arizona as "Johnny Crinkleheimer". We know it's the same person because those ads were in the area prior to the crash, and disappeared afterwards. 

**Director Lutz:** But why hasn't its brand name been nationally dragged through the mud by, say, 60 Minutes?

**Researcher Nouriani:** My current assumption is that there's a mild antimeme at work that Johnny's trying to break through, but also allows it to drive carelessly, because the public will just forget about it causing accidents if it doesn't have ads plastered everywhere.

*Director Lutz turns towards HMCL Bolton.*

**Director Lutz:** Bolton, how are those containment procedures coming along?

**HMCL Bolton:** Besides the normal "paint over the ads" drivel?

*HMCL Bolton leans forward with his elbows on the meeting table and his hands holding his head.*

**HMCL Bolton:** I initially considered that we hire Johnny full-time to bring him under our thumb, but not if he drives so recklessly. Too much danger to the public. 

*HMCL Bolton leans backwards in his chair.*

**HMCL Bolton:** The monthly newsletter's full of other Sites chasing down teleporting anomalies. I keep submitting letters to tell them to just knock them out when they can and keep them in a coma, or even a cryogenic chamber, or at least implant a tracking device in their abdomen, but no one seems to listen. 

**Researcher Nouriani:** Good God, not this shit again.

**HMCL Bolton:** I've been talking to [Vernon](SCP-6384) over in the Thaumiel Development Department, his boys have been developing this new knockout gas that's *far* less risky than the stuff we've been using before. He's been looking for subjects that aren't D-classes, and what do you know, Johnny shows up when we need him. 

**Director Lutz:** Your point?

**HMCL Bolton:** I propose we outfit a truck cabin with nozzles to dispense the new knockout gas, then when Johnny teleports in to start driving, we gas him to sleep. Then throw him in a chamber with a nurse to make sure he stays in a coma. Problem solved, entity is finally contained, the streets rejoice. 

**Researcher Nouriani:** And what if it just teleports out of there before the knockout gas can take full effect?

*HMCL Bolton scratches his chin.*

**HMCL Bolton:** I'm planning on using a high enough concentrate to induce unconsciousness in a regular adult for at least five days. Even a second of breathing that stuff in will be enough for a regular person to become delirious enough to stumble asleep. And even if it can teleport away before the gas takes full effect, we'll just tell the other Sites to be on the lookout for Johnny-on-the-spot, with a five-day head start.

**Researcher Kovac:** Sure, but there's still the possibility that 9384 doesn't *have* to teleport into an unoccupied truck cabin. It could teleport to Timbuktu for all we know. 

**HMCL Bolton:** That's why we get this done the first time around. No second chances. 

*HMCL Bolton turns towards Site Director Lutz.*

**HMCL Bolton:** Director, we don't have to let teleporting anomalies wander around freely to crash trucks and buses with no consequences. The current status quo of passively following such anomalies around *needs* to end.

**Director Lutz:** Hmm... 

**Researcher Nouriani:** Director, the Ethics Committee isn't gonna approve such a proposal. We can't trap sapient entities in a coma forever, there's no precedent for such a thing. 

**HMCL Bolton:** Nate, weren't you just bitching about Johnny's poor driving? Would Ethics rather have him run over an innocent pedestrian because he was too tired to see them? Or T-bone an innocent family? Until the Foundation figures out teleportation, we'll never be able to *truly* contain such anomalies unless we place them in an extended comatose state.

*HMCL Bolton leans forward.*

**HMCL Bolton:** I remember reading about a reality bender who could walk through walls and used his power to attack innocent people in their sleep. Even when the Foundation contained him, he still kept escaping, until he finally got shot and killed during one of his breaches. I'd argue putting him in a harmless coma would've saved a whole lot of money in chamber repairs, healthcare costs for the injured agents, amnestics for his victims, the pain and suffering of their families, and even spare that wall-bender from an undignified death.

**Researcher Nouriani:** There's still the round-the-clock treatment that comatose people need to prevent bed sores, infections, muscle failure... that also costs time and money.

**HMCL Bolton:** Not as much as picking up after these wall-benders when they breach containment. 

**Researcher Nouriani:** Containment breaches are already budgeted for in-

**HMCL Bolton:** Nate, explain *why* the Foundation should waste time, money and lives placating teleporting reality benders when a humane solution is *right here?* If they run around harming innocent people, and there's no reason to suspect they don't have normal human physiology, why not keep them from harming others by inducing a coma that, mind you, we can reverse at any time for any reason. 

**Researcher Nouriani:** And what if it turns into a vegetable? What if it doesn't wake up when we need it to? Are you gonna take that responsibility?

**HMCL Bolton:** I can think of a lot of innocent civilians turned into vegetables, or worse, because the Foundation dragged their feet on taking the right step that they should've taken years ago to protect, *protect*, everyone from hostile teleporters and wall-benders. 

**Researcher Nouriani:** Look, I know I complained about Johnny's driving, but this is a slippery slope. We could start justifying mass comas of sapients just because they slip away from us one too many times. And if we keep Johnny conscious, it could share some of its knowledge with us if we work to build that trust. We don't have to lock entities like this away forever as if they were just... an "Are We Cool Yet" painting!

**HMCL Bolton:** And we won't! You're making a mountain out of a molehill... or better yet, out of a grain of sand. There's teleporters out there I have absolutely no intention of containing this way, but Johnny's better off catching up on sleep with us than falling asleep behind the wheel and wiping out a school bus. 

*HMCL Bolton turns towards Director Lutz.*

**HMCL Bolton:** Director, Johnny is the *perfect* opportunity for a trial run. He's clueless about us, he's used to just appearing in trucks so we can trap him easily, and he can't really harm anyone without using a truck to do the job. What do you say?

*Director Lutz takes a deep breath.*

**Director Lutz:** Alright then. This anomaly hasn't hit the main central database yet, so we should be able to keep this under wraps. But if it freaks out, escapes, and starts complaining to the media about us, that'll all be on you. Do you understand, Bolton?

*HMCL Bolton places his right hand over his heart.*

**HMCL Bolton:** Director, I promise you, I will do every single thing in my power to make sure my containment proposal goes smoothly. 

*Director Lutz takes another deep breath.*

**Director Lutz:** Alright. You have my approval. Nourri, make sure he doesn't mess this up. 

*HMCL Bolton smiles. Researcher Nouriani nods as he rubs his face.*

**HMCL Bolton:** Remember, Nate. It's a team effort. Don't mislead me on anything, nothing'll go wrong. 

*Researcher Nouriani nods his head again.*

**Director Lutz:** With that settled, next on the agenda...

---

= **<End Log>**

Following Director approval, HMCL Bolton drafted the initial containment procedures for SCP-9384. A suitable containment chamber was arranged, the delivery vehicle was fitted with concealed knockout aerosol equipment, and the decoy delivery was accepted by SCP-9384.

---

**Addendum 03:** On April 10, 2015, initial containment of SCP-9384 was performed.

= **Transcript 9384-3**

**Personnel Involved:** Lead Researcher Nathan Nouriani, Hazardous Material Containment Liaison Benjamin Bolton, Epsilon-33 Agents

**Subject:** SCP-9384

**Location:** Speedy Courier Providers warehouse, San Joaquin, California, USA

**Date:** April 10, 2015

---

= **<Begin Log>**

---

*Researcher Nouriani and HMCL Bolton are standing in the warehouse parking lot, looking towards the truck assigned to SCP-9384. It has been outfitted with a knockout gas system and hermetically sealed to prevent leakage. A sensor has been installed in the driver's seat that will automatically activate the knockout gas when SCP-9384 teleports in. Epsilon-33 personnel surround the truck, dressed in plainclothes as to not alarm SCP-9384.*

*HMCL Bolton checks his watch.*

**HMCL Bolton:** Should be about... now.

*SCP-9384 appears in the truck's cabin, triggering the sensor and activating the knockout gas. It first appears not to notice, then begins to cough.*

*Notably, SCP-9384 does not attempt to teleport out, instead tugging on the sealed truck doors. It looks upward and starts shouting, continuing to cough until it is rendered unconscious.*

*HMCL Bolton grins and pumps his fists in the air.*

**HMCL Bolton:** YES! Yes! I did it! Ha ha ha!

*HMCL Bolton points at Researcher Nouriani.*

**HMCL Bolton:** And yet you doubted me! You kept telling me it wouldn't work! "Too many complications"!

*Researcher Nouriani shakes his head.*

**Researcher Nouriani:** There's no guarantee that next time's gonna go this smoothly, too.

*HMCL Bolton scoffs.*

**HMCL Bolton:** I just saved you *months* of goose chasing, and I probably saved an innocent family out there as well, and you can't even say "thank you"?

**Researcher Nouriani:** Could we just... take Jonnny out of the truck and take it to its chamber? 

**HMCL Bolton:** Alright, fine, but honestly... if you just wanna spoil the mood, then you should just leave now. Me and the Epsilon boys have it handled. 

*Researcher Nouriani looks at the Epsilon-33 agents engaging in idle talk and card games as SCP-9384 remains in the truck.*

**Researcher Nouriani:** Very well... I'll see you on Monday.

*Researcher Nouriani turns around and begins walking away as HMCL Bolton waves goodbye.*

**HMCL Bolton:** Bye bye, Nate! I don't need naysayers like you around anyway.

*Researcher Nouriani approaches his Mazda 626, enters it, starts the engine, and drives away.*

*HMCL Bolton turns towards the Epsilon-33 agents.*

**HMCL Bolton:** Hey everyone! After we're done with this, we're all getting drinks! On me!

*The Epsilon-33 agents cheer.*

---

= **<End Log>**

Following capture, Epsilon-33 successfully transported and contained SCP-9384 at Site-48. Anesthesiologist Doctor Dylan Sitton has been assigned to monitor SCP-9384. Reclassification to Euclid is pending.

---

**Addendum 04:** On April 11, 2015, the Foundation received the following message.

**To:** admin@speedycourierproviders.com

**From:** johnny@johnnyonthespot.com

**Subject:** (no subject)

---

Dear Sir/Madam, 

Your actions towards us have negatively affected our ability to operate as a delivery operations provider.

We will no longer be fulfilling contracts with your company in the future. 

Sincerely, 

Johnny Nouriani

Following the reception of this message, Site-48 containment personnel re-examined SCP-9384's containment chamber, confirming that it remained comatose and had not breached containment.

Epsilon-33 personnel conducted a wellness visit at Researcher Nouriani's house; he was not present. His personal vehicle was eventually discovered emptyAt the time of discovery, the engine was still idling and the driver's seatbelt was buckled in. on the side of a road, only half a kilometer from the location in which SCP-9384 was initially contained.

The whereabouts of Researcher Nouriani are currently unknown.
