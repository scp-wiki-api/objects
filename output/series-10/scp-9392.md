**Item #:** SCP-9392

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:**

SCP-9392 is currently contained in 100m x 100m of forest area. If the SCP-9392 phenomenon occurs, the cemetery where it occurred should be isolated with fence and barbed wire, the barricades should be upgraded as the nights progress.

**Description:**

SCP-9392 is a phenomenon that affects cemeteries around the world, it is currently affecting a cemetery located in a forest in the USA. When a cemetery is under the phenomenon of SCP-9392 and the current time of the day is night, SCP-9392-1 instances will begin to rise from the graves at the cemetery, or in the case of SCP-9392-1-B will rise from the ground that is closest to the nearest human, SCP-9392-1 instances will only appear at night and each night new versions of SCP-9392-1 instances will appear, they are designated A to H. SCP-9392 cannot affect multiple cemeteries at once. The following are nights and descriptions of their events:

Night 1:

On the first night corpses (referred to as SCP-9392-1-A) will begin rising from the graves and sometimes places without graves but near the cemetery.

Night 2:

During Night 2 new SCP-9392-1 instances begin to appear, those being SCP-9392-1-B and SCP-9392-1-C. Personnel who enter are advised to wear hazmat suits to prevent being poisoned by SCP-9392-1-C.

Night 3:

Night 3 is when SCP-9392-1-D instances begin appearing, before the beginning of night 3 fences are recommended to be electrified with 30 000 volts of electricity. Entry during night 3 is highly dangerous, and is only allowed if necessary, in that case 1 security team is to be equipped with riot equipment, while the other team follows behind making sure no SCP-9392-1-D instances get behind to attack the first team.

Night 4 and nights after:

SCP-9392-1-E, F, G, H instances begin appearing during these nights, however unlike the first 3 nights, not in order.

It is important to note that as nights pass SCP-9392-1 instances will become increasingly stronger and more durable.

SCP-9392 will continue until night 20. On night 20 there are 2 scenarios that could happen:

The first one, which has 12% chance to happen is that after night 20, SCP-9392-1 instances will stop arriving on the following nights, and thus SCP-9392 phemonenon on the cemetery is over.

The second one which has 88% chance to happen is after night 20 SCP-9392-1 Instances will not stop arriving the following nights after night 20, and will arrive every night in larger and larger numbers. As the nights pass the "deads" will become increasingly stronger and durable to the point where small arms have little to no effect, and the Foundation will have to resort to using Tanks, IFVs, bombers, rockets, missiles, and even nuclear weapons.

However this doesn't mean SCP-9392 can't be stopped, it is proven that bombardment, destruction or the use of nuclear weapons on the area where SCP-9392 occurred can completely stop SCP-9392.

In case the SCP-9392-1 instances don't stop arriving in the following nights after night 20, the area is to be destroyed immediately after all personnel have evacuated.

After the area has been destroyed, or in case of the first scenario where SCP-9392 stops, the phenomenon of SCP-9392 won't recur for a period ranging from another 6 months to 2 years.

**Addendum:** [SCP-9392-1 instances]

SCP-9392-1-A: SCP-9392-1-A instances appear during the first night, they are slow, and a little bit more durable than humans, they attack by biting, punching, or tearing the flesh with their fingers. After SCP-9392-1-A has been severely damaged it will begin to crawl, however when it is struck in the head, it will cease all functions, and be considered terminated. This is the case with all SCP-9392-1 instances  except SCP-9392-1-F and SCP-9392-1-G.

SCP-9392-1-B: SCP-9392-1-B instances appear on the second night along with SCP-9392-1-C. SCP-9392-1-B instance will dig itself out of the ground closest to the nearest human but also not too far away from the cemetery and will lunge towards humans when it's close enough.

SCP-9392-1-C: What's special about SCP-9392-1-C is that it throws its body parts to hit humans, these body parts have a poisonous liquid designated SCP-9392-1-C-1, the effects of SCP-9392-1-C-1 wear off over time and can be treated. SCP-9392-1-C-1 is not very fatal.

SCP-9392-1-D: SCP-9392-1-D is very fast, and unlike others  will try to attack from behind.

SCP-9392-1-E: SCP-9392-1-E instances are usually armored, their armor can include  spartan armor, medieval, or modern armor, these instances are slower but more durable than others.

SCP-9392-1-F: SCP-9392-1-F are larger versions of SCP-9392-1-A, and are more durable and stronger than the rest, except for SCP-9392-1-G.

SCP-9392-1-G: SCP-9392-1-G are larger versions of SCP-9392-1-E and are stronger and more durable than any other SCP-9392-1 instance.

SCP-9392-1-H: SCP-9392-1-H has a scythe, SCP-9392-1-H is slightly taller than SCP-9392-1-A, B, C, D, E. SCP-9392-1-H can "summon" SCP-9392-1-B instances.
