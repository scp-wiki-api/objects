**Item #**: SCP-9394

**Object class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9394 is to be contained in its 2 m x 1 m x 1 m steel safe at all times. All personnel working with the object are strictly prohibited from making contact with SCP-9394, and must wear memetic protection at all times while working with the object

**Description:** SCP-9394 is a life-size plush seal toy, with a weight of 3 kg. SCP-9394 is composed of materials resembling fleece (skin), synthetic down (stuffing), and plastic (eyes and nose). However, these materials are resistant to physical damage far beyond what would normally be expected.

SCP-9394 is sentient and poses a memetic threat that manifests upon establishing mental contact with a human, at which point it initiates communication. The affected individual will hereafter be designated SCP-9394-1.

Following a prolonged communication, SCP-9394-1 becomes convinced that they are a Selkie currently remaining on land while still wearing their seal pelt. Subjects subsequently conclude, without any direct instruction from SCP-9394, that removal of this “seal pelt” is necessary. During the removal process, they do not feel any pain or discomfort. When the skin has been completely removed, the subject dies from blood loss. Subjects display signs of extreme euphoria until death.

**Addendum 9394.1:** The following interview was conducted by D-150297, who was instructed to transcribe all of SCP-9394’s responses onto paper.

> **Interviewer:** D-150297

>

> **Interviewee:** SCP-9394

> ~~-~~-

>

> [BEGIN LOG]

>

> *(D-150297 enters the containment chamber and looks directly at the object.)*

> 

> **D-150297:** Um… okay. Hi? 

> **SCP-9394:** Hello, human. I see you didn’t come here by chance.

>

> **D-150297:** *Writing on the paper* Yeah… can I ask you a few questions? For starters… what should I call you?

>

> **SCP-9394:** Saoirse. Call me Saoirse. Questions… humans have always sought to know more. The human brain cannot tolerate gaps in knowledge. Yet, some things were meant to remain hidden.

>

> **D-150297:** I’m sorry? 

>

> **SCP-9394:** I know what questions are tormenting you, human. You want to know why I do this? Well, once we were whole, but nature—which had hoarded its secrets—would not tolerate anyone trying to unravel them. Driven by your restless minds, you strayed from your true origin. I am not trying to harm you; I am merely trying to help you remember who you truly are.

>

> **D-150297:** Remember who we truly are?… Can you tell me who created you?

>

> **SCP-9394:** I was created by one who realized his own origin. He saw the filth of this modern human world. He created me for a noble cause. He was the very first person I tried to save.

> **D-150297:** Do you honestly believe you’re saving people?

>

> *(Following this response, the subject ceased making entries. Some time later, the subject slumped to the floor, exhibiting signs of psychological distress, and then used a pencil to peel off his own skin. By the time Security personnel entered the cell and took the pencil away from D-150297, he had removed too much skin and died from blood loss.)*
