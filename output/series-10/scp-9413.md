Once an SCP-9413-A manifestation is confirmed, MTF operatives are to mobilize to the designated area and administer amnestics to all potential witnesses of SCP-9143-A and -B. Due to their physical appearances, a disinformation campaign is ongoing to frame SCP-9413-A1 through 12’s yearly manifestations as a combination of non-anomalous children playing pranks or homicide cases. SCP-9413-C is currently considered at large. Locating SCP-9143-C has been classified a Beta-level priority due to the knowledge it has of the "monsters" described to Director Regan during its only confirmed manifestation.

**Update 11/1/2012:** Due to SCP-9413-A1 through 12 having not appeared in or near the aforementioned compound in a decade and the building itself exhibiting no anomalous properties, it has been considered safe to enter and has been converted into Provisional Site-9413. designed to better predict and respond to SCP-9413-A manifestations, determine the potential whereabouts of SCP-9413-C, as well as the containment of other anomalies.

**Description:** SCP-9413 is the collective designation for the disembodied spirits of the Kreaper family, a then-financially struggling farm family of Irish/Celtic descent who died under unknown circumstances on the night of October 31st, 1979.

SCP-9413-A consists of 12 Class-III-III-V identical ghost children who identify themselves as the children of the Kreaper family.  All 12 instances manifest as white transparent humanoid entities with the following visual characteristics:

* Wearing tattered white cloaks that resemble common depictions of the grim reaper

* An ectoplasmic composition identical in taste and texture to commercial sheep milk

* Wielding white scythes nearly twice the length of the entities bodies.  Though the SCP-9413-A instances appear to show no signs of burden while wielding them. 

* Carrying lantern-like objects shaped similarly to metallic milk churns

The entities comprising SCP-9413-A possess several anomalous properties typical of a traditional ghost, specifically levitation, invisibility, intangibility, and teleportation.  Additionally, should all twelve entities remain within a 4 meter radius of a human being for 13 minutes, that human will suffer near instant death from an unknown force.

The entities have shown immense levels of playfulness and affection to both Foundation personnel and each other, to the point of being able to effortlessly finish sentences between eachother and have rarely been recorded engaging in arguments between one another.  One such exception is in the presence of sheep milk, which they have shown incredible fondness for.

SCP-9413-B manifest themselves as spiritual white entities resembling a cross between domestic sheep and Old English Sheepdog puppies.  Each instance corresponds to a different SCP-9413-A instance, manifesting at SCP-9413-A's will and even allowing SCP-9413-A instances to ride upon them through the sky during active phases.  It is unknown what purpose SCP-9413-B instances serve to their corresponding SCP-9413-A instance besides this, though it is believed to be some form of companionship, judging by the constant affection SCP-9413-A receives from them.    

SCP-9413-A (and by extension SCP-9413-B) exclusively manifest during the month of October and are nocturnal, demanifesting and remanifesting when the sun rises and sets respectively.  For unknown reasons, all instances of SCP-9413-A appear to avoid areas of religious, cultural, or historical significance, such as churches, castles, and cemeteries.   This is theorized to be due to their religious background.  See Addendum 2 for further context.  Their most common point of gathering is the aforementioned farmland, located near ████, Ireland.

During the first 30 days of their active cycle, SCP-9413-1 through 12 will manifest at farmlands across the country to engage in playful behavior, utilizing their anomalous properties in order to pull pranks and scare farm owners.  This often includes but is not limited to:

* Scaring livestock or letting them loose.

* Misplacing tools

* Stealing milk and destroying crops

* Vandalizing barn houses with their own ectoplasm by throwing it an an “egging” manner.

 Significant changes in behavior occur from October 31st at sundown, during which they engage in religious activities and temporarily lose their playful demeanor.  These activities will hereby be referred to as "hunts"

 During these hunts, instances of SCP-9413-A will spontaneously manifest in random residential locations across the country, going from house to house with their lanterns outstretched in search of human targets. Each target of SCP-9413 is seemingly selected at random, with no clear pattern between targets.  Upon reaching the chosen target's current place of residence or location, they will proceed to repeatedly knock on any available doorway leading to the exterior until the occupant answers, presumably attempting to trick the target into believing that SCP-9413 are normal civilians engaging in the practice of trick-or-treating.  If the occupant doesn't answer within 6 minutes and 30 seconds after the initial knocking, SCP-9413 instances will proceed to enter the property by either phasing directly through the property or manifesting somewhere inside. 

When an instance of SCP-9413-A reaches its selected target, it will proceed to kill the target by delivering a deep laceration to the neck, waiting until the victim bleeds to death.  After the victim expires, the SCP-9413-A instance will proceed to perform a crude ritual in which the victim's body is placed on top of a “summoning circle”, drawn by the instances in their own ectoplasm.  The SCP-9413-A instance will then proceed to chant an unknown incantation in Irish, an unidentified white ball of light emerging from the victim’s body once said incantation is completed. The SCP-9413-A instance will then absorb these orbs into their makeshift lanterns before exiting the property, repeating this process until they have obtained an unknown number of light balls and disappear, ending the hunt.  These balls of light will only appear if a ritual performed by SCP-9413-A is completed, and it is unknown what happens to them after being absorbed or where they go after SCP-9413-A demanifests. 

**Discovery:** SCP-9413 first came to Foundation attention on October 23rd, 1984, during thorough investigation into rumors about the abandoned compound of what was formerly the Kreaper Killer’s headquarters being haunted began to gain traction throughout the month, after a group of 5 young children disappeared presumably while exploring the property.   After this incident caught the attention of the Foundation, MTF Chi-14 was sent to investigate 2 days later. On route to the property, Agent Mark Angles spotted the entities flying through the air in straight formation.  The entities appeared to have spotted Agent Angles, immediately turned around as they were nearing his location.

Agent Angles proceeded to pursue the twelve entities without backup from the rest of the task force.  It was noted by the MTF squad during a post-mission briefing that the entities appeared to be laughing as they were being pursued by him.

<summary>+ Addendum 2: History</summary>

Through what could be gathered from historical records, the Kreaper Family is a bloodline of shepherds that have their origins as early as 500 B.C.  They were notable not only for specializing in the production of mutton and sheep-based dairy products, but due to the Kreaper family being devoted paganists, viewing Halloween  Originally known as Samhain, which SCP-9413-A and C continuously refer to the holiday as.  as sacred, which was reflected in a lowered price sale for all products under the Kreaper Killer's brand during that month. According to historical records, the brand was incredibly popular with local civilians, citing that the milk especially "tasted like heaven."  The family allegedly suffered from dire financial struggles during the early 1800’s, their business eventually fading into communal obscurity.  Additionally, members of the family died at an accelerated rate throughout their history before the bloodline completely ended with SCP-XXXX-A, B, and C’s deaths in 1979.  No discernible cause of death for any member of the family has ever been confirmed.

Notably, the founder of the company  Who has currently not been identified. allegedly committed numerous acts of fraud in an attempt to keep his business open, up until his death, whose date of which has been lost.

Ghostly figures similar to SCP-9413 appeared throughout this period and were allegedly reported to have engaged in similar hunting behavior, though the method of murder, manifestation period, and ritualistic activities varied considerably between the groups of entities.  The only constants between these entities beside their bloodline was that they all possessed pale white bodies, white cloaks, carried white farming tools, and were accompanied by sheep/sheepdog hybrid spirits.

Due to the hunting cycle of each group of entities beside the current ones (SCP-9413-A1 through 12) ending before the Foundation discovered the anomaly, the information presented in the following log is mostly gathered from public word and archived reports.

<summary>+ Addendum 3: SCP-9413-C </summary>

**Description:**  SCP-9413-C is the spirit of Bas Kreaper, the biological father of SCP-9413-A1 through 12.  Like SCP-9413-A instances, SCP-9413-C's spirit manifests as a white humanoid figure wearing a white grim reaper-like cloak, as well as possessing a scythe that he has so far not been known to take off hand.  Before he came into his current form, SCP-9413-C was the then-owner of The Kreaper Killer’s Family Farm and a renowned game hunter in the nearby counties.

SCP-9413-C has only to date directly interacted with the Foundation once. On October 31st, 1997 at 12:00 pm, SCP-9413-C suddenly appeared before Director Regan in his office at Site-273 while he was looking into information regarding the entity mentioned by SCP-9413-A instances.

---

**<Begin Transcript>**

[Director Regan is searching through various about the anomaly in his office when SCP-9413-C spontaneously manifests before him.]

Director Regan: What the hell?

SCP-9413-C: Thank god they sent me back, you've seen my children right?  Is my little flock okay?

Director Regan: State your name and identity before I can say any more.

SCP-9413-C: Name’s Bas.  Bas Kreaper. I'm the flock's shepherd, and I've been waiting for a donkey’s age to finally see them again.

Director Regan: Fascinating.  We were certain that there had to be more of you.  By your “flock” you’re referring to SCP-9413-1 through 12, right?

SCP-9413-C: ..........What?

Director Reagan: Your 12 children.

SCP-9413-C: If you're half as savvy as all those bits of paper behind you make you out to be, you can guess as well as I. Happy then, for all of us, I can feel you met my lambs.

Director Reagan: The same lambs that have spent almost two decades slaughtering innocents for…. whatever these kooky rituals are for?

SCP-9413-C: Trust me, I...we don't want to do them any more than my lambs do, but we're all supposed to partake in them according to the brothers.  The monsters that emerged to this plane are no funny business.  Wish I knew about all this before Great decided to kick me in the bucket so I could tell my sons. They’re little balls of cotton and wouldn’t hurt a damn wolf.

Director Reagan: Then why aren’t you with them?

SCP-9413-C: Can’t really tell ya, but the flock's lucky that their old man has a knack for hunting.

Director Reagan: And what exactly are these monst-

SCP-9413-C: Listen, I'm not here to sit and chat. I'm here with a message and request. Which would you like to hear first?

Director Reagan:…….The message I suppose?

SCP-9413: SCP-9413-C: Tell my flock, my lambs, that this is the end. The last seasons of our service. They'll be coming home soon.  And don’t think I don’t know what you and your pigs are doing to my property.  I swear to Hecate and back, you better not try anymore fast ones, because if you mess with one of us, you mess with all of us.

Director Regan: What do you mean all of us?  There’s only you and your sons left.  If you think you’re on an equal playing field with us you’re wrong.

[Numerous entities similar in appearance to SCP-9413-C spontaneously manifest within the room, all of various ages, all carrying scythes, and surrounding Director Reagan from all directions. Numerous dog spirits also appear, growling aggressively at Director Reagan.]

SCP-9413-C: Ye were saying?

Director Reagan: What in the hell? How many of you are there?

SCP-9413-C: We’re a flock remember.  What did you expect?

Director Reagan: Frankly, one, or one family, a generation, or less, based on the records.

[Laughter ranging from chuckling to hysterics begins to break out among the entities before being cut off by SCP-9413-C]

SCP-9413-C: There's one thing you Christians got right. The devils always get their due, but they take more than a tithe.

[SCP-9413-C raises its arm as Director Reagan suddenly stumbles and places his hand on his head.]

Director Reagan: Apologies, the message…

SCP-9413-C: Sod it. Down where we emerged there’s a hunt goin on bigger than you and your boxes. Move too much in the brothers' game and they'll clear the board. And there's enough of us to make nice quick work of it.

Director Reagan: Ok, Ok Noted.  You said you had a request as well.

[The other entities begin to demanifest one by one]

SCP-9413-C: This is your one warning: pass my message to my flock and then stay out of their way. Otherwise, never forget, ye cunts step out of line, believe that we’ll be knocking on your doors first. Ye reap what you fucking sow. Ye always do.

Director Reagan: First?  What are you planning to do afterwa-

[Director Reagan body begins to pale as slides to his knees clutching the edge of his desk.]

[SCP-9413-C stares into the camera.]

SCP-9413-C: Fine, what's one more debt. I'll tell them myself.

[SCP-9413-C demanifests]

**<End Transcript>**

Following this incident, Director Reagen was revived several hours later and showed no adverse health issues.

Director's Note: If what SCP-9413-C is saying is true, then we cannot risk interfering with SCP-9413-A1 through 12’s former and current “life” anymore. Until we know more about these so-called "brothers", we cannot assume that SCP-9413 will remain constrained to the ritual patterns they've maintained thus far. We can only speculate on the damage that their whole "flock" can inflict should those behaviors change.

Death is at our doorstep, literally.  We are dealing with a family of ghostly entities that can probably kill us just by raising their goddamn arms. Not to mention once again our records on them are far, FAR from complete.  Who knows how many more of these reaper ghosts those "brother's" have at their disposal ready to spirit our organization away to god knows where the very moment we knock at the wrong door. At the moment we are defenseless, and the Foundation cannot afford to be defenseless.

<summary>+ Addendum 4: Event 9413-Alpha</summary>

On the night of October 31st, 2002, SCP-9413-A1 through 12 forewent their yearly hunting period and from 7:50pm to 7:50 am, were sighted near the border of the perimeters of the abandoned compound, all huddled together and making sounds described by personnel nearby at the time as “sobbing”. SCP-9413-1 through 12 then proceeded to mount SCP-9413-B1 through 12 and flew off in formation, yelling “We’re coming Papa!” before demanifesting shortly afterward.

 All personnel within the aforementioned 34.7 hectares immediately dropped dead during the incident, unable to be revived. In total, around ████ members of Foundation personnel were lost.  

The following morning, a message was discovered to have been etched into the dirt near the farmhouse at some point prior to the incident.  Despite having been covered in the same ectoplasmic substance as the one that SCP-9413 is composed of, the transparency of the substance meant the entire message remained fully legible. It read the following:

> Been a hot minute, huh fellas?  This year Great decided to throw me a bone, but I don't have much time left on this plane, so I'll cut to the chase for y'all. I know what those brothers have been forcing you to do for our......what's it 20? years apart. And I've got a few things to say. Remember those days when you fellas would cry when we had to gut the sheep?  Remember those days when we would carve out pumpkins in the shape of all of the ghosts and ghouls from the tales I used to read to you before tucking your little heads under the sheets for the night? Every time you feel like you want to cry for me, I want you to remember those times when we stuck together through thick and thin. All of us are sorry for dragging you into this mess. We tried to look for an easy way out of our strife and sold our souls to do so. While we can’t undo what’s been done to us, we can grab our scythes and keep sowing the seeds of freedom. The brothers can take my soul, the Foundation can take my farm, but no one can take my love. The rest of the flock down here are all rooting for you twelve. If death’s idea of punishment means tearing down this freak show, then we’ll tear down this freak show together. And whenever you dread of snatching the souls of the great grandchildren of our former customers like ye been doing all these years, remember this, you may be little ghosts now, but you’re also angels.  My little angels.

SCP-9413-A1 through 12 has not manifested within the perimeter of the farmland since this incident and no longer manifest throughout the month of October, now exclusively manifesting on the night of the 31st to carry out their hunts. Furthermore, reports of SCP-9413-1 instances manifesting in other European countries have been documented and are currently on the rise.
