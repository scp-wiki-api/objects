**Special Containment Procedures:** Containment of SCP-9435 is currently impossible, due to its ubiquitous nature. However, it is unnecessary to be contained as the public is unaware of the existence of SCP-9435. Effects of it are attributed to human error. Hence, no containment procedures are needed.

**Description:** SCP-9435 is an anomalous phenomenon in which small objects (such as documents or accessories) within a personal room inside of a home disappears after being cleaned up by someone else other than the inhabitant. This phenomenon occurs the most often in (but not exclusive to) middle-class or higher status households in which there will be someone to help clean up rooms within the home (e.g. stay-at-home mothers and domestic helpers), and rooms that are generally considered messy or unorganised. Objects that are affected SCP-9435 cease to exist, leaving no physical traces behind. These objects are also impossible to be found and reacquired by any means, even if the cleaner's steps have been retraced and the room has been fully searched.

SCP-9435's effects are dismissed as human error, as it may be attributed to three reasons:

* the room's cleaner misplaced the affected object during the process of organising the room

* the organised objects were not placed in a sensible manner

* the inhabitant of the affected room is at fault, as they failed to properly tidy up their room which lead to the affected object being lost easily

Due to SCP-9435's effects, it causes daily inconveniences and stress to humans, such as, but not limited to:

* missing deadlines for assignments or projects (when the lost object is specifically physical documents or relevant material)

* spending extra money to obtain another copy of the lost object

* wasting time to search for the lost object, leading to mild frustration

* misunderstandings and conflict between the room's inhabitant and cleaner, which may negatively affect the interpersonal relationship between them
