**Item #:** SCP-9438

**Object Class:** ~~Safe~~ Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9438 is to be contained in a locked room at Site-11, accessible to personnel of clearance level ~~1~~  2, no video games of the "shooter" genre, or any games that contain deadly weapons, are to be played on any PC connected to SCP-9438. SCP-9438 is to be kept 20 feet away from all Personal Computers at all times outside of testing. When testing is scheduled for the day, usually once a month, SCP-9438 is to be transported to Testing Chamber-01, also at Site 11, 1 (one) hour before the test, and is to remain in the chamber until 1 (one) hour has passed after the test. Personnel of Level  ~~1~~ 2 may use SCP-9438 during break hours, but during those hours SCP-9438 is to have a 1 centimetre screen protector over its screen to prevent personnel from getting anything they aren't meant to have via it's anomalous properties.

**Description:** SCP-9438 is a standard ███ brand flat-screen 27 inch Gaming Monitor of 

indeterminate origin. X-ray scans of SCP-9438 have shown that there is nothing wrong within it's circuitry.

**Anomalous Properties:** SCP-9438's anomalous properties allow any living thing to reach through the screen and take things from the videogame, tv-series, film, or anime that is currently being shown on the screen, and bring them into reality. ~~at this point no over anomalous effects have been found, and SCP-9438 can only bring inanimate objects into reality~~ {SEE ADDENDUM}

**Addendum:** SCP-9438 has been shown to be able to bring living things through the screen, said beings will reach their actual size when outside of the screen, as apposed to staying the relative size they were when displayed on the object, Object Class changed to: Euclid, and personnel now have to be Clearance Level 2 or higher to interact with SCP-9438. SCP-9438 has also been shown to show web browser results from other universes, testing on this effect has continued

**Testing Logs:**

**26/01/25:** D-1096 uses SCP-9438 to pull a "Bloxy-Cola" from Roblox into reality, now designated SCP-9438-2, SCP-9438-2 hasnt shown any anomalous effects aside from "tasting pixelated". 

**15/02/25**: D-0079 uses SCP-9438 to take a "Jarate" From Team Fortress 2, Kept in storage at Site-█, not given SCP classification as it is just a jar of urine.

**28/03/25:** D-1106 uses SCP-9438 to grab a "Ender pearl" from Minecraft, Designated SCP-9438-3, neutralised 30/03/25 when D-1106 tried to escape the security guards using SCP-9438-3, promptly terminated after accidentally teleporting right in front of another guard.

**05/04/25:** D-3939 Uses SCP-9438 and grabs "Arthur Morgan" from Red Dead Redemption 2 and pulls him through the screen, D-3939 is immediately terminated and, Now known as SCP-9438-4, is taken off site with his weapons confiscated, testing shows SCP-9438-4 has the "Dead-Eye" ability from the game, SCP-9438-4 is not allowed cigarettes / cigars at any point outside testing, medical examination shows that he does not have tuberculosis. 

**-TESTING HALTED FOR 6 MONTHS AS REVISIONS ARE MADE FOLLOWING SCP-9438-4's ARRIVAL-**

**31/10/25:** D-2049 Uses SCP-9438, at researchers allowance, brings the "Halo-3 Rat", into reality, not given SCP classification, but is given to the On-site D-Class personnel as a pet, dies of natural causes 3 months later.

**11/11/25:** D-14141 attempts to Google "Rick and Morty Portal Gun", but is terminated before he could grab it, object taken anyway, now designated SCP-9438-5.

**25/12/25:** D-6796 Googles "Christmas Present" to see what would happen. When present was opened there was nothing inside. Object Incinerated.

**01/01/26:** D-1096 uses SCP-9438, at attending researcher's questioning, Googles "[SCP-500](SCP-500 )", results showed up and a pill of [SCP-500](SCP-500 ) was taken from the screen (Now designated SCP-500-9438), File altered to show this. Other SCP's taken from SCP-9438 are to be designated SCP- [original number] -9438.

**Testing Halted Until Ethics Committee Approval.**
