**Item #:** SCP-9441

**Object Class:** Thaumiel (Provisional)

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9441 is to be housed in a 5m x 5m x 5m containment cell reinforced with a tungsten-carbide core and lead radiation shielding. 

Because SCP-9441 is generally cooperative when placated, compliance is maintained through quality-of-life provisions. At its request, the cell is to be furnished with comfortable amenities and a media entertainment system (movies). 

Personnel must maintain a respectful demeanor. Direct insults or derogatory remarks within auditory range of SCP-9441 are strictly prohibited, as they trigger immediate, violent psychological escalation. Dietary requirements consist of live rodents. Feeding protocols involving anomalous biological matter (such as SCP-049-2 instances) require explicit Site Director approval.

**Description:** SCP-9441 is a sentient, bipedal reptilian entity standing 2.1 meters tall. It possesses black scales, distinct dorsal plates, and a thagomizer on its tail. 

Radiological scans indicate SCP-9441’s internal biology functions as a living nuclear reactor. It constantly generates immense thermal and ionizing energy, which is regulated by a specialized "coolant" organ. To prevent this organ from rupturing due to over-pressurization, SCP-9441 must periodically absorb and redirect external energy (such as electricity or kinetic force). 

Unlike typical apex predators, SCP-9441 is highly intelligent and capable of understanding human language. It remains docile and willing to assist Foundation research and MTF operations, provided it feels respected. However, it possesses an volatile emotional trigger regarding its appearance. In one notable incident, after a researcher referred to its design as "cliché," SCP-9441 breached containment by tearing through 3 meters of solid steel to assault the individual.

SCP-9441's unique ability to absorb and redirect lethal radiation makes it an invaluable asset for neutralizing hazardous anomalies.
