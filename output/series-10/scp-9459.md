#### Special Containment Procedures:

Collected remains of SCP-9459 and the hardened synthetic resin previously containing it are presently stored at Site-91's Containment Room No. 817. It is to be logged as [https:*scp-wiki.wikidot.com/log-of-anomalous-items a more "typical", anomalous item], in accordance to Addendum #27 of the *12th Update on Standards, Classifications, and Procedures of the Foundation//.

Before its neutralization, SCP-9459 was temporarily stored in a provisional containment unit designed to immobilize SCP-9459 by submerging it in synthetic resin. Whilst en route to Site-91 after its reclassification as SCP-9459 by order of the O5-Council, rogue operatives of the Indonesian autonomous branch ("ID-Regional Command")of the Foundation caused the destruction of SCP-9459 and its containment unit. The remnants of both SCP-9459 and its containment unit were then secured by forward elements of Mobile Task Force *Alpha-1* ("Red Right Hand") as the main force of the MTF engaged the aforementioned rogue elements of the autonomous branch. After being transported to Site-19, SCP-9459 was declared neutralized when no further anomalous effects were detected from its remnants.

**Addendum 9459.I:**

In order to prevent further subversive acts from elements within ID-Regional Command, the O5-Council has ordered the Department of Internal Affairs to conduct an investigation into the branch's Executive Council and personnel. The proposal to dispatch both MTF *Alpha-1* and Mobile Task Force *Epsilon-11* ("Nine-Tailed Fox") to enforce this investigation made by O5-8 has been vetoed by the Ethics Committee.

As of %%updated_at|%Y/%m/%d%%, an O5-Council vote to override the veto has been initiated by O5-8.

#### Description:

Before being reclassified as SCP-9459, the object was previously designated as SCP-099-ID by and under the auspices of ID-Regional Command, the autonomous branch of the Foundation in the Republic of Indonesia.

SCP-9459 was a simple swing set, manufactured by the Dutch East Indies colonial government between 1929 and 1930. The seat of SCP-9459 was made of aluminium, hooked by a pair of chains to its iron frame. When first secured by the Foundation as SCP-099-ID, it had experienced mild rusting, and was afterwards cleaned, repaired, and restored as deemed appropriate without dismantling or replacing its components.

When immobile, SCP-9459 did not have any anomalous effects whatsoever. However, its anomalous nature could be activated whenever the seat of SCP-9459 was moved: global attention on and/or awareness of what is presently known as Indonesia would decrease upon regular swinging of SCP-9459. The area-of-effect of SCP-9459's anomalous properties had been determined to be [conceptually linked](http://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/tanhony-s-proposal-ii/offset/2) to the boundaries of the Dutch East Indies during the 1910sRefer to Point ii), Addendum, "SCP-099-ID — OVERVIEW" within the SCP-099-ID: Ver. 2.0.96-EN file in Addendum 9459.II. with the notable exception of the island of Bali. This was determined to have been intentional on the part of the Speciale Onderzoeken Sectie (S.O.Z.S.), the Dutch colonial government's "Section of Special Investigations" responsible for the creation of SCP-9459, likely for tourism-related purposes.

SCP-9459 was acquired as SCP-099-ID during a raid by ID-Regional Command in December 1996 on an office of the Indonesian Institute of Anomalous Affairs/I.A.A.The formal English name for the *Lembaga Urusan Anomali* (Lemrusan). in Jakarta. Afterwards, the object was then stored in special containment unit "4509-Masmira", an earthquake-proof, air-vacuumed unit tying and weighting the object to the floor and walls of the unit to immobilise the object. Unit "4509-Masmira" itself was located in Site-01ID, Surabaya.

Previously, SCP-9459's anomalous effects were mistakenly regarded by the I.A.A. and ID-Regional Command. It had been long thought that SCP-9459 affected the levels of "discontent"Defined as "discontent of general living circumstances, government actions, daily occurrences, and et cetera pertaining to the public aspects of society," in the last iteration of SCP-9459's documentation as SCP-099-ID. of the Indonesian public. Quoting *Internal Memo No. 1502/1997* sent to the O5-Council by the administrator-in-charge of the ID-Regional Command's Executive Council, "... [public] complacency increases when[ever] SCP-099-ID is swung faster."

After the then-director of Site-01ID proposed a reassessment of SCP-9459's effects, further research by the Department of Analytics found the previously mentioned assumption held by ID-Regional Command was falseThis reassessment was proven correct when the neutralization of SCP-9459 did not result in [https:*scp-wiki.wikidot.com/waving-along what would have been] an LV/BM-Class "Broken Masquerade" Scenario had the original assessment of SCP-9459's effects been correct.. Instead, it was found that SCP-9459 likely, "... 'dampened' general awareness of the global population to a region which had been more notable throughout its history.Paragraph 5, page 26 of Omkos, H. (2016). "Reclassification Proposal: 099-ID". *Observer: An SCP Foundation Journal//, Vol. 49." ID-Regional Command's failure to enact proper anomalous handling procedures gave cause for the O5-Council to freeze its Executive Council and re-designate SCP-099-ID as SCP-9459.
