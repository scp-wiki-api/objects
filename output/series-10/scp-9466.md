**Item #:** SCP-9466

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9466 is to be contained in a labelled Safe-class object locker at Site-91. Item must not be read unless permission has been granted by at least 3 on-site staff members of Level-3 clearance or higher. Alternatively, an Anomalous Item Usage Request may be filed which, if accepted by at least 2 Foundation personnel yielding Level-4 clearance or higher, would grant sufficient authorisation for the reading of SCP-9466. Textual copies of SCP-9466 host no anomalous properties and may be safely read.

SCP-9466 must only be read if at least 2 Foundation personnel capable of reading, reciting, and understanding The Foundation has found that there exist inconsistencies with the summoning of Event-9466, especially by inexperienced readers of Younger Futhark. A reader's understanding of what is being read is required for a successful summoning.  Younger Futhark A runic alphabet used throughout Scandinavia between the 8th and 12th centuries.  of Level-1 clearance or higher are present. All readings of SCP-9466 are to occur in areas secluded from public access by at least 5km. Readings should ideally occur in areas where sources of wood are abundant, such as places hosting woody plants or wooden structures. Coastal territories that have direct nautical access to the Humber in the East Riding of Yorkshire, England should be prioritised as summoning locations.

Should Event-9466 ever be halted, an authorised reader is to read a non-consecutive SCP-9466 instance in its entirety.

Due to the scale of Event-9466, readings should occur as infrequently as possible. In tandem with British, Irish, Norwegian, Danish, and German governments, the Foundation has designed public disinformation, distraction, and diversion campaigns to prevent unauthorised viewings of Event-9466. Obscuring protocols must be enacted by Foundation naval forces to minimise the likelihood of public viewing of Event-9466. Personnel who are subject to unsanctioned viewing of Event-9466 are to be administered Class-A General Retrograde amnestic agents. Non-Foundation owned media pertaining to Event-9466 is to be located and seized.

SCP-9466-S instances should not be disturbed when actively fulfilling tasks stated throughout the SCP-9466 anthology.

**Description:** SCP-9466 collectively refers to 8 poems written in Younger Futhark on Norwegian spruce wax reading tablets, designated SCP-9466-1 through -8. The contents of each SCP-9466 instance detail the Norwegian perspective of the Battle of Stamford Bridge at varying time stages, including events that occurred before and after the conflict, following the same linear timeline. Despite their age, each reading tablet is physically whole and fully intelligible, with only minor chipping and surface scratches indicating aging.

Reading and reciting the first SCP-9466 instance, SCP-9466-1, summons an army of approximately 10,000 humanoids which appear around the reader through spontaneous generation. Initial summoning is designated as Event-9466-1, with events labelled Event-9466-1 through 8, paralleling the SCP-9466 section recited. Summoned humanoids are collectively referred to as SCP-9466-S. Instances of SCP-9466-S appear to be invulnerable to external sources of physical damage. A vast majority of instances of SCP-9466-S are equipped with historically accurate 11th-century Norwegian battle equipment. 

Other sections of the army consist of individuals equipped with equipment akin to 10th and 11th-century European mercenaries. Given the historical precision of SCP-9466, these are believed to be Flemish mercenaries. Historically, Flemish mercenaries only joined the Norwegian army after Norway's forces have landed in England. The reason for this historical inaccuracy is unknown. 

Whilst it has been shown that SCP-9466-S instances are capable of interacting with non-anomalous lifeforms, these occurrences are sporadic and inconsistent. Subjects fail to maintain eye contact, terminate dialogue prematurely, and oftentimes ignore attempts at social initiation by non-anomalous lifeforms. SCP-9466-S instances are shown to be capable of inflicting harm and damage against non-anomalous life, however these manifestations are deemed to be non-deliberate. SCP-9466-S instances have also been observed relocating and destroying tangible objects as well as using modern equipment to eliminate physical barriers which hinder their execution of narrative events throughout the SCP-9466 anthology.

Upon expiration, the whole organic mass of a specified SCP-9466-S instance, as well as any clothes, accessories, and armour, gradually lose their opacity before becoming fully transparent and physically dematerialising. 

Some SCP-9466-S instances appear to hold authority over remaining instances. These instances use vocalisations to relay commands to the remaining SCP-9466-S specimens, demanding them to complete tasks outlined throughout the SCP-9466 anthology. 

These SCP-9466-S instances are equipped with golden jewellery and noble or regal cloths, and are believed to be manifestations of Norwegian generals and nobles present in the Battle of Stamford Bridge. Auditory recordings corroborate the claim that one of the SCP-9466-S instances is Harald Hardrada, the King of Norway from 1046 to 1066, who historically led the Norwegian forces in the 1066 Invasion of England and the proceeding Battle of Stamford Bridge. This SCP-9466-S instance has been designated as SCP-9466-H. An auditory recording of SCP-9466-H is attached below:

Foundation personnel observing Event-9466 have consistently stated that SCP-9466-S instances display positive emotions through actions and facial expressions when actively fulfilling commands. Vice versa, SCP-9466-S specimens may appear frustrated, confused, apathetic, and non-communicative if not completing commands. However, behaviour in SCP-9466-S specimens is non-binary; instances gradually lose activity and interest in performed tasks after the completion of an SCP-9466 section. Instances reach a nadir in activity approximately 2 days after the most recent recital.

If the chronology of SCP-9466 is broken during recital, all instances of SCP-9466-S expire.

Event-9466 collectively refers to the sum of all events that occur throughout a complete reading of SCP-9466. These events are disambiguated and enumerated Event-9466-1 through -8 for each poem present in the SCP-9466 anthology.

> 

> On the 20th of May 2015, the Department of the Research of Anomalies at Site 91 greenlit Operation "Vikings", allowing all of SCP-9466 to be recited, and for the whole of Event-9466 to unfurl.

> 

> The Foundation has collaborated with western European governments This includes the British, Irish, Icelandic, Norwegian, Danish, German, Dutch, Belgian, and French governments.  to facilitate the possibility of Event-9466 occurring in its entirety. The Foundation has also communicated with the North Atlantic Trade Organisation (NATO) to ascertain permission and operational integrity for this event. The first recital of SCP-9466-1 occurred on the 16th of September 2015, which gave the SCP-9466-S army 8 days to prepare its fleet and travel from Norway to England, as well as a single day dedicated for the battle at Stamford Bridge, on the 25th of September, 2015.

> 

> Since an occurrence of this size is very difficult to hide, the event was advertised as a professional recreational war re-enactment of the Battle of Stamford Bridge, with significant resources diverted towards misinformation about the event. To ensure minimal information leaks, a prohibition of any devices capable of photo imagery was enacted near areas of manifestation. A small wooden mock-up of York Minster, the cathedral at York, has been constructed near the city to prevent potential structural damage to the cathedral itself. Robust disinformation campaigns and excuse diversions have been prepared to secure the success of the event.

> 

> To ensure an appropriate and historical location, SCP-9466 was read in Svarvhelleholmen, Norway, a small uninhabited island on the nation's western coast. The site was cleared with help from the Norwegian government beforehand. Other staff and security personnel were present in the situation of an emergency.

>

Physical evidence of lacerations, blunt force trauma, and exhaustion appear on the bodies of SCP-9466-S instances engaged in combat. The battle lasts 12 hours.

> 

> **25th of September, 2015, at 8:30pm:** All SCP-9466-S instances are either slain or have dispersed. All SCP-9466-S instances then proceed to gradually become increasingly transparent (including their equipment) before dematerialising.

> 

> **26th of September, 2015, at 6:00pm:** No anomalous activity has been detected for 24 hours. The cessation of Operation "Vikings" is declared. Up to 3 Foundation personnel were dispatched to the scene of battle to monitor potential alterations in anomalous activity.

> 

> After the completion of Operation "Vikings", SCP-9466 was observed to have spontaneously generated a new spruce wax reading tablet. This generated tablet was found in SCP-9466's containment locker placed under the other SCP-9466 instances. The newly generated poem was henceforth designated SCP-9466-9, and has been translated to English below:

>> 

>> **SCP-9466-9 Translation:**

>> 

>> *Fierce fighting lasted long,*

>> *The blood-soaked air could hold no more*

>> *The Norse and Flemish fought till the last man*

>> *And in their dying breaths, looked up;*

>> *A glimmer of light had told them*

>> *That their passage to the afterlife had been secured.*

>> 

> On the 5th of October, 2015, the Foundation authorised a reading of SCP-9466-1 to observe and document anomalous consistency. However, no anomalous manifestations occurred, even after repeated readings of all sections of SCP-9466 including SCP-9466-9. Further examinations have confirmed that SCP-9466 was absent of any identifiable anomalous properties, with an SCP Reclassification Revision lodged by Senior Researcher Bernard Knight 3 years after initial cessation. This reclassification has been successful.

>

<summary>+ Open Document: SCP-9466 Revision - LEVEL 5 CLEARANCE REQUIRED</summary>

**Item #:** SCP-9466

**Object Class:** Neutralised

**Special Containment Procedures:** The collection of spruce wax reading tablets formerly designated SCP-9466-1 through -9 are to be located in a labelled Safe-class object locker in Site-19's Wing B of the site's Research of Time Anomalies Sector.

**Description:** SCP-9466 refers to the Battle of Stamford Bridge, an event that partially failed to manifest into reality when it occurred on the 25th of September, 1066. This failed manifestation event is deemed to have specifically affected the Norwegian side of the battle.

**Addendum:** After the completion of Operation "Vikings" and the observed cessation of all anomalous properties, SCP-9466's object class has been reclassified as 'Neutralised'.

> ****Note from O5-██:****

> 

> *Foundation analysis concludes that SCP-9466 was an anomaly in space-time, where an anomalous force prevented a section of the Battle of Stamford Bridge from occurring in reality. How this happened, and how the wooden reading tablets managed to trigger a successful manifestation of the Battle of Stamford Bridge in reality, is unclear. Timeline analysis shows that our timeline has been altered by the event, where before the completion of Event-9466, the Battle of Stamford Bridge was never mentioned or documented throughout available historical archives.*

> 

> *Following the completion of Event-9466, Foundation personnel have observed localised temporal distortions in reality in the form of higher Hume readings across Europe. Scranton Reality Anchor readings have observed sizeable increases in local reality, with available Kant counters documenting readings up to 135 Humes across the United Kingdom and the Faroe Islands. Such readings of Humes correlate to increased reality distortions and potential faults and fractures in time - these must be addressed immediately.*

> 

> *Decreased Scranton Reality Anchor activity has been swiftly enacted to maintain a stable level of reality across Europe.*

> 

> *The Foundation is to designate greater resources to its archaeological departments for the excavation and research of anomalous artefacts. The peculiar nature of the reading tablets contribute to the theory that there were other conflicts in history that similarly failed to occur in reality. How widespread this phenomenon is, and whether its occurring in the modern day, is unknown.*

> 

> *The Foundation must conduct further research into SCP-9466 and similar events to better understand how reality manifestation failures occur and, most importantly, if they are capable of incurring a ZK-Class Reality Failure Scenario.*

> 

> *Until then, we are glad we were able to give these men the rest they deserved.*
