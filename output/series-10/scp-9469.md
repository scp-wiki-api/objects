**SCP-9469:** Asphalt Vortex

**Written by:** 

**Photo by:** My dad! :D

**Item #:** 9469

**Object Class:** Zeno

**Special Containment Procedures:** A chain link fence, 5 meters in height, has been installed around the perimeter of SCP-9469. Audio and visual monitoring are to be performed remotely. Occupied vehicles are to be reported to the San Diego County Sheriff's collaboration line. To discourage entry, signs indicating the presence of hazardous materials are to be displayed on the front gate and around the site in 50 meter intervals.

Civilians located within SCP-9469 are to be removed and prosecuted for trespassing (and any additional applicable charges).

**Description:** SCP-9469 is a 13 acre lot located in Santee, California. It previously served as a drive-in movie theater for 32 years until its closure in 1999. In 2002, newly installed CCTV cameras captured an activation of SCP-9469's paranormalities. The land was subsequently purchased from the original ownersCompensation was provided in the amount of ~$14.3 million USD. The property owners willingly signed a non-disclosure agreement following the purchase. by the Department of Land Management.

SCP-9469's resting state is benign; the area will only begin to exhibit paranormalities when a vehicle enters and comes to a complete stop. Once a vehicle has entered SCP-9469, petroleum in the area surrounding the vehicle will begin to coagulate into an articulate facsimile of human arms and hands. These facsimiles will begin to grasp and embrace components such as bumpers, tire rims, driveshafts, and suspension. 

During this process, the asphalt around the vehicle will begin to slowly heat to 300-350°F, and the vehicle will begin to sink into the melting asphalt. After completely submerging the vehicle, the asphalt and petroleum will begin to cool, and SCP-9469 will return to its dormant state.

Ground penetrating radar indicates that SCP-9469 appears to be incapable of processing plastics, organics, and most metals. Deposits of lithium, melted plastic, steel, and bone, have been identified at depths exceeding 35 feet.

Since the purchase of SCP-9469, a total of five vehicles have entered the perimeter.

**Incident Report I-9469-1:** On May 23rd, 2011, [Withheld] Messier and [Withheld] Santos exited their vehicle and cut through the security gate leading to SCP-9469 using a pair of bolt cutters. The pair then re-entered their vehicle, parking in the northwest corner of the lot. Both Messier and Santos exited their vehicle, disposed of multiple aluminum cans, and entered the back seat. Analysis of FLIR recordings indicate that the pair began to engage in sexual intercourse after re-entering the vehicle. 

Observers located in Site-14 quickly noticed an increase in activity within SCP-9469. Within 4 minutes, the surface of the asphalt had reached an excess of 450°F, and the car began to sink. Multiple 9-1-1 calls were placed reporting screaming coming from the vicinity of SCP-9469. The San Diego County Sheriff's Department did not arrive until approximately 45 minutes after SPECOLPRO initially reported the trespassers. Additional private security patrols have been hired and assigned to SCP-9469 following this incident.

[Withheld] Messier and [Withheld] Santos were reported missing by their families. However, given the lack of physical evidence, no financial compensation is recommended at this time. The Department of Publicity has urged the San Diego County Sheriff to dispose of any records they may have regarding I-9469-1, in order to reduce the impact of private investigations and subpoenas. Their compliance remains unclear.
