**Item #:** SCP-9526

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** ~~SCP-9526 is to be moved to Lunar Are 32 until further notice~~ SCP-9526 is to be contained in an aseismic zone (area with little to no seismic activity) placed on a horizontal block of tungsten approximately 0.5 meters thick, 6 meters long and 2 meters wide until a more permanent containment procedure can be accomplished.

**Description:** SCP-9526 is a teal coloured 1957 Chevrolet Bel Air Vehicle found in Lovell Wyoming on ██/██/████. SCP-9526 was discovered by 05-██ before they joined the SCP foundation. SCP-9526 seems to be gaining weight at a rate of 7% every year and currently as of 05/07/20██ the vehicle weighs 457 tonnes. Multiple attempts to either damage or use SCP-9526 have resulted in failure, see Addendum-9526

**Addendum-9526:** All recorded neutralisation and experiment attempts on SCP-9526

| outline of experiment | description of experiment outcome | notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-class 5527 attempts to fill the car with gasoline | D-class 5527 poured a container of gasoline into the tank of SCP-9526. As soon as the gasoline entered the tank the pressure vapourised it creating a small explosion leaving D-class 5527 with 3rd degree burns on the top half of their body. SCP-9526 is unharmed. | further attempts to fuel the car will need level 2 clearance |
| D-class 6222 is equipped with a blowtorch, crowbar and diamond tipped nail to attempt to damage the car | D-class 6222 used the blowtorch first on the driver’s side door, no effect. Next, she attempted to use the diamond tipped nail to break the window. No effect. Next, she tried to puncture the tyre using the nail. No effect. finally, she hit SCP-9526 with the crowbar on all sides of the car. No effect. | D-class 6222 has been terminated by security for trying to use the crowbar on site personnel. |
| Incident report: attempted transfer of SCP-9526 to Lunar Area-32 | Transportation failed as SCP-9526 is too heavy to be removed from earth’s surface at this time. | *Continued weight gain of SCP-9526 could result in an effect on other celestial bodies or worst case scenario, a gravitational collapse* |
| A Unit 510 - Liebherr LTM 1500-8.1 - All Terrain Mobile Crane will be used to move SCP-9526 to an aseismic facility for temporary containment | Two (2) mobile cranes had to be used. During the transport the passenger side door was opened causing a vacuum previously unknown to the SCP foundation to collapse causing a small bang similar to a whip cracking. | Further experiments to enter the car will be conducted. |
| D-Class 47624 will attempt to sit in the driver seat of the Vehicle | The mobile cranes were used to open SCP-9526's driver side door. The strain on the wires of the crane indicates that the door handle weighs approximately 7 tonnes. The vacuum inside SCP-9526 collapsed causing another bang. no damage to the vehicle or other components of the cell or personnel were harmed. D-class 47624 was brought into the cell and told to sit in the car. He was not informed of the anomalous properties of SCP-9526. D-class 47624 tried to enter the driver seat of SCP-9526. As he crossed the boundary of the door frame he was instantly crushed by the pressure inside of SCP-9526. The vehicle seemed to spit out D-class 47624 leaving the interior as clean as before the experiment. | further testing with SCP-9526 will be discontinued following this experiment. Level 2 clearance will be needed if further testing needs to be done. |
