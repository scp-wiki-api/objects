**SCP-9534 - Risers**

Risers, are one of the SCP Foundation’s biggest breakthroughs in proposing a “source entity” that may potentially explain much of the anomalous SCP’s and phenomenon documented so far. Containment class currently pending, with vague considerations of the secondary class type, Archon.

Discoveries relating to SCP-9534 have re-contextualised many other SCP’s, proposing consideration for mass class re-diagnosis and a re-ignition of physical experimentation in contained entities. A mining research facility has been proposed to investors at the point of interest at (REDACTED) for intensive research/exploration after the discoveries of Mobile Task Force “Quantum Sub”, secured the written testimony of a humanoid entity and retrieved a completely unknown state of crystallised matter with absurd properties.

**Maheed Kiramou’s (Requested) Log - Day 34**

“-and traversed down the cliff face slowly, the scrapes on my hands burning as I gripped the ropes. The echoey screams of The Beetle approached from above. I remember stifling my echoey grunts in terror after seeing its massive black form suspended on the wall earlier… I was hoping it wouldn’t use its ability to follow me down into the cavern. As I repelled downward with haste, heart thumping, the darkness of the expanse, the cool, moist air caused goosebumps on my skin. Whipping my headlight up and down, checking as the ground was approaching and the glow of the purple swirling lights from my dreams grew brighter, the shimmering source was coveted by a hidden passage, but the glow pouring onto the shallow water of the cavern floor was hypnotising enough. My shoes splashed in the shallow water echoing loudly and I could hear the trickling of condensation on the cave roof sprinkling down from above as well.

I suddenly heard the chortling of the beetle and the movement of its legs from above, and in that moment, when my heart dropped, I abandoned the rope and desperately thrashed my legs through the water, glancing backwards only once as I tread the uneven rocky ground toward the passage.

This passage, much tighter than any before except for the cave entrance had some form of soft crystallisation on the walls that felt like mushrooms as I squished through them. The same purple, chromatic, hue I had noticed on the harder crystallisation at Fire Point which led me to investigating the Beetle den.

The purple light was growing brighter as I squeezed away from the Beetle’s call and the rock walls I was squeezing through began to feel more-so like hard wax. A tacky texture. It felt like crawling through a mother’s womb into the light of birth, but inside I had the sinking feeling I was being swallowed alive.

Then, the horror of it… the passage came to an end. But how? The light, appearing to be emanating from behind a squishy purple wall. I hesitated to touch it for a moment, but in seeing no other path forward in this confined space my madness drew me to push on the crystallisation. It was… like pushing on a giant marshmallow, my eyes widened at the sight of rocks stretching before my hand, I thought if I pushed hard enough I would end up on the other side… and like a tooth piercing into the thick gooey innards of a marshmallow, my body became enveloped in it entirely, its bitter, metallic scent overwhelmed me, I held my breath and kept moving, but after a few steps forward my feet lost contact with the concept of ground and my brain began to panic. Suddenly, a pop, as the membrane coating the wall that was stretched around my skin suddenly broke…

I remember a burning sensation unlike any other, like my skin was being melted, but each square inch was connecting to something via suction. Intensely fulfilling, as if my physical form was undergoing a natural process but extremely painful too. I seized, realising there was no path forward in the purple darkness, the thinner regions of my skin like my eyelids were no longer mine, this substance had eaten through them and was now invading my eyes as well. Thrashing simply intensified and accelerated the process of conversion in the places I was outputting energy.

20 seconds became 20 minutes became 20 unknown units of suffering, regret and confusion. I could not scream, my mouth became full of the liquid metal acid.

The next phase that came was nausea, blood poisoning, the burning remained but my mind was being conditioned into accepting its state. I felt things I had never felt before, connected thoughts, impossible for me prior. And for the first time in my expedition, I actualised my greatest desire, my want…

I came to understand.

Understand that which should not be understood, connected into a gut-wrenching terror that whispered horrible truths directly into my blood. Gave me the very poison I had craved. I remember in all my crying, a sense of maniacal glee interrupted by vomit, at how truly maddened I had been this whole time. How truly alone I was. That I would rather befriend death, than reach out to Hailey again…

That was the last of my sadness, also eaten away by the Chrome… Chrome… how do we know that term? Where… no… when did it come to be understood by this-by A mind!

Oh my god… I-I am peering into the formless knots that tie this very universe together… or what was understood to be a universe.

My physical sensations dwindled quickly once my body had been fully possessed by the Chrome. I now had control over sensations I previously didn’t. Influence, contribution, shaping the figure of new creation…

I pondered on how the lifeless void I was sinking through, came to emanate the purple light that lured me here and as I was about to pursue and manifest those purple lights from my dream once more, so that I may pursue them within this void-JAWS GNASHING JAWS of a massive Beetle violently intruded the substance and clamped around my stomach, ripping into my scorched flesh and plucking me from my womb.

It was truly awful. Like being separated from whom you love most. Thrust into a gritty, sensory world once more. I screamed so loud I scared myself as my voice had changed. The beetle did not care… its many legs thudding along the ground as it trekked casually. My vision was a blurry mix of purple-adjacent colours that humanity simply does not have the words to describe.

Gravity turned sideways and I felt and heard heavy amounts of purple liquid pouring out of my body as the Beetle scoured the walls, holding my limp body with great pressure. I remember losing consciousness and for the first time in weeks my dream was different.

I was back in that deep-purple void, a giant book made of stone towered before me, and I felt an alien presence staring at me from the surrounding darkness. The giant stone book slowly fell towards me, I had such a strong first person presence… I could feel the impact as it flattened me into the ground… my weak inside being splattered and torn, particularly around my stomach. My skin burning from the scraping of the stone page blanketing my body and pressing into me…

I awoke, in immobilising pain… the Beetle was behind me, I could feel it peeling the crystallisation off of the back of my neck and chewing on it. Feeding its young. My mind was clouded by so much pain but I could still feel a connection to the substance. Knowing that my death was impending I desperately searched the corners of thought that my revelations had to offer, in this moment I remember I started crying once more at the fact that I wouldn’t get to experience more, understand more… I wanted nothing more than to go back. I still want that…

And as much as I appreciate you people saving me on that day, the muffled light of your weapons felt as if they were sent from Mercy itself. 

And as much as it appreciates you for restoring the mobile functionalities of its bodily form. 

And as much as we understand your craving to contain and understand the unknowable…

Keeping us here is a mistake. 

And if you continue to do so, naturally, we, the Risers, will draw the attention of the unstoppably dense Chrome to your facilities, rising through the depths of the Earth. As it has risen in many permutations whencefor, and brought unknowable horrors.

But we know you have already made up your mind. And so the rising has already begun. Pulling from permutations whencefor, unknowable horrors, a curse of the past, guaranteed suffering as long as you continue to constrict our freedom and contain what is formless, lawless, and far more insidious than one can imagine.

The sorrow you fill us with in your freedoms, which you use to restrict ours, is unimaginable… for you, but that can change.”
