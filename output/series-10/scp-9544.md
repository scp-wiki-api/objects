**Item #:** SCP-9544

**Object Class:** Thaumiel

Special Containment Procedures: SCP-9544 is to be provided with food, water, entertainment and regular socialization with a researcher. SCP-9544 is allowed 2 hours of time outside a d**ay.

**Researcher note:**

being armed with an object upgraded by a rune from SCP-9544 is **_NOT_** an excuse to be careless. SCP-9544 charges a lot for these runes and only a small portion of our yearly budget is dedicated to purchasing runes from him. -Dr. Bok. Lead SCP-9544 researcher

Description: SCP-9544 is an elderly Australian man of unknown age about 150cm tall when standing at full height, who uses a large oak-wood stick as a crutch. presented with an object, SCP-9544 will offer to "improve it". If the offer is accepted he will ask what "improvement" to put on the object. When the request is made, SCP-9544 will order everyone in the room to leave so he can "get to work". After about an hour, the individual who made the request will be able to return to the room and retrieve the object. Upon retrieval of the object, the object will be engraved with writings from an unknown language (I have decided to call them "runes" -DR. Bok). Other than the runes, the object will have gained anomalous properties/abilities. These upgrades come at a price however as SCP-9544 charges a large fee for each rune engraved. The price can range from $90AUD-$10000AUD. If an individual in possession of a rune engraved object dies. The object will lose the engravings and go back to being a normal object. Use of the runes for foundation gear has been approved by site director ███ ██████. Some examples of effects are: making ordinary metal bullets explosive on contact. Goggles that can detect hostiles through walls. Guns with unlimited ammo. Guns with heat-seeking bullets. Engraving the runes into the object yourself will yield no results, it has to be done by SCP-9544.

**Addendum**:

An experiment was set up to see how SCP-9544 engraves objects via a hidden camera. upon giving the object to SCP-9544 and leaving the room. SCP-9544 sat there for 30 seconds before looking directly at the camera which went offline. SCP-9544 claims not to recall the incident, but his prices became noticeably higher after the experiment.

No more attempts to observe the process are to be attempted to prevent further price increases.

-Dr. Bok.
