**Item #**: SCP-9552

**Object Class**: Safe

**Special Containment Procedures**: SCP-9552 and SCP-9552-B are to be stored in separate containment lockers for inanimate objects. Personnel wearing SCP-9552-B are to come into contact with SCP-9552 exclusively for testing purposes, only by level 4 or higher personnel. Class-D personnel are not to be used for testing of SCP-9552 (see Incident-9552-D). Any access to the containment rooms without authorization will be severely reprimanded. SCP-9552-B is to be marked with a spot of red spray-paint to distinguish it from other gloves of its type.

**Description**: SCP-9552 is a 16 Oz Rubber Assembly Mallet belonging to no known brand. When wielded without SCP-9552-B, SCP-9552 has no anomalous properties.

SCP-9552-B is a plain yellow rubber glove belonging to no known brand. When worn, SCP-9552-B allows the wearer to induce the anomalous effect of SCP-9552. Activating SCP-9552’s anomalous effect requires striking an object with either flat face of the mallet-head. Once an object is struck in this manner, the first stage of the anomalous effect will begin. During this stage, cracks will appear on the surface of the object, spreading over the entire area. The space between these cracks appears black in color. This stage can take anywhere from 5 - 60 seconds, depending on the size of the object in question.

Stage 2 occurs once the aforementioned cracks have propagated across the object’s entire surface. The object will emit sounds comparable to those of glass ‘squeaking’ when under tension, before shattering into approximately [(object mass in kg)+1]^9 shards. These shards appear not to follow the constraints of gravity, floating outwards radially from the geometric centre of the object. The shards proceed to completely disintegrate, being completely erased from existence with no traces left in all cases. This stage can take anywhere from 5 - 30 seconds. Liquids and gases appear to be completely unaffected by SCP-9552.

**Incident-9552-D**:

Subject: D-3434

Aims of testing: determine the extent of SCP-9552’s ability.

Procedure: D-3434 to be introduced to a room containing the following: 1 apple, 1 brick, 1 uprooted fir tree. D-3434 instructed to strike each object once using SCP-9552 without SCP-9552-B worn, and once with SCP-9552-B worn.

Result without SCP-9552-B worn:

* Apple: inflicted a large dent.

* Brick: no effect.

* Fir tree: no effect.

Result with SCP-9552 worn:

* Apple: disintegrated completely within 10 seconds total

* Brick: disintegrated completely within 46 seconds total

* Fir tree: disintegrated completely within 83 seconds total

D-3434 was instructed to replace SCP-9552 and SCP-9552-B, but ignored the instruction. He proceeded to use SCP-9552 to disintegrate the door to the testing chamber and was terminated by guards shortly after. SCP-9552 and SCP-9552-B were recovered by guards without incident. Containment procedures updated accordingly.
