Item Number: SCP-9683

Object Class: Thaumiel

Special Containment Procedures: Given the size of SCP-9683, physical containment is not possible. Monitoring equipment is located at Site-██. In the event of an anomaly in light shift and opening of space gate, civilians who have been affected are to receive a Class-A amnestic in accordance with Protocol ████.

Description: SCP-9683 is a sentient being in the galaxy Andromeda. SCP-9683 displaces part of space-time creating a stable Einstein-Rosen bridge which enables one to travel instantly to any coordinate in the entire observable universe.

In the process of communicating with SCP-9683, its intelligence has been revealed to show the ability to communicate using spoken patterns of the ███████ British dialect. SCP-9683 acts as the backup protocol in the event of handling SCP-001. In case of an RK-Class breach of SCP-001 The Scarlet King. SCP-9683 will serve as additional containment measure. SCP-9683 does not seem to have any hostile intentions towards humanity. Communication with SCP-9683 is restricted to Level 5 personnel and requires authorization from 05-██.
