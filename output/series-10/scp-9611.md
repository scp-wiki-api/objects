**Special Containment Procedures:** Due to SCP-9611's anomalous properties, the Foundation keeps it housed in an 8 × 8 metal room with nothing but a single lightbulb to illuminate the surroundings and the materials needed to assemble the table. Personnel do not need to take precautions before entering the room or when interacting with the anomaly, but they are instructed to ensure that all materials remain present in the room before they exit. It is imperative that all personnel remain calm and level-headed if attempting to interact with SCP-9611.

**Description:** SCP-9611 is a 110 × 70 cm brown oaken dining room table originating from the furniture store IKEA. It consists of four legs, four side panels, and a rectangular top that is screwed into place. Instruments needed to assemble the table include a Phillips-head screwdriver and Allen key, along with twelve 8 mm screws.

![](https://cdn.imgchest.com/files/877c17e65cbf.png)

Illustrated diagram of SCP-9611 prior to assembly.

When constructing the table, the anomaly's properties seem to prohibit its construction from completing. Despite the table having each part needed and put into place, upon tightening the final screw, a random part of the table will collapse with no explanation. The amount each screw is tightened is irrelevant; regardless of its tension, the screw is still susceptible to coming loose, with the rest of the table following due to structural compromise. If materials aside from the ones provided are used with the anomaly, SCP-9611 will seemingly reject their presence and eject the materials backwards toward the user. Similar occurrences will happen with equipment such as drills; their use will force the materials out of SCP-9611 and back at the user.

Attempts to destroy SCP-9611 prove unsuccessful. No matter the method used, SCP-9611 is impervious to all kinds of damage, including but not limited to: fire, falling from extreme heights, mallets, hammers, and other brute forces striking its body, running it over with a vehicle, and animal bites.

A single instruction manual is provided alongside the anomaly, printed in English with picture depictions of the steps needed to construct the table. Following the instructions will yield the same results, in that the table will not be able to finish construction and will reject the final piece needed. Attempts to construct the anomaly in a way other than depicted will not work.

It has been observed that failed attempts tend to lead to elevated anger levels, though it is not known at this time if these are a result of the anomaly or simply the failure of constructing the entity. Scientists are currently evaluating this hypothesis.

### ADDENDUM 9611.1 - Supplemental Testing Archive

The following records are condensed from attempts to determine whether SCP-9611's assembly failure can be overcome. Tests continue to be inconclusive.

<details>
<summary>+ ACCESS SUPPLEMENTAL ASSEMBLY RECORDS</summary>

**TEST 04 — FAILED**

A Foundation maintenance technician was instructed to assemble SCP-9611 without consulting the provided manual. The final fastener ejected upon tightening and the structure collapsed.

---

**TEST 07 — FAILED**

The supplied screwdriver and Allen key were replaced with functionally identical Foundation-issued tools. Both replacement tools were forcibly ejected toward the subject upon contact with SCP-9611.

---

**TEST 11 — FAILED**

Industrial adhesive was applied to the joints before assembly. The adhesive separated from SCP-9611's surface in a single intact layer immediately before the final screw was tightened.

---

**TEST 16 — FAILED**

Personnel attempted to secure the completed structure with external clamps before tightening the final screw. All clamps were ejected simultaneously when the final fastener was turned.

---

**TEST 22 — FAILED**

Assembly was attempted remotely using a mechanical manipulator. The manipulator sustained no permanent damage; the final screw was ejected and SCP-9611 collapsed as in previous trials.

---

**TEST 29 — FAILED**

All twelve screws were tightened incrementally in alternating order so that no single fastener could be identified as the final point of assembly. Upon the last adjustment required to bring all screws to equal tension, one screw detached and the structure collapsed.

---

**TEST 31 — FAILED**

A professional furniture assembler was given unlimited time and instructed to consider the assembly complete at their own discretion. After confirming that no further work was necessary, the subject stepped away. SCP-9611 remained intact for 3.4 seconds before collapsing.

### ADDENDUM 9611.2 - Interview 9611.1

> **Interviewed:** Class-D ████

> 

> **Interviewer:** Doctor Evans

> 

> **Foreword:** The first Class-D to attempt to assemble SCP-9611 after its arrival, Class-D ████, was interviewed shortly after to analyze his thoughts and feelings.

> 

> **[BEGIN LOG]**

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** Had you heard anything about this particular anomaly before you came face to face with it?

> 

> **████:** Nah. Gossip gets you in the know how fast, but that table has been here for a day, and I was the first to see it. No way would anyone have known about it. I wish *I* didn't know about it.

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** Why? Did it elicit any particular, strong emotions from you?

> 

> **████:** Don't pretend like you don't know.

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** I'm not trying to—

> 

> **████:** It *pissed* me off. It would've pissed you off, too. Before I was thrown in here, my least favorite thing to do was assemble furniture. And now you've got me doing it right before I die.

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** Surely your sentence can't be too close. *(She coughs.)* Uh, regardless, you only felt anger? Nothing else?

> 

> **████:** Want me to pull out a thesaurus? I was angry. Mad. Frustrated. Irate. Peeved. Furious. Shall I go on?

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** No.

> 

> **████:** I already know your next question. The entire experience is what made me angry.

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** *(Sighs.)* ████, please respect me. I'm just trying to do my job.

> 

> **████:** *(Grumbles something unintelligible.)* Sorry. I'm just… still mad. I haven't had enough time to cool off.

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** That's okay. What happened when you tried to construct the entity?

> 

> **████:** It wouldn't construct. That's the thing. No matter what I put where, no matter how many times I did it right, it kept… *rejecting* every single piece I tried to put on last. It was completely functional and fine until I got to the last screw. Then it would launch the screw back at my eye as soon as I tightened it, and I'm pretty sure I could've gone blind from the speed it threw itself out. Then the entire thing would just collapse, and it was like I'd done no work at all.

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** Did your anger feel unnatural?

> 

> **████:** No. I don't think so, at least. I don't know. Everyone knows the stress of furniture that refuses to assemble. This one just refused to assemble at any cost, and that's what pissed me off the most.

> 

> **[END LOG]**

> 

> **Closing Statement:** ████ was escorted back to his cell. Multiple other Class-D's were instructed to try to assemble SCP-9611, to no avail, and they showed similar levels of agitation.

> **████████:** You tell me, doc. How many men are going mad from this fucking table?

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** We don’t have a determinate number yet.

> 

> **████████:** Someone died, didn’t they?

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** You know I’m not allowed to share those details with you, ████████.

> 

> **████████:** If no one had died, you would’ve just said so.

> 

> *Silence again.*

> 

> **████████:** You’ve been quiet long enough. It’s a lot, isn’t it? Nail right through the fuckin’ eye, wasn’t it? Right?

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** No.

> 

> **████████:** Right. It’s some compulsive need, isn’t it? You guys have to understand *everything.* Even this fucking thirty-eight dollar nightstand from a Swedish furniture company. You’re killing people over a table because you can’t stand not understanding it. You’re sacrificing your cattle’s life for no reason.

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** ████████, you know you’re not allowed to speak to us that way.

> 

> **████████:** I’m dying soon anyway. I’ve been aching to get that off my chest. How can you sleep at night?

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** For seven and a half hours, usually. Look, ████████, we don’t know what this thing is capable of. That's the problem. Maybe it's nothing more than a table that can’t be put together. Maybe it isn’t. We don’t get to assume the former because we’d prefer it to be true.

> 

> **████████:** Say somebody actually builds the fucking thing. Then what?

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** We study the completed object.

> 

> **████████:** So putting it together doesn't even end this?

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** Again, anomalous properties are—

> 

> **████████:** So there's nothing it could do that would make you stop.

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** ████████, that's not what I said.

> 

> **████████:** Then tell me what would.

> 

> *Silence.*

> 

> **████████:** Yeah. So what happens to us?

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** What do you mean?

> 

> **████████:** If you never stop. You build the table, you study the table. You figure out what it does, then you figure out why it does it. Then what? You find something else to shove in front of us?

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** Your involvement with this particular anomaly will end when testing no longer requires you.

> 

> **████████:** That's not what I asked.

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** Then I don't understand the question.

> 

> **████████:** What happens when we're done?

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** ████████—

> 

> **████████:** There has to be a fucking *done,* doesn't there?

> 

> *Dr. Evans does not respond.*

> 

> **████████:** Fuck. You know, that's the only reason I do any of this shit. I keep thinking if I make it through one more test, one more month, whatever, eventually, I'm out. I see my wife again. My kids.

> 

> **Dr. Evans:** ████████, there is no version of your life where you walk out of this place and go back to the one you had before. The sooner you accept that, the better.

> 

> **[END LOG]**

> 

> **Closing Statement:** For the next interview with the same subject, equip him with the proper gear to measure his vitals. It is imperative to see whether or not the anomaly is affecting his disproportionate anger.
