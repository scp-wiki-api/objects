**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9620 is to be stored in a sealed room accessible only through a controlled area. Both entry into and exit from the room must be subject to mandatory inspection in order to prevent the accidental removal of SCP-9620 or any of its components from the secured area.

Use of SCP-9620 is permitted exclusively as part of approved experiments. Following each use, both the containment room and personnel involved are to be inspected for traces of the makeup.

**Description:** SCP-9620 is a cosmetic palette measuring 20 × 10 cm and containing makeup consisting primarily of white and black pigments. The object is visually indistinguishable from standard makeup palettes intended for creating a mime appearance.

No anomalous effects are initially observed when SCP-9620 is applied to a person's face. Anomalous activity manifests after the subject begins mimicking actions or objects traditionally associated with mime performances, including opening invisible doors, interacting with invisible walls, and manipulating other imaginary objects.

When depicting a wall, the subject begins to perceive it as a physically existing barrier. Other individuals not affected by SCP-9620 are able to move through the corresponding area without obstruction. The subject, however, continues to perceive the depicted barrier as physically present and is unable to pass through it.

Removing the makeup from the subject's face does not terminate the effect. The mechanism by which SCP-9620 maintains anomalous objects after the removal of the makeup remains unknown.

The amount of makeup contained within SCP-9620 is not constant. After the palette is closed and subsequently reopened, its contents are restored to their original volume.

The mechanism responsible for SCP-9620's anomalous properties remains unknown. According to subjects' reports, physical interaction with a depicted object can be initiated simply by mentally anticipating the interaction. For example, if a subject expects to touch a previously depicted wall during their next movement, they begin to perceive its physical surface after several moments.

According to subjects' reports, the properties of perceived objects correspond to their subjective mental representation of those objects. In particular, subjects are capable of perceiving the texture, smell, and taste of objects depicted using SCP-9620.

The effects of objects created using SCP-9620 appear to be limited to the subject who created them. Interaction between such objects and other individuals is either impossible or produces no observable effects.

# **Notable Experiments**

****Experiment 4****

**Date:** [DATA EXPUNGED]

Two subjects, both of whom had previously applied SCP-9620, were selected for the experiment. The purpose of the experiment was to determine the nature of interactions between objects created by SCP-9620, other subjects, and the surrounding environment.

Subject #1 depicted a 9 mm handgun in their hand, while Subject #2 depicted a wooden target. Following several shots fired at the target, no damage to either the target or the surrounding room was observed.

Subject #1 was then instructed to fire several shots at Subject #2 from close range. Following three shots to the abdominal region, no injuries to Subject #2 were observed.

Subject #1 was subsequently instructed to fire the weapon at themselves.

After a brief hesitation, Subject #1 aimed the depicted handgun at their own head and fired.

The effects manifested immediately. Subject #1 sustained a gunshot wound to the head and was pronounced dead at the scene.

****Experiment 19****

**Date:** [DATA EXPUNGED]

A subject characterized by a low capacity for visualizing objects was selected for the experiment.

The purpose of the experiment was to determine the effects of SCP-9620 when the subject's mental representation of the created object is insufficiently detailed.

The subject was instructed to depict a closed box with a volume of 1.5 m³ and place themselves inside it. The box was to be constructed from a material that, according to the subject's own perception, could be easily broken or dismantled. An additional requirement was that the subject visualize the interior of the box without attempting to visualize its exterior.

According to the subject's description, the box was made of polystyrene foam.

When attempting to escape the created object, the subject discovered that they were unable to break through it. After breaking through the first layer, the subject continued to perceive another layer behind it, characterized by greater density.

After several minutes of unsuccessful attempts to escape, the subject entered a state of panic and began removing SCP-9620 from their face.

Removal of the makeup resulted in the subject losing the ability to affect the created object in any manner. This significantly complicated the termination of the experiment, resulting in the subject being [DATA EXPUNGED].

****Experiment 28****

**Date:** [DATA EXPUNGED]

A subject with previous professional experience as a diver was selected for the experiment.

The subject was instructed to carefully depict a diving suit and the associated equipment, including an air tank.

After completing the depiction, the subject was submerged to a depth of 3 m.

After a period of time, fluid was observed entering the subject's lungs, despite the subject continuing to perceive the created equipment as functional. The subject remained capable of staying underwater and continued performing movements consistent with those of a diver.

After 20 minutes of submersion, observations of the subject's behavior suggested that the supply of air within the depicted tank had been depleted.
