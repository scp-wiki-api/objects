**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9621 is currently uncontained, with containment efforts ongoing. All current procedures aim to contain individuals affected by the anomaly.

Any discovered SCP-9621-A instance is to be contained in an individual humanoid containment chamber at Site-17. All contained instances are to follow the "9621-A procedure" during containment.

<details>
<summary>SHOW 9621-A PROCEDURE</summary>

All following procedures are in place to prevent any contained subjects from creating imagery resembling SCP-9621. 

Each contained instance must be equipped with the following items:

* Restrictive hand-covering restraints

* Custom lip guard

* Protective headgear

* Specialized indoor footwear

These items are to remain implemented on all contained instances at all times. All necessary activities that cannot be performed by themselves, must be assisted by Foundation personnel. 

Whilst contained, each instance must be monitored by Level 2 personnel at all times to ensure all equipped items remain intact. In the event where an equipped item is damaged, a trained member of personnel is to fix the item whilst it is still attached to the instance. If at any point a contained SCP-9621-A instance is to successfully produce a visual representation of SCP-9621, all visual feed of their containment chamber is to be halted. Upon the instance's expiry, on-site personnel are to remove the instance and incinerate them before visual feed is resumed.

If a member of personnel is believed to have viewed an SCP-9621-A instance upon them producing a visual representation of SCP-9621, they are to be contained as an SCP-9621-A instance. Additionally, no member of Foundation personnel is to attempt to create a visual depiction of SCP-9621 without permission from the O5 Council.

</details>

All SCP-9621-B instances are currently contained in a secure storage locker at Site-17. Handling of these instances requires permission from Dr. Thomas.

Currently, there are 141 SCP-9621-A instances in containment, and 399 SCP-9621-B instances in containment.

**Description:** SCP-9621 is believed to be a single humanoid entity of unknown origin. The entity is estimated to be 1.9 m tall. Skin is white, with a texture similar to slightly creased paper with no signs of sexual organs or features aside from facial features. SCP-9621's eyes are two black, circular scribbles, no visible nose and a mouth that is a small, thin and black horizontal line. It has never been observed to alter its facial expression. On each hand, the entity has only four fingers, lacking thumbs. Feet are absent of toes, however it remains in proportion with the rest of the entity's body.

SCP-9621's behaviour involves targeting individuals that have a hobby or career revolved around image creationPhotography and AI image creation as primary hobbies or careers do not cause SCP-9621 to target the individual.. All targeted individuals are classified as SCP-9621-A instances, with the entity able to target multiple individuals at once. Upon an instance being targeted, a process known as a "Griffonage Event" will begin.

<details>
<summary>SHOW GRIFFONAGE EVENT PROCESS</summary>

The following is a typical recorded development of a Griffonage Event:

**Days 1-6:** Individuals will report losing interest in all recreational activities, aside from their primary image creation hobby. SCP-9621-A instances will see SCP-9621 in their peripheral vision, however it is unknown if this is SCP-9621 or a hallucination. Notably, they will also report audible hallucinations of sounds they associate with their primary method of image creation, whilst not engaging with it.

**Days 7-14:** Affected individuals start to lose interest in maintaining their social life, basic hygiene and basic survival necessities with a sole focus on engaging with their primary image creation method. Instances begin to believe they are serving an entity they refer to as "The Scribbled One", the method in which they learn this name is currently unknown. They will continue to create imagery, which they believe is their way in serving SCP-9621.

**Days 15+:** SCP-9621-A instances grow an urge to create an image of SCP-9621 through their preferred method. Upon them successfully creating an image of SCP-9621, the Griffonage Event will end. Urges to create an image of SCP-9621 will grow stronger the longer a Griffonage Event lasts. All psychological effects of a Griffonage Event will immediately cease upon the creation of an image of SCP-9621.

</details>

Upon the completion of a Griffonage Event, SCP-9621 will emerge from the created image of the entity. It will initially appear as a liquid form, representing a white ink that slowly solidifies as it fully emerges. Witnesses have reported a sound of tearing paper during the entity's emergence. SCP-9621 will then chase the person that created its image. Once it reaches the individual, it will grab them and produce an unknown substance from its eyes until it covers the individual. This substance has an unknown chemical structure, however it is visually identical to black ink. Once the substance touches an individual's skin, it will instantly harden. Upon the individual being fully covered, SCP-9621 will tear the hardened substance off the individual's body, with the substance instantly de-manifesting upon contact with SCP-9621's skin.

Once all of the hardened substance is removed from the individual's body, the entity will liquify and de-manifest back into the surface used to create the image of it. Notably, SCP-9621's liquid form at this stage is a deep red colour, however it still makes a paper-tearing sound. Following the attack, the individual's body is transformed into an arts and crafts structure, composed of papier-mâché, paper, card, red ink and pipe cleaners. The structure represents the shape of the individual, however they are considered deceased upon this transformation being complete. In some instances, affected individuals exhibit involuntary movements post-transformation however the mechanism causing it is unknown. 

If any individual observes an SCP-9621 attack, they will immediately become an SCP-9621-A instance and starts a Griffonage Event. Observers have reported that aside from its emergence and de-manifestation, the entity produces no audible sounds. It has been confirmed that any individual who attempts to draw SCP-9621 will cause the entity to emerge from the used surface and attack the artist. These individuals don't have to be involved in a Griffonage Event, and the drawing doesn't need to look like SCP-9621, as long as the artist intended on depicting the entity, it will cause its attack. However, writing of names for the entity does not cause a manifestation, unless an image is drawn next to the label.

During observed manifestations, SCP-9621 appears to display spatial awareness however its intelligence levels are unclear due to no expressed emotions and no responses to any external stimuli. Aside from engaging in the assault, SCP-9621's only other exhibited behaviour is stroking the victim before de-manifesting. The reason for this behaviour is unclear. Despite these observations, a containment attempt (SEE ADDENDUM 1) suggests SCP-9621 may be an intelligent entity, however this is still unconfirmed.

SCP-9621-B instances refer to the drawing created by the SCP-9621-A instance, depicting SCP-9621. Upon SCP-9621 de-manifesting, SCP-9621-B instances transform into a highly detailed image of the victim. The image is believed to show the victim's final facial-expression prior to SCP-9621's attack, however this is unconfirmed.

**Discovery:** SCP-9621’s existence was confirmed in 1979, following a Foundation-led investigation into multiple disappearances of individuals and the unexplained appearance of large quantities of arts and crafts materials. Research into victims' behaviour prior to their disappearance revealed they'd been isolating themselves from others, and engaging in image creation for "The Scribbled One". It is believed that SCP-9621 has existed longer than this, as various believed SCP-9621-B instances have been located near cave paintings in Indonesia.

**Addendum 1:** After these discoveries, the following containment attempt was carried out Site-17, following orders of lead researcher Dr. Thomas.

= **CONTAINMENT ATTEMPT**

---

**DATE:** 12/09/1979

**NOTE:** D-009 was ordered to draw an image of SCP-9621 inside of a containment chamber at Site-17. The paper used to draw the entity had an incendiary device attached set to trigger automatically upon SCP-9621 fully emerging.

---

[BEGIN LOG]

**Dr. Thomas:** (over intercom) D-009, please draw SCP-9621.

**D-009:** Can you tell me what it looks like maybe?

**Dr. Thomas:** (over intercom) Draw what you believe the anomaly looks like.

**D-009:** Alright, if you say so.

*D-009 draws a stick figure with no facial features and labels the drawing as "SCP-9621". They hold the drawing up to an attached camera in the room.*

**D-009:** Is this go—

*SCP-9621 begins to emerge from the image drawn by D-009. Dr. Thomas turns away from the visual feed to prevent exposure to the entity's effects.*

**D-009:** Wait.. Wait. (yelling) WHAT THE FU—

*SCP-9621 grabs D-009 and begins to cover them in an unknown substance. Once the substance hardens it tears it off revealing D-009 to be composed entirely of arts and crafts material. The attached device used was activated to incinerate the used paper.*

**Dr. Thomas:** (over intercom) On-site personnel prepare weaponry if entity attempts to breach containment.

*Upon D-009 being transformed, the subject appears to violently twitch and leak a deep red fluid. SCP-9621 turns to where the paper was and stares at it for a few seconds before turning to the containment chamber door and freezing in place. Muffled screams can be heard from outside the containment chamber.*

**Dr. Thomas:** (over intercom) What's going on?

*SCP-9621 begins to twitch as screams continue. Feed cuts to outside the chamber where a personnel member is clutching their head whilst screaming.*

**Personnel:** (screaming) FUCK... FUCK... MAKE IT STO—

*Personnel member pauses, points their firearm at another nearby personnel member and opens fire. The personnel member is killed as the other personnel member begins to use the exposed blood to form an image of SCP-9621.*

**Dr. Thomas:** (over intercom) Stop him... STOP HIM!

*Other personnel members pull them away, however SCP-9621 begins to disintegrate in their containment chamber before emerging from the drawing and assaulting the personnel member. During this, on-site personnel looked away from the affected personnel member. Following the attack, the entity de-manifested. An SCP-9621-B instance formed.* 

[END LOG]

Following this containment attempt, all future containment attempts of SCP-9621 were abandoned with focus shifted towards containing SCP-9621-A instances. It is believed the entity is capable of controlling the progression of Griffonage Events, which is why the personnel member drew the entity. The reason for the current consistent escalation of the Griffonage Event following this observation is unknown.

Additionally, upon removal of the SCP-9621-B instance, cleaners reported hearing screaming that sounded similar to the personnel whilst cleaning the instance that depicted them. 

**Addendum 2:**  Following this report, various tests were done on contained SCP-9621X-B instances, in an attempt to trigger reported sounds from cleaned instances.

 **Log Format:**

> **Used instance:** [SUBJECT USED]

> **Medium:** [WHAT THE SCP-9621-B INSTANCE WAS FORMED WITH]

> **Performed action:** [WHAT WAS DONE ON THE SCP-9621-B INSTANCE]

>  **Result:** [WHAT OCCURED DUE TO THE PERFORMED ACTION]

Testing Researcher: Dr. Thomas

> **Used instance:** SCP-9621-B-05.

> **Medium:** Paper and ink.

> **Performed action:** Water was poured on the instance.

> **Result:** Gurgling sounds could be heard, matching how the individual spoke.

> **Used instance:** SCP-9621-B-11.

> **Medium:** Paint on a canvas.

> **Performed action:** The canvas was torn.

> **Result:** Loud scream could be heard as instance was ripped. Scream matched how the portrayed individual would scream.

> **Used instance:** SCP-9621-B-02.

> **Medium:** Chalk on a chalkboard.

> **Performed action:** Chalk was used to scribble over instance.

> **Result:** Sound of flesh being cut alongside a loud scream that matched the portrayed individual's scream.

> **Used instance:** SCP-9621-B-20.

> **Medium:** Affected individual's armIt appears that if the drawing is performed on the individual, the part portraying SCP-9621, will not be transformed alongside the rest of the subject..

> **Performed action:** Intense heat was applied to the arm.

> **Result:** Audible screams could be heard, arm began to shake as individual's blood began to leak out of the drawing.

*We tested the arm after this test due to the anomalous amount of blood produced. During this test, we found no traces of ink that was present prior. More testing required. -Dr. Thomas*

> **Used instance:** SCP-9621-B-01.

> **Medium:** Ink on paper.

> **Performed action:** Ink was chemically analysed. 

> **Result:** Ink was 100% chemically identical to the portrayed victim's blood.

*All testing involving SCP-9621-B instances has been suspended pending ethical review. -Dr. Thomas*

**Addendum 3:** Incident SCP-9621-B-B

= **VIDEO LOG**

---

**DATE:** 07/11/1982

**NOTE:** Following a failure in the 9621-A procedure, SCP-9621-A-19 was able to draw SCP-9621 by biting their lip to reveal blood, and using the exposed blood on their chamber floor. The instance was then attacked by SCP-9621. The following video log was recorded afterwards.

---

[BEGIN LOG]

*Pile of arts and crafts materials are present on the floor. On-site personnel member enters cell to remove the transformed instance.*

**Personnel 1:** I hate doing this.

*As personnel member begins cleaning up the instance, the transformed subject suddenly begins to violently twitch, and emitting noise.*

**SCP-9621-A-19:** (muffled screams)

**Personnel:** Wha... What the fuck?

*Subject stands and walks slowly towards personnel. Subject's arms twitch with red ink leaking from various spots where joints would be. Legs begin to compress.*

**SCP-9621-A-19:** (muffled yell) HELP!

**Personnel:** (panicked shouting) I— what the hell—

*Subject's leg snaps off as red ink continues to leak from more points on their body. As they go to grab the personnel member, the member pulls out a gun and terminates the instance.*

---

[END LOG]

**Addendum 4:** In an attempt to fully understand this recorded incident, Dr. Thomas ordered a test. D-085 was ordered to draw SCP-9621 and told after they were "transformed", to try move and speak to Dr. Thomas for an interview. Despite these instructions D-085 was unresponsive after being transformed.

Following various other tests on various D-classes, D-043 was the only one who was able to become responsive. The following is an interview log with D-043 following their transformation.

= **INTERVIEW LOG**

---

**DATE:** 07/11/1982

**INTERVIEWER:** Dr. Thomas

**SUBJECT:** D-043

---

[BEGIN LOG]

**Dr. Thomas:** D-043, are you able to hear or respond to me?

*Subject doesn't respond.*

**Dr. Thomas:** I repeat, D-043, are you able to hear or respond to me?

*Subject doesn't respond.*

**Dr. Thomas:** Last attempt before we terminate this test, D-043, are you able to he—

*Subject starts shaking violently as it stands up*

**Dr. Thomas:** D-043, are you able to speak?

*Subject's body begins to compress as red ink leaks from various growing holes across the subject.*

**Subject:** (muffled yelling) YES!

**Dr. Thomas:** Understood. D-043 what sensations are you feeling at this current moment?

*Subject's knee twists under pressure and snaps off, as red ink pours out the exposed wound.*

**Subject:** (muffled yelling) EVERYTHING!

**Dr. Thomas:** Please clarify.

*Subject starts lowering self to the ground, as the subject's limbs begin to twist and break.*

**Subject:** (muffled yelling) JOINTS... BONES!

*Subject collapses into a pile and becomes unresponsive.*

---

[END LOG]

It is unclear at this present moment if all SCP-9621-A instances are alive upon their transformation and only some are able to move, or if only some are alive and these are able to move and speak. No instance has had visible/audible activity last for longer than five minutes upon transformation. All transformed SCP-9621-A instances are to be incinerated for ethical reasons, unless permission is given by Dr. Thomas.
