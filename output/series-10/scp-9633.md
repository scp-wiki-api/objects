**Item #:** SCP-9633

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9633 is to be contained within a disassembled state, within a standardized containment chamber located within Site-19. The chamber itself is to be at least 3.5mx3.5mx3.5m, and is constructed of steel-reinforced concrete. The chamber itself is to have a hexagonal array of around 12 Lang-Scranton Reality Anchors located within the chamber's floor, walls and ceiling. 

Testing of SCP-9633 is to only be authorized by two (2) Level 4 personnel. At least 5 personnel with Hazmat suits are to be within the chamber while one member of personnel inputs the settings. When SCP-9633-3 activates; audiovisual equipment, automatic transcription, translation/interpretation and equipment dedicated to bringing samples are to be activated.

**Description:** SCP-9633 is an eigenmachine configured to be a dimensional creator. SCP-9633 is separated into 4 components: SCP-9633-1, SCP-9633-2, SCP-9633-3, and SCP-9633-4.

SCP-9633-1 is a tripod, approx. 25cm wide, 1.5m long, and 65cm tall, when laid horizontally. SCP-9633-1 has three legs in a triangular pattern, attached to the central base via flexible steel beams. The tripod ~~ is believed to~~ contains at least 5 devices that are crucial to the function of SCP-9633-2 and SCP-9633-3. SCP-9633-1 contains a lithium-ion battery with charge lasting up to 168 hours, and ultra-long cables with plugs belonging to different wall sockets in different region of the world. SCP-9633-1 has the following engraved words located midway within the tripod's length: "HERCULES WORKING INTERNATIONAL".

SCP-9633-2 is an holographic display emanating from a camera located at the top of SCP-9633-1. The holographic display is a floating transparent screen, with the text being translated to the most fluent language of any person seeing it.I.e. a person who is most fluent in Mandarin will see the text in Mandarin, while a person who is most fluent in English will see the text in English. Accents are included. The holographic display has around 6 tabs for creating pocket dimensions, aptly named: "Dimension Settings", "Portal Settings", "Inhabitant Settings", "Worldbuilding Settings", "Physics Settings" and "Miscellaneous Settings". When a person hits the green "CREATE" button, SCP-9633-3 will activate.

SCP-9633-3 is a transparent quartz orb. When SCP-9633-2 is being used, SCP-9633-3 will display a holographic version of the dimension that updates in real-time with the changing of settings. When SCP-9633-3 is activated, the restraints holding SCP-9633-3 in place will be released, and SCP-XXXX-3 will then levitate to a height at the middle of the floor and ceiling height. SCP-9633-3 will then illuminate the room, as an instance of SCP-9633-4 is created. After the creation of SCP-9633-4, SCP-9633-3 will continually keep levitating until the red "CANCEL" button is pressed on SCP-9633-2.

SCP-9633-4 instances is a pocket dimension, with an varying, but possibly indefinite, size of the interior, and fixed size of the exterior, depending on the room's size where SCP-9633-1 is being held. Notably, this is not a hallucination nor a displacement of personnel consciousnesses, but instead an actual tangible experience that can be experienced by personnel within the room, and personnel entering the room through the use of any pre-existing door that existed before SCP-9633-3 was activated.

When the red "CANCEL" button is pressed on SCP-9633-2, SCP-9633-3 will then transform into a black hole, pulling and spaghettifying everything native to the pocket dimension. After the entire pocket dimension has been consumed, SCP-9633-3 will then turn transparent, and the now non-dimension will collapse back to the room where SCP-9633 was located until the activation. SCP-9633-3 will then return to it's resting position within SCP-9633-1 and its restraints will be tightened.

**Addendum 9633-1:** Discovery

SCP-9633 was discovered on October 4th, 1997 when it was purchased by an agent of the Department of Procurement and Liquidation from a garage sale from an unknown elder. The item was bought within a briefcase, with disassembled components of SCP-9633-1 being inside. Along with that, an matte-black envelope was found inside, with two things: an instruction booklet for assembly and disassembly, and a weathered, folded sheet of A4 paper being inside.

Congratulations on buying our newest and greatest product: the **Pocket Dimension Creator!**

# When you're done, just click "Cancel", and it will be over!

We hope that with your experience, you will be amazed beyond your imagination!

Enjoy!

--Hercules Working International

February 1974.

It was kept in storage at Storage Site-33 for 2 months until it was tested in Room 991, and it's anomalous capabilities were revealed.

After an extensive discussion with the O3 Court, it was to be determined that SCP-9633 is to be transferred to Site-19, and have authorization only restricted to any Level 3 personnel stationed on-site at Site-19, contained within a storage locker. However, due to misuse from Level 3 personnel, an discussion with the O3 Court and O4 Council agreed to let two (2) Level 4 personnel stationed on-site to be the only ones who can authorize a test.

Testing continued for 3 years until in 2000, when SCP-9633 anomalous capabilities is believed to have been researched to it's fullest extent. The current containment procedures were drafted in 2000 and were implemented by the Department of Containment in 2001.

**Addendum 9633-2:** Experiment Logs

SCP-9633 was tested from December 1997 to March 1999. Along with that, 3 experiment logs were found noteworthy and has been displayed below. Relevant materials has been collapsed.

**LOG #:** Experiment Log 9633-13

**DATE:** December 23, 1997

**INDIVIDUALS:** D-1089

---

**PROCEDURE:** D-1089, with a hazmat suit, is to configure SCP-9633-4 via SCP-9633-2 to be a snowy landscape resembling North and South America.

**RESULT:** D-1089 was successfully transported to SCP-9633-4(13). D-1089 was not harmed.

**LOG #:** Experiment Log 9633-49

**DATE:** May 9, 1998

**INDIVIDUALS:** D-9532

---

**PROCEDURE:** D-9352 is to configure SCP-9633-4 via SCP-9633-2 to be a diverse fantasy world with generic fantasy species and creatures, along with a large scale of worldbuilding. D-9352 is to stay for at least 21 days.

**RESULT:** D-9352 stayed for 21 days within the real world, but was inside SCP-9633-4(49) for a perceived time of 231 years. D-9352 upon leaving, was frantic but was quickly able to calm down, and received extensive psychological screenings and evaluations for 1 year.

**LOG #:** Experiment Log 9633-102

**DATE:** July 5th, 1999

**INDIVIDUALS:** D-11424

---

**PROCEDURE:** D-11424 is to configure SCP-9633-4 via SCP-9633-2 to be an abstract metaphysical complex pocket dimension that can access reality.

**RESULT:** D-11424, upon leaving, experienced time within SCP-9633-4 for at least 2 million years. D-11424 now exhibited Class-V Scranton-Type abilities, and was cooperative within containment, and was given an SCP classification.
