**Item #:** SCP-9638

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9638 must be kept inside a sealed room at Site-██. Looking inside the containment chamber is strictly prohibited. SCP-9638’s appealing and delicious appearance may deceive personnel and create the urge to consume the doner. A backup generator must be connected to the containment chamber. In the event of any power outage, this generator must be capable of supplying power to SCP-9638 for 24 hours. If SCP-9638’s power source is interrupted for even 1 second, a 24-hour lockdown procedure must be enacted.

**Description:** SCP-9638 is a Remta D08 model doner cooker. A reset occurs every 24 hours. During these reset events, an untouched and cooked chicken log manifests on the doner cooker. Any food prepared using the chicken doner manifested by this doner cooker will be classified as SCP-9638-1.

SCP-9638-1's must not be consumed under any circumstances. Individuals who take even a single bite of SCP-9638-1 will begin to exhibit certain symptoms. A person who consumes even the smallest piece of the food loses control over themselves until the entire meal has been finished. According to victims, the food tastes completely normal and delicious. However, toward the end of the meal, individuals begin to feel as if they are eating insects instead of chicken. After consumption, most victims experience nausea. It has been reported that some victims vomit immediately after consuming the meal. Individuals who consume SCP-9638-1 begin to feel disgust toward chicken döner and refuse to eat it again. In subjects who consume SCP-9638-1 together with fermented dairy products, the effects of SCP-9638-1 have been observed to lessen. For detailed information, refer to Test Log 9638-7.

SCP-9638 must be supplied with power at all times. If SCP-9638 loses power in any way, the doner log inside SCP-9638 will uncontrollably produce salmonella. Until this doner log has been consumed, under no circumstances should any food produced from SCP-9638 be consumed. If consumed, severe nausea and vomiting, abdominal pain, watery or bloody diarrhea, headaches, high fever, and fatigue may occur. Without timely intervention, this may result in death.
