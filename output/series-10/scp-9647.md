**Item #:** SCP-9647

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9647 is kept in a standard containment locker at Site-18. The password for the combination lock is changed monthly. Any researchers wishing to conduct testing must obtain permission from Doctor Mark. SCP-9647 must be handled carefully due to its damaged state.

Instances of SCP-9647-A acquired during testing are stored in Site-18’s High Value Item Vault for further study.

Doctor Mark is forbidden from bringing SCP-9647 or any instances of SCP-9647-A to his weekly D&D sessions in the Site-18 third floor break room.

A request by Agent Montpelier to use SCP-9647 to mass-produce anomalous equipment for field agents is under review.

**Description:** SCP-9647 is a heavily-damaged copy of the “███████ ███████ █████” supplemental book for the tabletop role playing game Dungeons and Dragons. The item has several sections torn out, water damage, markings and stains on many pages, and burn marks throughout.

SCP-9647’s primary anomalous abilities manifest when a conscious human being touches an illustration in the book. This property will only activate when the illustration is less than ~10% damaged or defaced, and some illustrations, primarily ones of locations, lack the effect. Upon making contact with the page, the user will report feeling a physical object corresponding to the contents of the illustration, rather than the page itself. The user is then able to grasp and lift the object out of the book, provided the object is light enough and can fit through the dimensions of the page. Some objects that do not significantly exceed the page’s boundaries, such as armor, will appear to warp the page in order to be extracted.

These objects, hereafter SCP-9647-A, usually exhibit anomalous properties similar to their use in-game, and will retain these properties indefinitely unless they can be "used up". Upon withdrawal of an item, the page will appear to "ripple" as if it were liquid, and the illustration will become monochrome, losing its anomalous properties. It will slowly regain color over the next 24 hours, after which another instance of the item can be withdrawn. A theoretically infinite number of items can be withdrawn in this way; the Foundation currently has 352 instances of SCP-9647-A-3, "Wand of Magic Detection", in containment.

The book possesses some differences in content from the standard-issue ███████ ███████ █████. Instead of a description of each "magic item", an illustration of each item is provided. (In the standard version, a text entry for each item describing its use in the game is given, and only some items are illustrated.) Illustrations of items that were depicted in the original edition are identical to their non-anomalous counterparts. New illustrations are consistent with the art style of the original ones. Some items included in other supplemental books are included, and some are entirely new.

Additionally, in place of the satirical "disclaimer" at the beginning of the book, the following message is written:

> I know you loved this game. Enjoy!

> 

> -Chill Dude

SCP-9647 was found at a thrift shop by Doctor Mark. The proprietor was unable to recall who had brought it in. Noticing its deviations from the standard ███████ ███████ █████, Doctor Mark purchased the book for one dollar due to its heavily damaged state. He discovered its anomalous properties the following day while leafing through it at home, withdrawing an instance of SCP-9647-A-1, "Eversmoking Bottle" (arbitrarily designated as such due to being the first item recovered). Doctor Mark promptly delivered the item to Site-18 for containment. An agent was dispatched to the thrift store in question, but no other wares were found to be anomalous.

The identity of "Chill Dude" is unknown, as is the supposed identity of the recipient. It is assumed that the damage to the book was the result of misusing instances of SCP-9647-A, rather than intentional.

<summary>+ Testing Log 9647</summary>

All tests must have at least one armed guard and one researcher supervising.

D-class personnel are disallowed from participating in testing regarding the withdrawal of anomalous weaponry or powerful items. Such testing is to be done by a Foundation Agent at a testing range.

The following format is to be used for all tests.

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-X (where X is the number assigned to the particular illustration/item)

**Item Name:**

**Description:**

---

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-1

**Item Name:** "Eversmoking Bottle"

**Description:** A round bottle appearing to contain a swirling grey substance, with wisps of it leaking from around the stopper.

Upon being opened, the item released a cloud of dense smoke that filled the chamber. Notably, while Guard Anderson (the guard present at the time of the experiment) reported heavily obscured vision, D-68012 claimed to be fully able to see through the smoke, and correctly determined how many fingers Doctor Mark was holding up from the other side of the room. D-68012 was treated for moderate smoke inhalation.

Later testing outdoors produced a 20 m x 20 m cloud of dense smoke, centered around and moving with the bottle. The cloud was unaffected by wind, and dissipated within 10 minutes of closing the bottle.

---

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-2

**Item Name:** "Cap of Water Breathing"

**Description:** A simple blue cap embroidered with ocean waves. Upon donning the cap, D-68012 was able to stay submerged in a pool of water for upwards of 60 minutes. Biometrics indicated regular respiratory activity during that time.

---

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-3

**Item Name:** "Wand of Magic Detection"

**Description:** A stylized metal wand with a pointed bulb at the tip. Initially thought to have no anomalous properties, it was found to illuminate when pointed at SCP-9647.

Upon authorization by Doctor Mark, an assortment of random items mixed in with several Safe-class SCPs with no externally visible anomalous properties was displayed to D-68012. SCP-9647-A-3 was able to correctly discern which items were anomalous, lighting up when pointed at an SCP.

Note: This could be incredibly useful. It’s literally the most helpful possible property an item could have given our line of work. We tested this with every anomalous object on site and it worked every time. None of our technologies come close to its efficacy. Requesting the extraction of as many possible instances of this item for distribution to sites and MTFs.

 — Doctor Mark

*Approved.*

 — O5-11

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-4

**Item Name:** "Gauntlets of Ogre Strength"

**Description:** An ornate pair of plate gauntlets engraved with roaring humanoid faces (presumably ogres). Upon donning the gauntlets, D-68012 demonstrated the ability to easily lift the 400-kilogram metal table in the testing chamber. Items confiscated to prevent possible escape attempt.

---

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-5

**Item Name:** "Helm of Comprehending Languages"

**Description:** An ornate helmet with a design resembling lettering from various alphabets. Upon donning the helmet, D-68012 was able to understand all spoken and written languages provided, regardless of prior fluency, though he was not granted the ability to speak or write in these languages.

---

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-6

**Item Name:** "Bag of Holding"

**Description:** A small satchel embroidered with the image of a smiling face. The interior of the bag was discovered to be 5 times larger than the exterior, suggesting a spatial distortion. Turning the bag inside out resulted in [DATA EXPUNGED].

---

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-7

**Item Name:** "Bag of Devouring"

**Description:** Identical to SCP-9647-A-6, but the face depicted is scowling. Upon reaching into the bag, D-68012 was violently drawn into the bag by a large, prehensile tongue. Item found to have near identical properties to SCP-101. Research into a shared origin is ongoing. Remains of D-68012 not recovered. Testing aborted for the day.

---

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-8

**Item Name:** "Ring of Water Walking"

**Description:** A silver ring inlaid with blue crystals in the shape of footprints. Upon donning the ring, D-5345 was able to walk across the surface of the testing pool. D-5345 reported feeling a solid, slightly shifting surface beneath his feet. Measurements show no decrease in D-5345’s density or weight.

---

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-9

**Item Name:** "Immovable Rod"

**Description:** A heavy metal rod capped with designs of rearing horses. A small button is on one end. After pressing the button, D-5345 reported that he was unable to move the rod. The item remained stationary in midair, and was unable to be moved even when D-5345 pulled on it with his full strength. Pressing the button deactivated this effect.

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-10

**Item Name:** "Necklace of Fireballs"

**Description:** A quality gold necklace featuring 10 red beads of unknown composition. Said beads were able to be detached easily, and when thrown in the testing range, created large bursts of flame upon impact. Throwing the entire necklace (7 beads) resulted in a much larger explosion, causing severe damage to the testing range.

---

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-11

**Item Name:** "Brazier of Fire Elemental Command"

**Description:** A large, heavy golden bowl engraved with patterns resembling flames attached to a metal stand. SCP-9647 was observed to distort around the item to allow it to pass through.

Upon placing coals in the brazier and igniting them, the flames produced assumed a form similar to SCP-457, which appeared to "leap" out of the bowl. Agent Montpelier, who had previously helped end a containment breach by SCP-457, reflexively fired upon the entity, which retreated, but appeared entirely unaffected.

After the Agent regained his composure, it was found that the entity obeyed simple commands from him, including "come here", "go over there", "burn that" (indicating a wooden test dummy) and "bring me that rock" (entity attempted to pick up the indicated stone, but only managed to char it due to lacking a physical form).

Entity appeared unwilling or unable to travel farther than 100 m from SCP-9647-A-11, and immediately demanifested upon the still-burning coals being doused.

---

**Item Designation:** SCP-9647-A-12

**Item Name:** "Vorpal Sword"

**Description:** A high-quality, abnormally sharp longsword forged with beryllium bronze. Agent Montpelier, upon grasping the hilt, gained an innate knowledge of swordfighting techniques, and demonstrated greatly increased strength and reflexes. Agent Montpelier was able to slice through the wooden testing dummy, a boulder, the reinforced concrete wall, and material from SCP-143.

Item was subsequently ~~stolen~~ confiscated by Doctor Kondraki ~~for personal use~~ for further study.
