**Item #:** SCP-9651

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** Keep SCP-9651 in a containment cell at all times. Do not turn let light in while SCP-9651 is being observed. Researchers and personnel are allowed to talk to SCP-9651.

**Description:** SCP-9651 is a 5'4 humanoid with a hat ragged clothing and two eyes with tiny shining dots. When someone observed SCP-9651 while it is in light it breaks all of their limbs and crushers them into a cube. SCP-9651 will then send out a EMP big enough to turn off the light that caused it. Communication with SCP-9651 is possible and sometimes encouraged. When a individual talks to SCP-9651 it causes a individual to be able to talk about everything that bothering them to SCP-9651 and get it off thier chest.

**Addendum:** 

> FIRST CONTACT:                                             MTF was sent on the 5th of October 2007 to Perth Australia after reports about people turning into "broken cubes" when MTF entered the location they reported smelling rotten meat before seeing a dead person folded into a cube. Around a corner one MTF agent spotted SCP-9651 He called out "get on the ground they is something dangerous in this house". he then turned on a light and got broken and shaped infront of the rest of the MTF before the light went out. SCP-9651 called out to the rest "please I dont want to hurt anybody dont turn on the lights" he reportedly sounded broken and sad.

> Anomalous Profileing: SCP-9651 was kept in the location for 3 days as he explained what it figured out about its effects. MTF Then loaded SCP-9651 into a truck with no light to Site-40. It was put into a standed containment cell with the lights taken out
