**Item #:** SCP-9656

**Object Class:** [Safe-celaris](scp-7188) Object or items related to object are not to be interacted with or perceived under any circumstances.

**Special Containment Procedures:** The door to SCP-9656 is locked with a biometric identification lock. No further security measures required. Testing may only be authorized by two (2) Level 4 personnel stationed on-site at Site-22.

**Description:** SCP-9656 is a vacant room located within Hallway B13 of Site-22. The nameplate on the door of SCP-9656 says "DO NOT ENTER, CONT. CELL OF [UNREADABLE]". SCP-9656 may exist as a logical or ontological gap into Reality itself, however, this has yet to be confirmed or debunked through testing.

**Addendum 9656-2.2:** *Communication Attempt #SCP-9656/04-09-2017-(01)*

Paper notes and writing instruments was to be put in the chamber by a member of D-Class personnel, and then attempt communication by writing on one of the paper notes and then putting it in the chamber.

**Question:** Who are you?

**Response:** I don't know. I forgot my name and my number.

**Question:** How long have you been here for?

**Response:** 40-50 years.

**Question:** Who jailed you here?

**Response:** I was jailed years ago by the Foundation, but it was only till, somewhere between the 80s, that I've been put here.

**Question:** Describe yourself.

**Response:** I think I have olive skin, and the fact that I have brown eyes? I don't know, they never gave me a mirror.

**Question:** What is your number?

**Response:** 8431?

After this, a brief power outage occured in Site-22. The D-Class Personnel was then evacuated.
