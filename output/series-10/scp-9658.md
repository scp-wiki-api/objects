By Vicious Spirit

**Special Containment Procedures:** All villages with an Orthodox Christian church located within 50 kilometers of Wierszalin are to be considered potential resurgence areas. Every year, a Foundation agent must be assigned to each potential resurgence area for the 50 day period from the Orthodox Easter to the Trinity Day. During that time, they must attend Divine Liturgies and use the cover story of being new to Orthodox Christianity in order to form bonds with other members of the congregation and the clergy. SCP-9658's manifestation is usually preceded by:

* Rapid increase in the veneration of Prophet Elijah.

* The unexplained appearance of the Flaming Chariot icon in the church.

* Discussions related to building the “Capital of the World” and a “Paradise on Earth”.

* Sermons about the End of Times.

* The rumors of a “saint” being seen in the area.

When the aforementioned phenomena are observed, the agent must immediately report the assigned area to the SCP-9658 control team and leave it within 24 hours. Any person reported to have contacted SCP-9658 is to be treated according to the Wierszalin Protocol. 

**Description:** SCP-9658 is a recurring entity that manifests as an older Caucasian male and possesses behind the Veil knowledge classified under Level 5 clearance. The first registered sighting of SCP-9658 dates to 1936, when it visited multiple settlements in Eastern Poland, encouraging the residents to build the "Capital of the World" before the "apocalypse". The construction of a village called Wierszalin began in the same year; however, in 1939 local authorities arrested SCP-9658 for leading the unauthorized construction of a temple. The settlement was soon abandoned.

In 2014, the entity began manifesting within a fifty kilometer radius of Wierszalin’s ruins on both Belarusian and Polish sides of the border. While SCP-9658 appears to the civilians and willingly shares the classified information, the entity has consistently demanifested before any capture operations could be set in motion. New methods of detaining SCP-9658 are currently developed to reduce the costs of the Special Containment Procedures.

**Incident W9658:** On the 1st of June, 2024, Foundation agent [REDACTED] failed to report the phenomena preceding SCP-9658 manifestation in the potential resurgence area N58. At the time, he had worked in the same area for 8 years. The agent prioritized his personal and religious feelings, and acquired knowledge classified under Level 5 clearance upon entering unauthorized contact with SCP-9658. While this knowledge of the world behind the Veil has no confirmed anomalous properties, individuals who receive it directly from SCP-9658 show resistance to targeted amnestics. The Wierszalin Protocol is currently the only way to erase the classified information from a person's memory. 

In accordance with the Wierszalin Protocol, [REDACTED] was fully amnesticized, moved to Minsk and given the name Alexander Petrov. His new memories included: 

* Having always lived in Minsk, Belarus. 

* Working a full time job in a fast food chain restaurant.

* Having no desire to pursue any other lifestyle. 

* Having no connection to Orthodox Christianity. 

* Having a lifelong friend Nikolai Kormov, to whom he must confide.

**Post-Incident Report:** On the 2nd of August, 2025, Agent Nikolai Kormov submitted the audio-record of his conversation with Alexander Petrov.

**Petrov:** I can’t live in this damn city anymore. I shouldn’t be here… I don’t belong.

*Petrov stares at the half-empty vodka bottle.*

**Agent Kormov:** What's wrong, Sasha? You used to smile more at school.

*Agent Kormov lightly slaps Petrov's back.*

**Petrov:** It's hard to breathe outside. At work I keep forgetting the customers' orders.

**Agent Kormov:** You loved to work there, didn't you? 

**Petrov:** I used to… but now I think of quitt–

**Agent Kormov:** Why? It’s a stable job and it’s close to home.

*Petrov glances at the photo of his new parents on the wall.*

**Petrov:**  Home... It's so easy to just drink and watch TV after shifts when there's no one telling me what to do.

**Agent Kormov:** Come on, others have it way worse. You have a job and a friend to drink with.

*Agent Kormov pretends to drink a shot.*

**Agent Kormov:** Are you free tomorrow? My Dad has made more of his moonshine.

**Petrov**: I'd rather stop drinking on Saturdays... The next day I always wake up at noon and feel like I missed something I shouldn't have.

*Petrov looks at an empty corner.*Agent Kormov suggested the subject was looking for a "sacred corner", which is where icons are displayed in homes of Orthodox Christians.

**Petrov:** It's like I should've gone somewh--

**Agent Kormov:** Gone where? When was the last time you wanted to wake up on weekends?

*Petrov presses his fist against the bridge of his nose.*

**Petrov:** You're gonna call me crazy, but I've been having this dream where I go through a green field at sunrise: the birds are chirping, a fresh breeze blows into my face. I enter a wooden church...

**Agent Kormov:** Church? Do you even like churches? Remember when we had a party planned and that crazy grandma of yours tried to make you--

**Petrov:** I guess I don't like churches. Just in that dream these people were so nice. We drank tea, I helped them pull the weeds and feed the cattle. Then they called me to the church.

**Agent Kormov:** Villages are boring and they are full of people like your grandma. 

**Petrov:** I don't know. I've always been happy to see this dream but I keep waking up before I can enter the church... I just wish yesterday it had ended there as well.

*Petrov attempts to drink from an empty shot glass.*

**Petrov:**  I saw a flaming chariot icon in front of the altar and ran away. I just sat sobbing under a tree until an old man came and hugged me. I told him that I was nothing but a scorned Judas, that I was not worthy of seeing him!

*Agent Kormov sits up in his chair.*

**Petrov:** His warm smile when he absolved me of my sins... It remained even when he told the End of Times was nigh. He called me to a city without sin, where we'd all build a temple of pure white stone and pure gold domes.The planned scale of the Wierszalin Temple is not known. We would paint it with murals of all of God's miracles, the spiral bell towers would carry our songs to the Seventh Sky... Even when the locusts would swarm the Earth, and the terrible beast would leave the sea, God would spare the temple; he'd bring it to heaven, along with the World's Capital.

**Agent Kormov:** The believers always believe in the strangest things.

**Petrov:** He kept telling me how happy everyone was in paradise... He didn't even remember anything until...

*Alexander Petrov hugs Agent Kormov and whispers into his shoulder.*

**Petrov:**  Until he raised his cassock and... Oh, God, these wounds. 

*Alexander Petrov begins to sob.*

**Petrov:** He came to save us and they sent a firing squad into his cell. He was the only man to enter Heaven without knowing death and they killed him.

*Agent Kormov carefully leaves Petrov's grip and moves his chair away.*

**Agent Kormov:** Dreams are strange, aren’t they, my friend? 

**Petrov:** I saw Prophet Elijah cry, weep at what we did to this world. He came back to paradise and its gates were closed. The cherubs didn't sing, the souls didn't rejoice. There were no saints or angels, there was no God...

*Alexander Petrov stands up and stumbles to an open window, wiping his tears with his shirt.*

**Petrov:** There will be no End of Times. The Capital of the World won't be built, it won’t ascend; Wierszalin will rot here, with us. 

*Alexander Petrov looks down at a busy street.*

**Petrov:** God sits locked in the deep white cel–-

**Agent Kormov:** Come on, you’ve had enough drinks for today. Let’s get you to bed.

*Agent Kormov helps Petrov walk.*

**Petrov:** I'm tired. I want to go to heaven, I really do. I just wish it wasn't empty...

**Incident Conclusion:** Wierszalin Protocol has proven to be ineffective in Alexander Petrov's case. The subject showed strong resistance to amnestics and the gradual restoration of the erased memories. Agent Kormov has reported that the subject is unsatisfied with his assigned lifestyle and experiences intense mental torment. Neutralization request has been forwarded to the Ethics Committee.
