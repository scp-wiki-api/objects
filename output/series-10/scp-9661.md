**Item #:** SCP-9661

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:**

SCP-9661 is to be stored unplugged in a standard Safe-class containment locker at Site-81. Access to SCP-9661 is restricted to authorised personnel only.

SCP-9661 may only be connected to a power source during approved testing or emergency treatment procedures. Under no circumstances should the microwave door remain closed after the timer reaches 00:00.

If an individual becomes affected by SCP-9661, the subject is to be placed under observation and provided with access to a standard microwave oven. The affected individual must operate the microwave and allow its timer to reach 00:00 without opening the door. Personnel must remain present throughout the procedure.

**Description:**

SCP-9661 is an ordinary-looking white household microwave oven with no visible branding. SCP-9661 is functionally identical to a standard microwave and displays no anomalous properties unless operated by a human subject.

If an individual activates SCP-9661 and fails to open the microwave door before the timer reaches 00:00, the subject will begin hearing a continuous microwave beeping sound audible only to themselves. The sound cannot be recorded by any recording device or heard by other individuals.

The auditory phenomenon persists regardless of distance from SCP-9661, preventing sleep and leading to progressively worsening insomnia, paranoia, and cognitive decline. Prolonged exposure results in severe neurological deterioration, ultimately leading to organ failure and death. 

It has been observed that activation of SCP-9661, or any other microwave by an affected subject, followed by allowing the device to reach 00:00 without opening the door, results in immediate cessation of the beeping noise.

**Addendum 9661-1: Recovery of SCP-9661**

SCP-9661 came to the Foundation’s attention after the death of ███ █████ and the subsequent hospitalisation of the spouse ████ █████ in ███████, Ohio.

Both individuals were elderly patients who reported hearing persistent microwave beeping audible only to themselves after operating the same household microwave. Multiple psychiatric evaluations and sleep treatments failed to alleviate symptoms. Medical personnel initially attributed the reports to stress-induced psychosis associated with advanced age and prolonged insomnia.

On the eleventh day following initial exposure, ███ █████ died due to complications of severe sleep deprivation resulting in neurological failure and multi-organ shutdown.

Approximately six hours later, ████ █████ attempted to prepare a meal using the same microwave. CCTV footage showed the subject opening the microwave door before the timer hit reached 00:00, before standing motionless in front of the appliance and eventually collapsing into sleep on the kitchen floor.

Hospital staff later reported the subject remained asleep for seventeen hours and displayed no anomalous symptoms upon waking.

Foundation agents quickly recovered SCP-9661, and ████ █████ was administered amnestics.

**The following are experiments conducted with SCP-9661. They have been listed in chronological order based on the dates the experiments were performed.**

**Date:** 18/09/2015

= **Experiment #9661-01**

**Procedure:**  

D-1184 was instructed to operate SCP-9661, heat a meal up, and open the microwave door before the timer reached 00:00.

**Result:**  

D-1184 complied successfully and reported no anomalous effects.

---

**Date:** 19/09/2015

= **Experiment #9661-02**

**Procedure:**  

D-2031 was instructed to operate SCP-9661 and leave the door closed when the timer reached 00:00. D-2031 was questioned what he heard.

**Result:**  

Subject reported immediate continuous microwave beeping sounds audible only to themselves. No external sound was detected by recording equipment or nearby personnel. Subject exhibited acute distress and sleep deprivation within 24 hours.

---

**Date:** 20/09/2015

= **Experiment #9661-03**

**Procedure:**  

Subject D-2031, still under SCP-9661 effect, was instructed to repeat microwave usage using a standard non-anomalous microwave oven.

**Result:**  

Upon completion of the second microwave cycle (timer reaching 00:00 without opening the door), the subject immediately reported cessation of the auditory phenomenon and showed visible signs of relaxation and fatigue. Subject entered sleep almost immediately.

---

**Date:** 22/09/2015

= **Experiment #9661-04**

**Procedure:**  

D-2290 was instructed to ignore the auditory beeping sounds for an extended period without re-exposure to any microwave device.

**Result:**  

Subject reported escalating sleep deprivation, hallucinations, and severe cognitive decline over a period of 6 days. Experiment terminated following loss of consciousness. Subject recovered after emergency controlled re-exposure protocol using SCP-9661.
