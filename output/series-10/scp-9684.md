**Item #:** SCP-9684 Virtual Reality Glasses

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9684 has been sealed in a special containment box at Site-31 where it as been placed under armed guard and watched by a security camera which uses the entire electromagnetic spectrum.

**Description:** SCP-9684 is a pair of virtual reality glasses.  Anyone who wears the glasses has trouble distinguishing the virtual world from the physical one even after the glasses are removed.  The effects are accumulative and based on two main factors, the amount of time the glasses have been worn and intensity with which the virtual reality has been explored.  This means those who put on the glasses looking for answers are effected even faster.

For the first 2 hours there is only a sense that something is not quite right.  The person will react to ghost images which can not be seen or felt.  More concerningly, they have the desire to keep on using the glasses.  This fades at a rate of roughly 10 minutes for every minute the glasses have been used.

The next stage starts after 2 to 5 hours.  The person will hallucinate seeing objects and characters previously experienced in the virtual world.  Some of the characters resemble previous victims.  These characters react in a way virtual world characters are expected to react.  They know basic information like when the victim was born or where they went to school, but do not know personal information like how they feel about a given situation.  This happens regardless of whether or not the person who the victims beforehand.

From 6 to 12 hours the person loses the ability to distinguish the virtual world from the physical one.  The virtual world becomes more and more real to them until they are unable to sense the physical world at all.  They stop eating and their movements slow.  Eventually even their breathing stops resulting in death.

Although it is fairly easy to remove the virtual reality glasses from someone under its effects, it is suspected more than one such item exists.  Therefore its current object class is Euclid.

**Discovery:** The first known use of SCP-9684 was on April 8, 20XX in the city of [REDACTED].  A package containing SCP-9684 was delivered to Michelle [REDACTED], a girl in her late teens along with a letter saying she had been selected to be a Beta tester for a new virtual reality simulation.  Eagerly she went to the given website, hooked the glasses up to the computer, and tried them on.  

A fantasy city appeared all around her filled with human and humanoid characters of various shapes and sizes.  During her exploration she lost all track of time.  Her father had to physically remove the glasses to get her to come down for supper.

Michelle told her parents all about the virtual world and the things she had discovered there.  Although they had their doubts, her parents told her she could play long as she completed her schoolwork which she did.  The next day she kept seeing and hearing things that were not actually there.  These were things she had experienced in the game earlier.

Michelle felt the only way to stop the hallucinations was to put the virtual glasses back on and keep exploring the virtual world.  This was the exact opposite of what she actually needed.  She lost all interest in the physical world.  Her movements became sluggish.  Her breathing and heart rate slowed.  On the morning of April 10, 20XX she was found dead in her room after playing the game all night.

The local police filed a report which was read by an SCP agent.  A team was immediately sent to contain SCP-9684 and those directly involved were given amnestics.  It is still unknown who sent Michelle the glasses though it is quite clear whoever it was had malicious intent.

**Testing:** Through testing on D-Class Personnel we have learned the virtual world appears different to each person.  It can be fantasy world, a grim city in the 1950s, an underwater city filled with merpeople, or a virtual chatroom.

One of the more disturbing features of SCP-9684 are characters based on previous victims.  Only those people who actually died as a result of SCP-9684 have characters made in their image.  Whether it is some sort of afterlife, simply virtual reality copies of the victims, or something else is unknown at this time.
