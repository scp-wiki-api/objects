**Special Containment Procedures**:

**APPROACHING SCP-9722 CAN POSSIBLY RISK YOUR LIFE.**

Any staff member who accidentally enters a verbal exchange exceeding three minutes with either instance must immediately report to a medical bay for auditory evaluation and mandatory Class-A amnestic administration to mitigate severe cognitive fatigue.

Description: SCP-9722 is the collective designation for two human female siblings, designated SCP-9722-A and SCP-9722-B. While identical to non-anomalous human subjects, both instances cause a continuous localized cognitive effect that induces acute auditory distress, elevated blood pressure, and rapid stress in humans within around 13.33m radius.

The secondary aspect of SCP-9722's anomaly involves an innate compulsion to initiate circular, non-sensical disputes regarding Foundation policies, general ethics, and non-anomalous administrative decisions. The instances display total immunity to logical counter-arguments, escalating verbal volume in response to factual correction.

On 08/01/2026, SCP-9722 somehow, attempted to sue against the SCP foundation Department of Containment, the two siblings wrote a notice, claiming the foundation has "wrongful termination and unethical treatment" of SCP-173. SCP-9722-A demanded the immediate transfer of containment responsibilities to their personal oversight, alongside **5 million USD** in restitution for distress caused by site operations.
