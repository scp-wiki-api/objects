This article contains descriptions of (online) grooming and manipulation and exploitation.

**Item #:** SCP-9723

**Object Class:** Euclid, Hypatia, Perpetua

**Special Containment Procedures:** Program agents are to locate instances and intercept communications with subject if possible. Subjects are to be debriefed through Publicity Department cover story template in conjunction with the Federal Bureau’s policies towards Internet exploitation rings. Additional support recommendations to be given upon subjective assessment. 

**Description:** Discovered in late 2004, initially identified through 4chan message board (9/30/2004) before secondary identifiers located through other online avenues (Omegle, 10/28/2004) (Something Awful, 11/03/2004) (MySpace, 11/03/2004) (rotten.com 11/04/2004) ([slendermansion.com](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-9407) 11/09/2004). SCP-9723 is a paranormality targeting isolated individuals lacking stable connective social circles, sometimes existing in more extreme areas of the internet. Initial interaction appears as a series of conversations with an obscure user interacting with subject. User self-descriptors, personality, temperament do not remain consistent per instance, as every instance appears to roughly model around what subject may perceive as personally idealistic to them. Despite this, SCP-9723 may lose track of self-descriptors, creating noticeable yet minor discrepancies in topics such as but not limited to age, personal life, employment. If engaged with through a visual medium, view of SCP-9723 may be partially obstructed, unfocused, or out of frame, with photos unable to be traced back to any individuals and locations shown. Depictions do not correlate with civilian birth records. Vocalizations also follow this pattern.

SCP-9723 will engage with subject over a period of time (range also inconsistent), typically moving conversation to secondary, private online platforms. Correspondences begin as menial before slowly opening into more personal topics partially tailored to subject’s own life, specifically points of stress. Subject’s bond towards SCP-9723 increases during this stage with user responding in turn and reflecting emotions back onto subject. Despite closeness, SCP-9723 may experience outbursts targeted towards and relying on subject’s presence. Outbursts may also consist of user sending subject various shock videos depicting mutilation of living beings, violent deaths, and “[screamer](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-9862)” jump scare clip compilations. Subject will typically keep interactions with SCP-9723 to themselves and avoid identifying user's presence towards other figures in personal life. Further into this stage, fauna populations within the subject’s immediate area will grow sparse.

Once subject reaches emotional capacity, and/or significant event(s) occur within subject’s life, if subject confides in SCP-9723, paranormality will offer comfort, consolation, and rest. Following this, SCP-9723 will typically question subject's trust of it. If subject denies, user will lash out in various ways, ranging from various threats (violence, blackmail, self-harm) to continuously sending previously referenced explicit and graphic content. Behavior persists until affirmation is made. If subject affirms, user will implore a potential future meetup and send various addresses to subject (always isolated, hidden, abandoned).

Subjects that approach named locations are deemed lost. Between 3-41 days following disappearance, subject will appear in online media through instances of shock and gore content, undergoing various forms of torture, mutilation, and death. Visual media is contradictory in regards to forms of violence and expiration that subject receives, as multiple photos and videos of the same subject depicts expiration in different methods. Electrocution, decapitation, gunshot to the head, exsanguination, etc.  Overall range of media contents display both fatal freak accidents and fatal violence inflicted upon subject. Corpses of mutilated animals may also be spotted within backgrounds of media, typically strewn around area or piled in back corners. Remains of both have never been recovered. 

After subsequent dissemination of footage online, or if SCP-9723's behaviors towards subject are discovered by a third party pre-meeting, all accounts linked to the SCP-9723 instance will self-terminate and all interactions it had with subject will be erased.
