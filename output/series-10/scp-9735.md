**Item #:** SCP-9735

**Object Class:** [Cogito-Hypatia-Shekhinah](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/series-0-glossary#toc7)

**Special Containment Procedures:**  Cables servicing OUTPOST-9’s water elevator are detached. Remote access to system cameras is to remain intact to confirm the aquatic nature of the surrounding landscape.

Site-GreenHeadquarters of the Paranormal Oceanic Division (POD). stores all bodycam footage in analogue form. It is not to be watched. Each viewing causes the outside world sequence to progressively brighten.

[[include <a href="/component:image-block">component:image-block</a> name=https://scp-sandbox-3.wdfiles.com/local--files/unobtainium235-2/outside%20view |caption=Sys.Cam[E] – View of Patefield shelf from OUTPOST-9 airlock. |width=50%]]

**Description:** The complex’s construction initially funded by NASA in 1994 for astronaut candidate training; OUTPOST-9 is a pelagic deepsea SCP research habitat. Located on the Patefield continental shelf above an extinct subduction zone—[trench](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-9862) extends down 1,500ft of visible oceanic environment. Depth of non-viewable environment unknown due to waveform and signal disturbances. OUTPOST-9 lies within the aphotic zone.(without sunlight)

OUTPOST-9 research team is no longer in contact with surface. Heat signature recorded as fluctuating between cold and warm. Communication module compromised(?). Two-weeks of radio silence; emergency team of six operatives descend via water elevator although journey is recorded taking two minutes longer than expected. Habitat discovered in an abandoned-state.

[[include <a href="/component:image-block">component:image-block</a> name=https://scp-sandbox-3.wdfiles.com/local--files/unobtainium235-2/Red_sprite.jpg |caption=Sys.Cam[F] – View of “red flashlight” by Patefield trench.|width=40%]]

Steel door of airlock fails to respond to SCP access code. First door hacked and opens to reveal stains along the waterpumps. Matter is pecked at by small fish. When the airlock is restored, the team finds large quantities of chawdron within the vents leaching an effluence into the metal. Unidentified corpse of a researcher eventually pried from between floor panels. Body shows signs of intense pressure-trauma. Abdomen evacuated. A black leathery skin is present across 90% of body, possible signs of sunburn. Described by team as a bruise-like-tissue (coverage includes eyes, teeth, nails, hair follicles, etc). One operative reports hearing the researcher’s voice. Another reports seeing a red flashlight beyond a porthole outside.

Team heads through the antechamber down the western hallway before stopping at the hygiene module. A damp saline smell emanates from within. Uniforms and diving suits wet with saltwater are discovered across the bathroom floor, seemingly disposed of. No signs of tears or injury stains were found present. The team explains that they begin to observe a construct made up of bound-together clothes rising into the air. Communication cannot be made however. Note that bodycam footage does not support this incident occurring. They eventually continue to the messhall and barracks; both are empty of bodies.

An apparent state of delirium is entered. Team identifies two additional members (twins “Frankie and Dylan Shopen”) as they are guided down a long passageway. Bodycam footage shows operatives discussing their route, although the conversation is directed at an empty room corner. The corner has a porthole showing fields of abyssal seagrass and a distant red esca hovering closer. Command questions who this discussion pertains to, although the team lead asserts the twins prior knowledge of the habitat. “Frankie” supposedly thanks them. Simultaneously, a few team members closest to the porthole report sunburn on clothed tissue.

Several hours pass. Luminous phenomena are found while heading towards the research module and isolation room.Used for onboarding of research material. Operatives all report a red light which spreads across surface geometry. The walls and flooring are colonised, although the red light does not spread to their skin. The source emanates from the window of the isolation room door. While the bodycam shows the isolation room door remaining closed, surface access to OUTPOST-9’s system (via the aforementioned hacking) indicates otherwise. Several team members are removing clothing items due to sunburn irritation.

Operatives become aware of a landscape beyond the portholes. Command asserts they should only be capable of viewing water through the hull. An operative directs their bodycam to the window as an unidentified human hand points from offscreen accompanied by a vocalisation stating “look”. The camera shows verdant grasslands. Operatives describe the grass as warm, explaining that the source of this heat are the numerous instances of humans buried beneath the soil. Footage shows a hillside swelling with bodies illuminated by red lights in the sky. The camera is directed towards a red sun floating on the horizon as it extends cnidarian-feelers into the hillside, vacuuming blackening objects up into the light. It brightens. The operative recording turns back towards their team, all clothless and noticeably lower. Each member—including Frankie and Dylan—begins to congratulate and shake each other's hands. The team lead says thanks to Frankie, who pats his back. The soil is up to their waists. Footage stops.

OUTPOST-9’s system shows that the exterior airlock door is open and the unidentified body is no longer present within.
