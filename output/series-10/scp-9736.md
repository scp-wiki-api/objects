**Item #:** SCP-9736

**Object Class:** Percept-MalleableObject is malleable to perception of it.

**Containment Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:**

SCP-9736 is to be contained at Storage Site-33, in Room 410 of the Safe-Class Wing. SCP-9736 containment chamber is to be 30m x 30m x 30m, and be made out of sealed concrete.

Testing of SCP-9736 may only be authorized by two (2) Level 3 personnel stationed on-site, who have detailed information about SCP-9736.

**Description:**

SCP-9736 is a shapeshifting concept that either manifests as an item, entity or presence. Despite the fact that it is essentially an immaterial concept, its manifestations can be moved to locations, and the concept will follow the manifestation."Conceptual Manifestations and how the Concept follows it's Manifestations", Roy James and David Bat, 14/08/1978.

SCP-9736 primary anomalous capability is when a significant portion of the population surrounding itTesting of this property was inconclusive, but around 65-75% of the population within a 15-30 meter radius of SCP-9736 is believed to be the trigger. thinks that SCP-9736 is an item, entity, or presence. When this happens, SCP-9736 will trigger a metamorphosis into that said item, entity, or presence.

SCP-9736 inert form is that of a rotating white orb of around 0.1 meters. The orb emits around 15 lumens,A lumen is a standard measurement of light. and does not interact physically, mentally, kinetically, thaumaturgically, metaphysically, conceptually, etc. Interacting with SCP-9736 will cause the item to phase through, with zero changes in the object and/or individual interacting with it.

**Addendum 9736-1:** Discovery

SCP-9736 was discovered on May 18, 2017, after reports of an inert white orb that has the ability to transform into any object via "thinking about it", circulated around Miami, Florida. The Department of Procurement and Liquidation soon acquired the warehouse where SCP-9736 was being stored from a storage company, and the internet was then scrubbed of any traces and persons who came to contact with witnesses and/or the object were given Class-B amnestic treatment.

**OBJECT:** 1 kg of steel.

**DATE:** 5/20/2017

**INDIVIDUAL:** Junior Researcher Billy

**OBJECT:** A mouse.

---

**PROCEDURE:** 2 D-Class personnel were advised to vaguely think about it being a mouse, with a third member of D-Class advised to vaguely think about it being a rat.

**RESULT:** Transformation occurred into a mouse. The mouse manifestation contained an disease usually transmitted from rats.

---

**NOTES:** It appears as though SCP-9736 can transform into an object, but actually retain some qualities of an other object.

---

**DATE:** 5/21/2017

**INDIVIDUAL:** Senior Researcher Julia

**OBJECT:** A human, D-Class.

---

**PROCEDURE:** 4 D-Class personnel is to be situated on the corners of the chamber, and think about a human person.

**RESULT:** Transformation occurred. Human manifestation was aggressive. Neutralized without incident.
