**Item #:** SCP-9746

**Object Class:** ~~Safe~~ Apollyon

**Special Containment Procedures:** Numerous attempts to contain SCP-9746 have failed. Drastic means of decomissioning are currently being discussed by the O5 Council to prevent a potential ZK-Class "Reality Failure" Scenario. See Addendum 9746-1. 

SCP-9746 currently grazes across the *Field (agriculture)* page of *wikipedia.org* and can at times be visible on-screen in the form of a low resolution .gif file identical to its original appearance. Word deletions caused by SCP-9746's consumption are explained as an "unusual form of wiki vandalism" to the civilian population.

**Description:** SCP-9746 is a formerly physical horse capable of retrocausally annihilating conceptual entities from existence.

SCP-9746, known by its owners as "Daisy Cakes," was initially recovered from ████ ████████ Ranch outside Provo, Utah before being transferred to Site-22 in San Buenaventura, California. At the time, SCP-9746 resembled an otherwise non-anomalous member of *Equus ferus caballus,* save for its singular ability to harmlessly consume and readily digest any material offered to it.

Site-22 researcher Dr. Louis Raphael is credited with the discovery of SCP-9746's ability to consume forms of nonphysical sustenance, including digital files, bureaucratic decisions, In the case of retroactively undoing his own divorce.  and abstract concepts which govern the physical world. Following the discovery of the latter form of sustenance for SCP-9746, which resulted in a core concept of reality being erased from time, The now unknown concept of "plurifoniousness." Dr. Raphael's field notes remain the only surviving record of such a concept.  all testing on SCP-9746 was halted. Dr. Raphael was then transferred to an unrelated Site-22 project and on-site liaisons for the O5 Council were installed to monitor all interactions with SCP-9746. Dr. Raphael's discoveries failed to suggest a clear correlation between SCP-9746's consumed non-physical sustenance and the severity of its retrocausal annihilation.

Shortly after the new containment restrictions were placed upon SCP-9746, Site-22 faced a sudden violent incursion from the Group of Interest known as the Chaos Insurgency. SCP-9746 was the sole target of the attack, which resulted in its subsequent forceful relocation from Site-22 into the possession of CI forces. It was immediately determined that the CI's interest in SCP-9746 stemmed from its retrocausal abilities, and later investigations revealed Dr. Raphael to have been an informant for the Insurgency. Dr. Raphael has since been terminated.

Through means not yet understood, Chaos Insurgency operatives managed to entirely digitize SCP-9746 into a form of living computer malware. One standing theory is that SCP-9746 was offered a negative identity concept as a form of sustenance, such as "SCP-9746 is not a computer virus."  Once this process was completed, SCP-9746 was implanted into Foundation databases and proceeded to cause massive internal damage. SCP-9746 then proceeded to breach its digital containment and escape into the broader World Wide Web.

In both its physical and digitized forms, SCP-9746 has displayed clear personal preference in regards to offered sustenance. Of note, SCP-9746 will refuse to consume any concept, identity, or axiom that would remove itself from existence either directly or indirectly. Additionally, SCP-9746 will not consume any digital media relating to soils, crustaceans, or IPAs. India Pale Ales
