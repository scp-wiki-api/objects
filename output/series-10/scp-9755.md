**Item #:** SCP-9755

**Object Class:** Safe

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9755 is to be kept in a cell measuring 25m² (269.10 square feet). Due to SCP-9755's nature, the entrance to said cell has 2 (two) doors as to prevent SCP-9755 from unnoticeably escaping containment.  The cell currently contains:

* 1 (one) light blue legless couch with a heated cushion.

* 1 (one) small round table next to the couch. All legs of said table are to be wrapped with sisal rope in order to prevent SCP-9755 from damaging the wood.

* 1 (one) different cat tree measuring 203cm (6'8") in height.

* 1 (one) cat scratching post measuring 74cm (29.13 inches) in height.

* 1 (one) white automatic cat feeder.

* 2 (two) food bowls for both water and wet food. Both bowls have the name "Sophie" written on them.

SCP-9755 is to be fed twice daily. Her automatic feeder must be stocked with dry food at all times. SCP-9755 should be fed wet food three times a week, all on separate days. On such days she should only be fed one wet meal. SCP-9755 should not be left alone for over two hours. 

**Description:** SCP-9755 is an adult female house cat (Felis catus). Her true physical form and appearance, due to her anomalous properties, is yet unknown. SCP-9755's given name is Sophie, this is the only name she responds to.

The entirety of the animal's body appears to be absent. The body is both completely invisible and intangible. Attempts at making physical contact with the animal have proven to be unsuccessful. In spite of this, people report sensing a warm presence at her percise location when attempting to touch her, suggesting she is physically present. While people are incapable of making contact with the animal, she is fully able to touch any physical surface. Sounds produced by the animal, whether vocalizations or those resulting from its movements, are heard without issue.

SCP-9755 does not like other cats or any other animal. Despite not being very social, she does not seem to mind human presence, even showing signs of distress when left completely alone for extended periods. The animal is very playful, often resulting in the destruction of papers, mugs, plants or furniture, hence her being kept in a containment cell.

**Note:** If Sophie lies on your lap, do not move. Let her sleep. You're lucky.
