**Item #:** SCP-9768

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9768 is contained in a soundproof 20x20m chamber at Site 512, with the chamber split in half so researchers can study SCP-9768 without listening to it. SCP-9768 has shown incredible resistance to termination, with SCP-9768 having survived termination ██ times. SCP-9768 is contained within the soundproof chamber, which is itself contained in a vacuum chamber to prevent any sound from leaking out. 

Current efforts to prevent SCP-9768 from hypnotising others include having all scientists and researchers have at least █ members of Security Department and having at least █ Security Lieutenants present to keep guard. All personnel entering SCP-9768's chamber must first wear industrialised noise-cancelling headphones, then wear additional earplugs and have white noise blasted into their ears to cancel out SCP-9768's voice. Personnel must then go through an airlock and equip oxygen tanks before entering the vacuum. After passing through the vacuum, personnel will then pass through yet another airlock before finally reaching SCP-9768. Personnel are allowed to be in the soundproof chamber on the side of SCP-9768 for a maximum of  █ minutes, and can be allowed to be in the side without SCP-9768 for a maximum of ██ minutes. There is a transparent soundproof barrier splitting the two sides, and the whole chamber is transparent, so researchers can see inside at any time.

At most █ CD can be allowed in the chamber on a given occasion, and CD entering the chamber will be terminated immediately afterwards by Security Guards. CD can only enter once per █ days.

**Description:** SCP-9768 was first discovered in 18██ in [ DATA EXPUNGED ], with reports surfacing of a creature hypnotising people through sound. Afterward, SCP-9768 disappeared for ███ years before appearing in [ DATA EXPUNGED ] in 18██ where more reports surfaced. Finally, in 19██, SCP-9768 appeared in [ DATA EXPUNGED ], where Mobile Task Force Eta-11 ("Savage Beasts") started the containment procedure. 

For the first wave, Mobile Task Force Eta-11 was ill-equipped for the mission. They brought along equipment that could record the sound so further research could be done before capturing it. Disaster struck when Team A first heard the sound and began to fall into a trance. They started to [ DATA EXPUNGED ]. Team B, C, and the rest all retreated.

For the second wave, Mobile Task Force Eta-11 sent Team B, who were equipped with soundproof headphones to block off the voice. Team B succeeded in capturing SCP-9768, and Team C and D created the temporary Site-511 to contain SCP-9768. After █ days, SCP-9768 was transferred to Site-512 to have proper research conducted on it. Site-511 was therefore decommissioned.

Once SCP-9768 was safely contained in Site-512, it was then sealed inside the soundproof chamber and vacuum chamber. Dr. Chan was then assigned as the lead scientist for SCP-9768. After several tests and trials, SCP-9768 still remains a mystery, but some parts of it are now known.

SCP-9768 looks like a regular human being, with its height being 180 cm and weight being 70kg. It has 1 distinctive difference: the mouth, which is around 3x the size of a regular human mouth.

**Addendum:** 

Dr. Chan's discovery:

After several tests involving ██ CD, and ██ species of animals, I have now come to this conclusion. SCP-9768 only affects humans, as all ██ species of animals were unaffected. Once human ears picked up on the noise, SCP-9768 would instantly stop moving and freeze in place. Its mouth would be wide open, but no movement could be detected. SCP-9768 will then stay in this state for 5 minutes, before going back to normal. During the 5 minutes SCP-9768 was in its frozen state, no other humans could be affected.

From now on, the human who heard SCP-9768's voice will be referred to as SCP-9768-2. Once SCP-9768-2 hears the voice, it will also freeze in place. Its eyes will roll backwards, and all limbs will become stiff. Both SCP-9768 and SCP-9768-2 will become fully unresponsive and invulnerable during these 5 minutes, before suddenly returning back to normal. Once 5 minutes pass, SCP-9768-2 will now be firmly under the control of SCP-9768. SCP-9768-2 will continue to behave normally, but SCP-9768 will be able to control the mind of SCP-9768-2. SCP-9768 will then use SCP-9768-2 to talk with the researchers. Here are some transcripts of some conversations.

Date: ██/████/████ Time: ██:██ Location: Site-512

Dr. Chan (D): "What did you do to [ DATA EXPUNGED ]?"

SCP-9768 (S) (Through SCP-9768-2): "I just gave him a new purpose in life."

D: "How do you do that"

S: "It's in my blood"

D: "Can you revert it?"

S: "No"

D: "Is there a time limit?"

S: "Not that I know of."

D: "How many people can you control?"

S: "I don't know, but I once controlled [ DATA EXPUNGED ] at once."

[D is shocked, and waits 30 seconds]

D: "Wow. Can you control who your voice targets?"

S: "No, I can't."

D: " I need some time to understand this."

S: "Sure, but please can I keep him [SCP-9768-2] longer this time?"

D: "Fine, but for 10 more minutes, and I will have a further conversation with you"

Date: ██/████/████ Time: ██:██ Location: Site-512

D: "Now, can you elaborate more on what you were talking about last time?"

S: "Sure. I can control people by sending out soundwaves that are ██hz, which interfere with their brains and let me have access to them.

D: "Has this method ever failed?"

S: "No, never."

D: "What do you do with them [SCP-9768-2]?"

S: "I usually just get them to lead more people to me, so I can get more of them. I also have them go through life regularly, but I can access their brains at any time if necessary."

D: "Do you remain in control of their child?"

S: "No."

SCP-9768 is capable of speech alone, but prefers not to unless it's necessary, like in Conversation #65.

SCP-9768 also shows a large emotional attachment to Dr. Chan, as SCP-9768 directly warned him that the containment breach was going to happen. During the breach, SCP-9768 was also seen looking around and avoiding doctors, as it was scared of infecting Dr. Chan. 

During the containment breach, SCP-9768 found a janitor who was hypnotised. During the 5 minutes, multiple personnel with proper equipment tried moving SCP-9768 back to its cell but were unable to do so as SCP-9768 was deeply anchored in the ground.

D: "You escaped!"

S: "I warned you!"

Another interesting thing Dr. Chan found is that whilst SCP-9768 is not inherently dangerous, it will become dangerous if it does not create any SCP-9768-2 in the past █ days, and will try to escape containment to find a target. 1 such containment breach was noted, and SCP-9768 was shown to be completely invulnerable and was able to break through containment with ease. Once SCP-9768-2 was created, SCP-9768 became peaceful again and slowly went back to his containment chamber.

SCP-9768 is also very cooperative as long as it has a steady supply of targets that can become SCP-9768-2. After ███ tries, none have failed in becoming SCP-9768-2. SCP-9768-2 also never become regular humans again, and they all have no recollection of what happened in the 5 minutes it changed, and has no memory of anything that happened when SCP-9768 was activily controling its mind.
