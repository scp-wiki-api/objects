= **Addendum 9781-4 | SCP-9781**

---

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9781 is to be kept in a standard humanoid containment cell, equipped with walls capable of blocking telepathic influence. SCP-9781 is not to be allowed within 50 meters of fires, except in controlled environments during testing approved by at least 2 personnel with clearance level 3 or above.

**Description:** SCP-9781 is a 35-year-old Caucasian male capable of telepathically morphing any fire within 50 meters, as long as the fire is still able to sustain itself in that shape.

He was discovered by the Foundation in the small town of [REDACTED], after the local police station started receiving reports of a "fire controlling wizard". After investigating the case, embedded agents within law enforcement contacted the SCP Foundation and spread a cover story centered around a deranged man with molotov cocktails.

**M:** Neutralized?

**E:** Yup. Read the incidents.

---

**Incident 9781-3:** During routine testing, D-4156 insulted SCP-9781. In response, he inflicted second-degree burns across D-4156's body.

After reviewing the relevant camera footage, D-4156 was disciplined for his actions. Further investigation revealed that the weapon used by D-4156 was accidentally left over from a previous test and the personnel on guard duty were attending the site's Christmas party at the time of the incident.

*[Silence on recording]*

**Miller:** Right. Wait here for a few minutes please. I need to make a phone call.

= **Addendum 9781-11 | SCP-9781**

---

**Special Containment Procedures:Acquiesce: Item is so big in scope or is so integrated into non-anomalous life and consensus reality, that research into viable containment methods has been deemed unnecessary. This does not necessarily mean the item is harmful or veil breaking.** SCP-9781 cannot be contained without significant damage to the veil and humanity.

Foundation webcrawlers are to look for media mentioning the anomalous nature of fire or SCP-9781's ritual and remove it from the internet immediately. The method of summoning SCP-9781's noospheric form is stored in a document only accessible to personnel with level 5 clearance. Since SCP-9781-1 instances are believed to be well documented scientific phenomena, no disinformation campaigns are necessary at this time.

In the event that the public becomes aware of SCP-9781-1 instances' anomalous nature, researchers are to follow the instructions detailed in addendum 9781-B.

**Description:** SCP-9781 is an entity residing in the noosphere, possessing weak antimemetic properties.

SCP-9781-1 is the phenomenon known as fire. Whenever there is sufficient heat, fuel and oxygen in one place, a part of the entity manifests as an instance of SCP-9781-1.Current theory indicates all fires are caused by SCP-9781.

The entity is able to shape its manifestations into any physically possible form and is capable of sensing the world around them. Testing has shown that this ability does not extend to all reduction-oxidation (redox) reactions, only to those producing visible flames. SCP-9781-1 instances strive to destroy all matter capable of storing information (paper, conscious beings, etc.) they come into contact with. Extinguishing fires has not been shown to affect the entity.

It is possible to summon SCP-9781's noospheric form through a complex ritual.

**Discovery:** On 8/6/2024, civilian explorers discovered cave drawings in a previously hidden part of Cave [REDACTED], depicting ancient humans partaking in a ritual and fighting against a powerful entity. When the Foundation became aware of this, the cave drawings were copied and the new part of the cave was sealed off. A cover story of a disastrous cave-in was broadcasted on major news networks. The civilians are currently being monitored. Given the nature of their findings, amnesticization is permitted as a last resort should they attempt to share any part of the drawings or perform the ritual.

= **Addendum 9781-A: VIDEO LOG**

---

**DATE:** 8/13/2024

**Foreword:** A few days after its discovery, SCP-9781 attempted to communicate with the Overseer Council through an instance of SCP-9781-1.

---

**<Begin Log>**

**O5-8:** Again, as all of us know-

**O5-2:** {{STOP. DETECTING ANOMALOUS MOVEMENT FROM THE DIRECTION OF THE FIREPLACE.}}

**O5-7:** Letters, possibly? If they are, they're a bit deformed. I can't quite make them out.

**SCP-9781:** Apologies, it has been a long time since I have tried this.

**O5-9:** Who are you? Where are you? How are you controlling the fire?

**SCP-9781:** I *am* the fire.

**O5-9:** What does it mean by that?

*[The Overseers' implanted chips search through the database, looking for a match, but come up with nothing.]*

**O5-2:** {{UNKNOWN.}}

**SCP-9781:** I exist in what I believe you call the noosphere. What you know as fire is merely a small part of me manifesting in your world.

**O5-8:** And why are you talking to us?

**SCP-9781:** You're in posession of information about me that I have spent thousands of years trying to destroy.

**O5-6:** Does anyone know what the hell it's talking about?

**O5-1:** I believe I do. A report came in earlier today. The details aren't important right now, but the point is our researchers found cave paintings related to some kind of big fiery entity.

**SCP-9781:** That would be correct. A long time ago ancient humans managed to summon my true form using a ritual. I tried to communicate with them but they were scared and started attacking me. I managed to escape and I have been trying to destroy any information about that ritual ever since.

**O5-4:** And you want us to destroy the instructions?

**SCP-9781:** It is for everyone's good.

**O5-1:** I'm afraid that's not how we handle things at the Foundation.

*[O5-1 extinguishes the fire.]*

**<End Log>**

= **Addendum 9781-13 | O5 Deal**

---

Foreword: The Overseer Counci-

---

**<Begin Log>**

**O5-1:** Do we really need that foreword, 2? We all know why this meeting was called.

**O5-2:** {{APOLOGIES.}}

**O5-1:** 5, light a fire for us, please.

*[O5-5 lights the logs in the fireplace.]*

**O5-7:** Are we connected, SCP-9781?

**SCP-9781:** Why have you contacted me?

**O5-1:** We have come to make a deal with you. If we understand correctly, you have control over all the fires in the world?

**SCP-9781:** As I have told you before, fires are merely my manifestations.

**O5-8:** We want complete control over fire. If we say something, you make it happen.

**SCP-9781:** And what could possibly make you think I'd accept the deal? You cannot offer me anything that would make me do so.

**O5-1:** You misunderstand. If you don't agree to our deal, we are prepared to leak the instructions you have been working so hard to destroy to the public.

**SCP-9781:** You *do* understand that I am in the noosphere, right? I can quite literally see your thoughts. I know you do not intend to do that.

**O5-1:** Oh, so you can read our minds? Interesting. I'm thinking of something else now. Please, do try reading my mind again. We have killed gods before. We won't kill you, of course. You're far too important. But we can do so many worse things to you than that.

*[The fire freezes for a few seconds, then resumes forming letters, much quicker than previously.]*

**SCP-9781:** Please, I did not know! Have mercy!

**O5-1:** From this point on, the fires of the world will obey our every command. Understood?

**SCP-9781:** Yes! Yes, of course!

**O5-1:** Good.

*[O5-4 extinguishes the fire.]*

**O5-1:** Now we just have to make sure no one can investigate us.

**<End Log>**

**Evans:** There's still one thing I don't understand. Why were the O5s so careless? In every case so far, they managed to cover their tracks well enough that we didn't have any proof. And that's not even accounting for the who knows how many cases we don't know of!
