**Item #:** SCP-9358

**Object Class:** ~~Keter~~ Euclid

**Clearence Level:** 3: Secret

**Disruption Class:** 2: Vlam

**Risk Class:** 4: Danger

**Containment Procedures:** SCP-9782-1 is to be contained behind two airlock doors, both electrically charged with 575 or more volts charged in each one, with rubber surrounded concrete walls. Two Tesla Coils must descend from the ceiling, also charged with 575 volts, and must immediately be charged during a breach of any SCP. An emergency generator is to be placed with the capability to produce 2300 volts every hour for up to 3 hours. A viewing room must be raised more than 23 meters above the containment, and all testing Class-D must wear body cameras during testing. SCP-9782-2 is a titanium 10x10x10 cm cube, which SCP-9782-1 guards for no apparent reason. SCP-9782-2 is to be kept in a room with rubber coated walls, and should be locked by a small door, that Class-D can't access, but has to be open for SCP-9782-1 to return to its cube. No Class-D may **EVER** get close to SCP-9782-2. SCP-9782-1 is to be kept in a 25mx25mx25 titanium walled box with rubber padding and SCP-97782-2 is to be kept in a bullet proof glass box 15cmx15cmx15cm within a 3mx3mx3m sized titanium walled room. The room must have a 50% silicon alloy door. All walls must be at least 52cm thick.

**Origin:** SCP-9782-1 or SCP-9782-2 were both found at Lake Rogers State Park in Odessa, Florida. Multiple impaled bodies were found (About 34), and SCP-9782-1 started getting defensive of SCP-9782-2 when MTF units approached (Hence why MTF units discovered SCP-9782-2).

**Description:** SCP-9782-1 is a 2-meter-tall humanoid anomaly, with black fur all other its body, making it nearly invisible at night, and 12 gray 3cm sharp spikes on its back, which is about one meter long. SCP-9782-1 has the appearance of a wolf, and has been known among foundation personnel as "The Spiker", or more commonly "The Spike Wolf". It has a rotund head with small, glowing, green eyes and a furless face. It has a small, stout nose, and can cover its eyes with completely black eyelids to blend in with the dark. It has large feet and strong muscles, and has the ability run up to 35 miles an hour.

**Abilities:** SCP-9782-1 is instinctually violent towards humans, yet no other biological lifeforms. Its skin is thick enough to take low caliber bullets, knifes, or blunt weapons, however it can be harmed enough with 5.7x28mm or a higher caliber. After being harmed enough it can run back to a small titanium cube, SCP-9782-2, its "home". SCP-9782-2 has no apparent abilities, other than being the home to SCP-9782-1. SCP-9782-1 has an instinctual fear of all electricity with 575 or more volts. The anomaly recoils upon touching electricity, and with enough charge could theoretically be killed, however the estimated volt count to terminate SCP-9782-1 is 200 billion volts. SCP-9782-1 uses its back spikes to pin victims to a wall and sometimes enjoys killing its victims, possibly meaning it has no need for hunger. SCP-9782-1 can run at 35 miles an hour, and has been seen using its arms to slam victims to the ground, then using its spikes to kill them. SCP-9782-1 has heat seeking vision, the ability to destroy most materials. Silicon above 45% purity is especially weak to SCP-9782, although titanium is extremely hard for SCP-9782-1 to damage.

**Recontainment Procedures:** MTF units attempting to recontain SCP-9782-1 must shoot it until it shows visible weakness (Limping, screams, etc.) and then must coordinate with electric producing weapons (Tasers, stun sticks or handheld tasers not recommended.) to send it back to its cube, of which it will stay there for the next week.
