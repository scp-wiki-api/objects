**Item #:** SCP-9787

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** The SCP-9787 colony has been allowed to keep Junior Researcher Leigh's credit card, and $15,000 USD is to be wired into the account monthly for gas and maintenance fees. The colony's vehicle has been equipped with an explosive device and a tracker. SCP-9787 instances have been permitted three days of driving every week, and their vehicle is to be stored in a Foundation-owned garage when not mobile. Any assaults will lead to a strict month-long ban on driving.

No SCP-9787 instance is, under any circumstance, permitted access to orange Fanta.

**Description:** SCP-9787 is the collective term referring to 201,378 anomalous instances of *Polistes aurifer*Also known as the Golden Paper Wasp, native to the Western United States. nested inside of a gray 2014 Honda Civic Si. An instance of SCP-9787 possesses unusual intelligence and durability, extreme pettiness, and the ability to telekinetically exert 0.03N of force on an object within sight. SCP-9787 instances also project a slight memetic effect causing individuals to ignore them. This effect diminishes in strength with time and with closer inspection.This effect is noticeably stronger on gas station operators, mechanics. SCP-9787 instances have demonstrated knowledge of traffic laws inside of the United States and possesses valid licenses for Utah, New Mexico, Texas, and Nevada.

An SCP-9787 colony will remain neutral to humans unless insulted, disrespected or inconvenienced. If aggravated, all instances will attempt to assault the offender.SCP-9787 instances have been recorded pursuing offenders for up to three days. Instances will always remain passive at DMV offices, gas stations, and mechanic shops.Currently unknown as to how SCP-9787 procured money for these services prior to Incident 9787.1

**Addendum 9787.1: Discovery**

The first recorded SCP-9787 sighting was reported by a police officer who pulled over the SCP-9787 colony's vehicle. The officer was amnesticized following his testimony.

**Bodycam Footage**

**Date:** 26/07/25 - 23:08 - 23:14

---

[Begin Log]

*The officer approaches the vehicle, knocking on the window. The SCP-9787 instances lower the driverside window. The interior of the car is covered in SCP-9787 instances.*

*Buzzing.*

**Officer:** Good evening to you too. Do you know how fast you were going?

*Buzzing. The SCP-9787 instances inside the begin flying in circles around the driver's seat.*

**Officer:** Regardless, you were exceeding the speed limit.

*Anxious buzzing. The officer adjusts his bodycam.*

**Officer:** License and registration, please.

*Dejected buzzing. Several SCP-9787 instances slowly open the glovebox and hand the officer a driver's license and several papers including their registration and insurance.*

*The officer hands the papers back.*

**Officer:** Right. You weren't going too fast and there aren't many people on the road this late, so I'll issue a warning. Please watch your speed on the road in the future.

*Apologetic buzzing. The window begins to roll up. The officer writes in a notepad before taking two steps back.*

**Officer:** Wait a *fucking* second.

*The window stops rising. The officer rubs his eyes.*

**Officer:** Sir, could you step out of the vehicle for me, please? *Muttered:* It's not *that* late. What the hell?

*The door opens. The officer takes three steps back.*

**Officer:** Jesus *Christ!*

*Inquisitive buzzing.*

**Officer:** No, you're not in trouble. Just... Uh, good night. Drive... safe?

*Affirmative buzzing. The door slams and Honda Civic drives away.*

**Officer:** What the *fuck?*

*The bodycam turns off.*

[End Log]

This incident was forwarded to the Federal Bureau of Investigation's Unusual Incidents Unit, who in turn reported it to the Foundation. 

**Addendum 9787.2: Incident**

Following the UIU's report, FTAFoundation Temporal Array Station #15 near Mesquite, Nevada was attacked by the SCP-9787 colony. The colony was aggravated by Junior Researcher Martin Leigh, who cut their vehicle off at a stoplight while attempting to commute to the station. The aggravated SCP-9787 instances proceeded to tail JR Leigh to the station and assault him and Junior Researcher Estelle Mayers.

[Begin Log]

*JR Mayers opens the door and holds it open for JR Leigh before slamming it shut and locking it. Both are breathing heavily.*

**Mayers:** What, the, *fuck?*

*Leigh jabs a finger at Mayers.*

**Leigh:** You *closed* the door on me back there, you fucking asshole!

*Mayers shrugs.*

**Mayers:** Every man for himself. I just needed to outrun you.

**Leigh:** Fuck you! I thought I could trust you-

**Mayers:** You've known me all of *three weeks*! Besides, you stole my yogurt.

**Leigh:** So you leave me out to die for bees?

**Mayers:** They probably smelled the yogurt on you, honestly.

*Silence for three minutes.. Mayers checks her phone.*

**Mayers:** Twenty minutes till an MTF gets here.

**Leigh:** Shut up.

*Loud metal banging is audible.*

**Leigh:** The hell is that noise?

**Mayers:** You're asking me?

*The banging stops and the ventilation system's grille hits the ground.*

*A massive swarm of SCP-9787 instances flood the room. High pitched screaming is audible, but transcriber was unable to ascertain if the noise was produced by JR Leigh or JR Mayers. Dull thumps can be heard.*

[End Log]

The SCP-9787 instances fled the area before an MTF team could arrive, with FTA Station #15 suffering approximately $156,000 in damages. JR Mayers suffered 23 stings across various body parts, while JR Leigh accumulated over 500 stings, severe bruising, and cranial trauma, alongside having his phone and wallet stolen during the SCP-9787 assault.

The SCP-9787 colony was later isolated at a gas station four hours later, where they surrendered peacefully. The colony returned JR Leigh's phone, but refused to surrender his wallet. 

**Addendum 9787.3: Interview**

Following containment, the SCP-9787 colony was subjected to multiple intelligence and aptitude tests overseen by Junior Researcher Estelle Mayers. Following the conclusion of the tests, the SCP-9787 colony requested to speak with Junior Researcher Mayers personally.

**Interview Transcript**

---

**Time:** 30/07/25, ██:██:██ - ██:██:██

**Interviewer(s):** Junior Researcher Estelle Mayers

**Interviewee:** SCP-9787 instances #1 through #201,378

**Notes:** SCP-9787 instances were given a laptop with a text-to-speech program for the interview. All SCP-9787 instances are referred to as SCP-9787. JR Mayers communicated with SCP-9787 through the Observation Room PA.

---

[Begin Log]

*Multiple SCP-9787 instances can be heard bumping into the glass divider. Clicking is heard as SCP-9787 types.*

**SCP-9787:** {{Why are you standing behind a wall?}}

**Mayers:** There's enough of you to crush me flat if you wanted, that's why. Why do you want to talk to me?

*Muted buzzing and clicking.*

**SCP-9787:** {{Let us out.}}

**Mayers:** No. I don't have the authority to, and I wouldn't want to.

**SCP-9787:** {{Let us out?}}

**Mayers:** No.

**SCP-9787:** {{Let us out, *please?*}}

**Mayers:** No. 

**SCP-9787:** {{We're sorry for stinging you.}}

**Mayers:** Apology accepted.

**SCP-9787:** {{Let us out.}}

**Mayers:** No.

**SCP-9787:** {{Why not?}}

**Mayers:** I just told you why. Besides, you're going to sting someone else if you do.

**SCP-9787:** {{It won't be you.}}

**Mayers:** Still. No.

*Gloomy buzzing. SCP-9787 takes three minutes to type out the next response.*

**SCP-9787:** {{Your mother's a whore.}}

**Mayers:** Fuck you. Like yours isn't, either.

*Embarassed buzzing. Clicking.*

**SCP-9787:** {{Let us out?}}

**Mayers:** No.

**SCP-9787:** {{Please?}}

**Mayers:** *No.*

**SCP-9787:** {{Is that Orange Fanta?}}

**Mayers:** Yes.

**SCP-9787:** {{Can we have it?}}

**Mayers:** Ask someone else when they come around.

**SCP-9787:** {{Is it just you here?}}

**Mayers:** Yes.

**SCP-9787:** {{Do you like our car?}}

*Mayers leans back to take a look at SCP-9787's car. The SCP-9787 instances partition themselves to allow her a view. Mayers peers at the vehicle for twelve seconds before sitting down.*

**Mayers:** Yeah, actually.

**SCP-9787:** {{Have you had dinner?}}

**Mayers:** Yes.

**SCP-9787:** {{Was it enchiladas?}}

**Mayers:** How the fu- yes.

**SCP-9787:** {{Will you let us out?}}

**Mayers:** Ye- No.

**SCP-9787:** {{Damnit.}}

[End Log]

**Addendum 9787.4: Concerning Rights**

On 09/08/25, the SCP-9787 colony petitioned for a meeting with the Ethics Committee on their rights and containment. Following a three-day discourse, the Ethics Committee allowed the SCP-9787 colony to draft up an agreement containing its current permissions and punishments.
