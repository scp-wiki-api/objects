**Item #:** SCP-9009

**Object Class:** Thaumiel

**Special Containment Procedures:**

Scp 9789 is to be kept in her room at all times. If she desires to leave said room, measures must be taken to ensure that does not happen. Contact with any human is strictly forbidden, and subject does not need food or water to sustain herself. If subject appears not to be in room, check that all windows and doors are sealed, and ensure that SCP 1182 is in containment. If SCP 1182 is not in containment, and one or more of the vents or windows are open, the site is to be put on a full AX-Class lockdown, and mobile task force Alpha-One is to be dispatched to the area with ultraviolet goggles. If SCP 9789 comes into contact with SCP 239, then all agents are to immediately comply with anything SCP 9789 requests, no matter the impact to the future of the foundation, or even the world itself.

**Description**: SCP 9789 is a former agent that was stationed at site-███. They are a 21 year old Caucasian female human with dark hair and blue eyes, standing at a height of 5'8". At first glance, she seems like a normal foundation agent, but was taken into custody following incident 9930-A. Further information is provided below.

 On October █ 20██, a disturbance was reported at site ███. Upon arrival, mobile task force- alpha found over 17 Keter class SCP's breached, and all personnel inside the blast shelter. Strangely, none of the SCP's seemed hostile despite previous testing. Inside the main control room sat SCP 9789. She looked mildly embarrassed and apologetic. Video transcript is provided below
