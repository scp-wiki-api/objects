**Item #:** SCP-9796

**Object Class:** Safe 

**Special Containment Procedures:** All historical mentions related to Vatican occultism discovered within the territory of Florence, which are interconnected with the occult chronicles, must be acquired and subsequently delivered to warehouse 14-3.

All historical records and related information addressed in academic circles must be analyzed by the foundation's historians and adjusted accordingly.

All historical artifacts associated with the dynasty must be examined for anomalous properties and placed under guard.

**Description:** SCP-9796 These are all interconnected occult chronicles describing the actions of the dynasty of the Cult of the Crimson King, whose motto is better known as "From mortal dust to the sovereign's diadem." All of the occult chronicles describe occult activities that describe ritual activities whose repercussions extend across the entirety of Christendom, invariably to its detriment

Also described are papal bulls written directly by cultists who obtained the pontificate of Rome. The very first occult chronicles describing the actions of the dynasty  Hereinafter - SCP-9796-1 were received from the Orthodox chapter of the Reformed Order of the Temple in the form of papal documents as well as bulls.

Apparently, 9796-1 ended with the reign of Pontiff Pius IX (1846–1878), which concluded with the dogma of papal infallibility. The further fate of SCP-9796-1 after 1878 remains undocumented. Certain chroniclers of the modern era (including anonymous sources within the curia) associate the resurgence of the dynasty with the pontificate of Francis, however, all such mentions are oral in nature and lack material corroboration. Repeated attempts at memetic interrogation of incumbent cardinals have revealed no awareness of 9796-1; the Pontiff himself is unavailable for standard scanning procedures due to extraordinary measures taken by the Vatican Guard

**Documentation:**  All primary information, as stated above, was provided directly by the Reformed Order of the Temple. However, subsequent chronicles were discovered directly by the Foundation's historians, require proofreading, and have not been corroborated by third-party sources.

In the new chronicles of Edward Radoshsky An Occult chronicler of the 16th century, known for extensive descriptions of occultism in the Vatican, members of the Church of the Broken God appear, seeking to seize the Vatican or otherwise alter the pontificate of Rome by their own means. The last mention of members of the Church of the Broken God appears in the chronicles " Broken Church " dated 1527.

<summary>Original chronicle of Niketas Choniates/Found during excavations in Greece, 1215 CE.</summary>

> **Date**: ca. 1215 CE.

> 

> **Source:** Daphni Monastery, Attica, Greece.

> 

> **Circumstances of discovery:** In 1987, during restoration work on the altar section of the monastery, workers discovered a stone sarcophagus walled into the foundation of the floor. Inside was a leather bag containing a parchment manuscript. The find was initially transferred to the National Historical Museum of Athens. However, upon comparison with known versions of Niketas Choniates' "History," it was determined that this copy contained additional pages absent from all previously known copies. The museum notified the Ministry of Culture, after which the information was intercepted by a Foundation agent.

> 

> **Description of the document:** The manuscript is a Greek chronicle covering the period from 1118 to 1207 CE. The majority of the text matches the known edition. However, on pages 258–259, there is a fragment that Choniates apparently deliberately included in the final version but which was removed during subsequent transcriptions.

>

> In the letter of Pope Innocent III to the Byzantine Empire, dated 1203, a phrase is contained that is absent from the official Vatican copies:

> 

> *"We call upon the most gracious lord who paid with His flesh for our creation. Let the people cry out to Him, and let the infidels burn in hellfire, in the name of the Creator, strong and fearless. In the name of the pious and orthodox lineage, by the dynasty I swear!"*

>

> Choniates accompanies this quotation with his own commentary, in which he particularly emphasizes that for the Pontiff of Rome, God is sealed and does not possess the powers that can be obtained in the name of the Roman crown. The chronicler directly points to a connection between the pope's words and the beginning of the Fourth Crusade, which led to the sack of Constantinople in 1204 and the establishment of the Latin Empire a state which Choniates calls a "puppet of the Crown."

> 

> Also mentioned is the Lord's Anvil, to which the crusaders hired for the war, in 1203, sent the bodies of the slain, as well as refuse, bloodied armor, and other waste products, including feces, broken relics, and the Christian robes of the Byzantines.

</details>

<summary>Papal bull </summary>

> **Date**: ca. 1314 CE.

> 

> **Source**: Piazza San Pietro, 00120 Vatican City

> 

> **Circumstances of discovery:** In 2001, a meeting took place in the Vatican between members of the Reformed Order of the Temple and the SCP Foundation, during which the Grand Chancellor of the Order handed over a folder of documents containing information about a papal bull known in the occult world as "Sacrificium Infidelium Domino." This bull has not been directly published by the Vatican, and the cardinals are unaware of its existence. Nevertheless, analysis of the documents proved that it was written by Clement V, the then Pope. The bull contains unknown symbols, as well as occult symbolism dating back to 700 AD, characteristic of the cults of the Children of the Scarlet King at that time.

> 

> **Description of the document:** The papal bull is a scroll containing a description of actions against the Reformed Order of the Temple, where, sometime after the Inquisition and the Council of Vienne, Jacques de Molay refused to swear allegiance to an unspecified lord. Most of the text has been erased, but the general meaning is clear.

> 

> The messages to Guillaume de Chevallier (the chief inquisitor of France at the time of 1307–1314) contain phrases encrypted in an unknown code. The only word that can be partially deciphered is "PBUARGRORS," derived from the Greek word for "Scarlet" and the Old Russian word for "Crimson."

> 

> Further supplementation is borrowed from the occult chronicles of the well-known Godfroy of Paris, a chronicler who conveyed the curse of the last Grand Master of the Templars as directly as possible. The chronicles describe four hours of torture endured by the Grand Master, during which, minutes before his death, Jacques de Molay uttered a phrase heard by half of Paris:

> 

> *"Usurper Clement... his accomplice, Guillaume de Nogaret, and King Philip... not a year shall pass before I summon you to the judgment of God, and you shall receive just punishment for your betrayal! A curse! A curse upon your dynasty of the Crimson Lord, unto the thirteenth generation!"*

</details>

<summary>New Epoch, chronicles of Edward Radoshsky / Discovered in Turkey. 1533 CE.</summary>

> **Date**: ca. 1527 CE.

>

> **Source:** Ayasofya Meydanı, Sultanahmet, Fatih, İstanbul, Turkey.

>

> **Circumstances of discovery:** During the First World War, when the Russian Empire defeated the Turkish Army, the Entente for a time controlled, among other territories, Istanbul—formerly known as Constantinople. The Tsarist Seers, being the occult service of the Russian Empire, spent a couple of weeks investigating Christian holy sites, during which they discovered an ancient book with an iron binding, dated to 1533. It was transported to Saint Petersburg and subsequently came under the management of the Hermitage Museum. During the Soviet era, the document was not destroyed but preserved. The modern government of the Russian Federation transferred the book to the SCP Foundation.

>

> **Description of the document:** "New Epoch" is a chronicle recounting the sack of Rome in 1527. It is narrated in the third person and addresses the war between the Roman Pontiff and the Holy Roman Empire, which was displeasing to him. Despite well-known modern chronicles related to the sack of Rome, this particular chronicle claims that the majority of Charles V's mercenaries came from the Ottoman Empire. Despite the existence of muskets, the entire mercenary army was made entirely of iron and blessed by the Creator. The narrative then describes a arduous campaign of twenty thousand faithful marching on the Vatican with the aim of vengeance and retribution for the desecration of the most holy anvil.

> 

> Upon arriving in Rome, the iron sacred army engaged the condottieri of Rome, who put up fierce resistance, halting the entire army. After minor skirmishes, the condottieri were defeated by the superior iron army. It is this chronicle that describes flying soldiers with iron wings who rushed to defend Rome. Afterward, this entire massive army advanced on the Vatican, which was defended by 189 elite thaumaturges of the Roman Pontiff. The protracted battle lasted approximately five hours, allowing the Pontiff of Rome to flee the Vatican through a rift. Despite this, he was captured and killed by the iron army. Many residents of Rome suffered in the process, and dozens of relics were removed from the Vatican and demonstratively destroyed. The then-extant St. Peter's Basilica and its cathedral were also destroyed.

> 

> The chronicle describes the tragedy in a remarkably vivid and sweeping manner. According to the author, all of Rome was drenched in the blood and flesh of the disfavored.

</details>

<summary>Document on the Immaculate Nature of the Pontiff / Provided by Cardinal ████████, an active member of the Department of Tactical Theology.</summary>

> **Date**: ca. 1870 CE.

> **Source**: Reliquary Area-27, SCP Foundation

> **Circumstances of discovery**: In 2011, Cardinal ████████ provided the Foundation with a document concerning the dogmas explaining why the Pontiff is immaculate. The original bull on papal infallibility differs significantly from the documents kept in the extensive Vatican Library, which is strictly guarded by the Roman Curia and the Swiss Guard.

> **Description of the document:**At a council, the Pope presented a list of church dogmas explaining his sinlessness. In them, he mentioned that the powerless God, hidden as if behind seals, had endowed him with the right to be a new witness of His divine works. He declared that the Pope of Rome is infallible and serves as the intermediary between God and sinful man, and therefore commanded that he be immediately invested with universal authority over the Church. About eleven bishops and three archbishops voiced criticism toward the Pope, in which they set forth reasons concerning the sinful nature of man after the Fall of Adam and Eve. Despite this, the Pope ordered the execution of every single bishop and archbishop. Additionally, three cardinals who defended this position at the Vatican Council were sentenced to public flogging and the cutting out of their tongues.

> 

> Following these demonstrative acts, which were godless for the face of the Church, the Pope was condemned by the Italian signori and by the residents of the districts themselves. This led to a massacre initiated by a thaumaturgical ritual, which was supported by certain members of the Roman Curia. Presumably, this was the Pope's cousin, who had been appointed to the position of a previously missing cardinal.

> 

> Due to the obvious threat emanating from the Pope, the cardinals abandoned their criticism of his dogma of infallibility. This led to the canonical conclusion of the Vatican Council and the establishment of a "full dictatorship" within the Vatican.

> 

> Also, one of the chronicles, "The Death of Kings and Monarchs," An occult chronicle containing compiled information on the deaths of major religious figures. It is currently held in Reliquary 27 for containment. Authorship is unknown, and it is dated as far as the year 3728 CE. After that point, the book abruptly ends. primarily describes the death of Pope Pius IX. It describes his agonizing death in the bed of the Apostolic Palace, where one of the apostles appeared to him and cursed him for his sinful deeds. Despite its remarkable resemblance to events, Pius IX was strangled in his sleep at the palace in that same year, and no signs of forced entry were found. About eleven members of the Swiss Guard were executed for negligence; however, further hints of a unified dynasty of the cult disappeared.

</details>
