**Item #:** SCP-9824

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9824 is to be contained within a specialized 3m x 3m x 3m concrete containment chamber at Site-19. This chamber houses a single salvaged architectural doorway confirmed to be actively affected by SCP-9824. Physical transit or entry through SCP-9824 is strictly prohibited for all personnel under any circumstances. Testing requires approval from the Lead Researcher (Level 3 or higher).

**Description:** SCP-9824 is an anomalous architectural phenomenon that manifests within standard doorways of residential and commercial structures. When active, SCP-9824 generates a continuous localized radiation reading of approximately 100 mSv. Instances of SCP-9824 are primarily detected via localized Geiger counter scanning or following civilian reports of sudden, acute radiation sickness. 

**Addendum 9824.1:** Anomalous Effects on Biological Organisms

Physical transit through an active SCP-9824 threshold results in instantaneous, total retrograde amnesia. This effect occurs regardless of the subject's biological classification, neurological structure, or cognitive complexity. Affected subjects completely lose all memories, semantic knowledge, and personal identity. 

Reversal of SCP-9824's amnesic effects cannot be achieved through standard medical procedures or amnestic counter-agents. The only viable method of cognitive recovery requires a comprehensive regimen of re-exposure therapy using the subject's complete, verified historical data and biographical records. 

Addendum 9824.2: Incident Log 9824-Alpha

On August 14, 2026, Foundation local suppression systems flagged an anomalous medical entry at St. Jude Hospital in Portland, Oregon. A 34-year-old male, later identified as Marcus Vance, had been admitted displaying severe, acute radiation sickness (estimated 4.2 Gy exposure) alongside a total absence of long-term memory, basic semantic vocabulary, and personal identity.

Foundation agents intercepted the civilian and transferred him to Sector-4 of Site-19 under the designation Subject 9824-01. A tactical sweep of Vance’s suburban residence successfully located an active instance of SCP-9824 manifesting in his second-floor master bathroom doorway.

The following audio transcript logs an interview conducted three weeks after Subject 9824-01's containment, following stabilized radiation therapy.

Interviewed: Subject 9824-01 (Marcus Vance)

Interviewer: Dr. Alan Vance (Note: Dr. Vance is the birth brother of the subject. He was assigned specifically to catalyze potential breakthrough memory triggers via re-exposure therapy).

[BEGIN LOG]

Dr. Vance: Marcus? Can you hear me? Look at my face. Do you know who I am?

Subject 9824-01: (Stares blankly for nine seconds, then winces, rubbing his temples) No. I... I know the word "man." I know "white coat." But I don't know you. My throat feels dry. Like swallowed glass.

Dr. Vance: That is a symptom of the radiation, Marcus. We are treating it. I need you to focus. We found something in your house. A door. Upstairs.

Subject 9824-01: (Agitated, breathing accelerates) The... the frame. The wooden frame.

Dr. Vance: Yes! What do you remember about the frame?

Subject 9824-01: I don't... I don't have a name for the room it led to. But it was wrong. I remember standing on the carpet. My skin was tingling. It felt like standing too close to a microwave. Hot, but under the skin.

Dr. Vance: Why did you step through it, Marcus? Please, think.

Subject 9824-01: (Shakes head violently) I didn't mean to! I was looking for something. I walked into the hallway to grab... a towel? A glass of water? I don't know. I forgot what I needed. So I turned around to go back into the room to remember. That’s what people do, right? You go back into the room to remember why you left it.Dr. Vance: Marcus, stay with me. What happened when you crossed the threshold?

Subject 9824-01: I didn't hit the floor on the other side. I just... blinked. And when my eyes opened, I was standing in the middle of a blank white hallway. And everything was gone.

Dr. Vance: What do you mean by everything?

Subject 9824-01: The words. The pictures in my head. I looked down at my hands and I didn’t know what they were called. I didn't know what I was. The door behind me was just an ordinary bathroom door again. It stopped tingling. I spent three hours staring at a bottle of mouthwash trying to figure out if I was supposed to drink it, cry into it, or run away from it. (Starts crying softly) Why did it take my childhood, Doc? Why don't I know my mother's name?

Dr. Vance: (Voice cracking slightly) Her name is Eleanor, Marcus. And my name is Alan. I'm your brother.

Subject 9824-01: (Looks up, eyes completely detached) I'm sorry. Those are just sounds to me. You're just a man in a coat telling me a ghost story about a stranger.

[END LOG]

Closing Statement: Subject 9824-01 remains under quarantine at Site-19. Despite continuous, targeted re-exposure therapy utilizing childhood photographs, family journals, and direct contact with immediate relatives, the subject’s cognitive baseline has shown 0% structural recovery.

Dr. Alan Vance has formally requested a transfer off the SCP-9824 research team, citing psychological distress.
