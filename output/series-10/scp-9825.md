**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9825 events can only be detected after they have already occurred; as such, containment procedures are focused on preserving the Veil and minimising civilian casualties.

Each Site is equipped with a modified  [Kant counter tuned to detect the specific Hume spectrum](and-this-one-explains-humes)^^ⓘ^^HumesA Hume is a way to measure the strength/amount of reality in an area. Often affected by ontological phenomena. A Kant counter is a device used to measure Humes. Click this link for more information. of SCP-9825-2's anomalous effect. Readings under 100 Humes are ignored.~95% of SCP-9825 events fall under this threshold. If a reading appears above this threshold, Foundation staff will notify local emergency services. If this is unavailable or infeasible, a Foundation medevac team will be dispatched.

**Update 20/08/2026:** SCP-9825 has been reclassified from Keter to Cernunnos.Cernunnos-class anomalies can functionally be contained; however the Foundation is currently either unwilling or unable to do so due to logistical or ethical concerns. Containment pertaining to SCP-9825 events will continue as normal. For more details, refer to Addendum 9825.4.

**Description:** SCP-9825 is the collective designation for SCP-9825-1 and SCP-9825-2, which manifest together in random events. These events are henceforth referred to as SCP-9825 events.

SCP-9825-1 is a humanoid entity. Following manifestation events, subjects will report fleeting awareness of a humanoid figure in a dark green suit and hat. Exact details are unknown and seem to vary between manifestation events. Research into SCP-9825 events suggests that this image is the result of the human brain attempting to rationalise a [thaumaturgic](goc-supplemental-thaumatology)^^ⓘ^^ThaumaturgyThaumaturgy is the term for what is colloquially referred to as "magic". Thaumatology is the study of magical principles as a practical science. Click this link for more information. footprint of significantly large magnitude.

SCP-9825 events are always focused around a targeted individual. The target (henceforth referred to as SCP-9825-A) must be currently undergoing "purification"; this purification is defined as a significant increase in internal body temperatureBaseline internal body temperature is 37 °C.. During this purification period, SCP-9825-1 will manifest an instance of SCP-9825-2 in the immediate area, beyond the eyesight of SCP-9825-A.

SCP-9825-2 is an anomalous object that takes the form of the lowest denomination physical currency in common use in the surrounding region. This form is functionally identical to any non-anomalous denomination, with one minor alteration; SCP-9825-2 instances will always have a small design of a four-leaf clover. SCP-9825-2 will always manifest in a position where SCP-9825-A will notice it within approximately five minutes. The object will emit an anomalous shine in order to catch the eye of SCP-9825-A. Beyond this shine, no [cognitohazardous](psa-cognitohazards-and-you)^^ⓘ^^CognitohazardCognitohazards are images or sounds that cause adverse effects upon perception, including memetic effects, injury, or death. Click this link for more information., [memetic](understanding-memetics)^^ⓘ^^MemeticsMemetics refers to the spread of information. Memetic anomalies may have a mental effect on an individual, such as an anomalous compulsion. Click this link for more information., or other similar effect exists around SCP-9825-2; the retrieval of SCP-9825-2 by SCP-9825-A is presumed to be purely a result of natural human curiosity.

Upon inspection by SCP-9825-A, an [ontological](clef101)^^ⓘ^^OntokinesisOntokinesis is the term for what is colloquially referred to as "reality bending". The adjective "ontological" is used to describe phenomena affecting reality. Click this link for more information. restructuring will take place; this restructuring will be reminiscent of either a stroke of good or bad luck. The method of determining whether SCP-9825-2 will have a positive or negative effect is currently unknown.**Update 21/04/2021:** This information is outdated. Refer to Addendum 9825.3. The effects and severity of this restructuring vary; however, they invariably appear within twelve hours of inspection. Following the event, SCP-9825-2 will lose its four-leaf clover pattern, becoming indistinguishable from a non-anomalous coin. Foundation agents have thus far been unable to procure an SCP-9825-2 instance due to this property.**Update 20/08/2026:** This information is outdated. Refer to Addendum 9825.4. In addition, SCP-9825-2 will cause a temporary, quantifiable increase in the local Hume field proportional to the degree of ontological restructuring.

---

**Discovery:** Due to the anomalous effects of SCP-9825-2, a minor urban legend had appeared surrounding a green man leaving pennies with four-leaf clovers. As such, groups and individuals began to seek out former SCP-9825-A instances, attempting to interview them in order to replicate SCP-9825-2's effect for themselves. Due to the existence of numerous similar urban legends, the Foundation deemed containment unnecessary.

<summary>▶ Incident 9825.1 (13/08/1995) ◀</summary>

= **INCIDENT LOG**

---

On 13/08/1995 at 07:43, an abnormally large spike in the Hume field was detected. Centred around a residence on the outskirts of Tauranga, New Zealand, this drew the attention of Foundation staff stationed at Site-85. Shortly afterwards, Mobile Task Force Lambda-9 ("Shields of Mortality") was dispatched to investigate. The Hume field readings had diminished to a fraction of their peak by the time Lambda-9 had arrived in the area (3 hours post-spike). 34-year-old resident Shayna Atwood was found in a distressed state, curled in the fetal position beside a personal tanning bed. Interviews conducted with Atwood revealed that she very recently received news that her dog had been euthanised following a vehicular collision. An interview with Atwood revealed the causal link between SCP-9825-2 and the accident. Despite attempts, SCP-9825-2 was unable to be found. Atwood was [amnesticised](updated-amnestics-guide)^^ⓘ^^AmnesticsAmnestics are memory-affecting drugs. Typically utilised by the Foundation for damage control and to maintain the Veil. Click this link for more information. as a preventative measure for possible future Veil-breaking actions relating to SCP-9825.

Following this operation, SCP-9825 was classified and designated Keter. Foundation efforts into monitoring SCP-9825 events were established. A unique Hume spectrum present in the area following the SCP-9825 event was used by researchers to improve the Foundation's detection capabilities of SCP-9825 events by a significant degree.

**Addendum 9825.1 (Event Logs):** 

*For the full documentation of SCP-9825 event logs, contact Senior Researcher Miles.*

In all cases, SCP-9825-2 has been unrecoverable; this is generally a result of SCP-9825-A losing or spending SCP-9825-2.

**Event Number:** 08

**Date:** 31/02/1997

**Subject:** 51-year-old Icelandic male

**Body Temperature:** 39 °C

**Location:** ████ hot spring, Iceland

**SCP-9825-2 appearance:** 1 króna coin

---

**Event Summary:** SCP-9825-1 manifested while SCP-9825-A was visiting a public hot spring near their home. SCP-9825-A was severely inebriated at the moment of the event. SCP-9825-2 was spotted and picked up by SCP-9825-A almost immediately upon exiting the hot spring. A minor degree of ontological restructuring immediately took place, causing SCP-9825-A to vomit profusely in the vicinity of other individuals utilising the baths. SCP-9825-A was subsequently banned from the hot spring indefinitely.

Foundation staff detected the relevant spike in the local Hume field; however, no Veil-breaking occurrences took place, and as such the effects of the event were left to stand.

**Event Number:** 56

**Date:** 17/07/2004

**Subject:** 19-year-old Chinese female

**Body Temperature:** 40 °C

**Location:** Chengdu, Sichuan Province, China

**SCP-9825-2 appearance:** 1 *jiǎo* coin

---

**Event Summary:** SCP-9825-A at the moment of the event was homeless, living in Sichuan Province, China. At the time of the event, SCP-9825-A was suffering from an intense fever as a result of infection. Shortly after exiting a local homeless shelter, seeking medication, SCP-9825-2 had manifested on the pavement next to SCP-9825-A. Having assumed it to have been dropped by a passing individual, SCP-9825-A picked up the coin. A minor ontological restructure took place, accompanied by the corresponding spike in the Hume field. Shortly afterwards, an upper-class passerby handed SCP-9825-A a 40 *yuán* note.Worth approximately $10 USD in 2026.

Due to no Veil-breaking occurrences, the Foundation elected not to interfere in the aftermath of this event.

**Event Number:** 89

**Date:** 04/03/2006

**Subject:** 27-year-old Corsican female

**Body Temperature:** 42 °C

**Location:** Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

**SCP-9825-2 appearance:** 5 centavo coin

---

**Event Summary:** During the event, SCP-9825-A was on holiday in Brazil with their current partner. SCP-9825-A was reclining on the balcony of their accommodation. High local temperatures led to SCP-9825-A suffering from early-onset heatstroke; while retrieving a glass of water, an SCP-9825-2 instance manifested within the hotel room. Upon SCP-9825-A retrieving the coin, a severe ontological restructuring took place. The resulting spike in the local Hume field alerted the local Site-145, who readied a medevac team for rapid response.

As a result of the restructure, an overloaded powerboard sparked an electrical fire. The fire swiftly took hold within the building, leading SCP-9825-A to hurriedly leave the building via the nearest fire exit. SCP-9825-A's partner was trapped under a burning wooden beam while attempting to escape; smoke inhalation and third-degree burns resulted in his death.

Foundation medevac arrived shortly after. Paramedics treated SCP-9825-A for heatstroke and second-degree burns at the scene. SCP-9825-A was transferred to a civilian hospital.

**Addendum 9825.2 (Update 13/11/2018):** During Q3 2018, a technology start-up achieved sudden, widespread success, culminating from approximately a decade of effort. Foundation webcrawlers recognised the start-up's CEO as being a former instance of SCP-9825-A. Further investigation revealed a causal link between SCP-9825-2 and the success of this start-up. The ontological restructuring caused by the SCP-9825 event pertaining to the subject resulted in the individual being abducted by bandits while travelling through Kazakhstan. During their capture, the subject bonded with a fellow captive; upon their rescue, the subject and the other captive remained friends, and decided that their "experience had taught them to seize life for all it is worth".Taken from a 23/07/2018 interview with the subject. This remark was met with large outcry from many environmental activist groups.

Soon after, Foundation staff assigned to the long-ignored SCP-9825 began searching through all past SCP-9825 event logs for the current status of each SCP-9825-A instance. In every case, the life situation of the former SCP-9825-A's had increased in a generally upward trend proportional to the degree of ontological restructuring during the original SCP-9825 event. However, none of these actions were due to anomalous compulsion; each SCP-9825 instance's prosperity was the direct result of their own actions.

**Addendum 9825.3 (Update 21/04/2021):** 

Recent technological developments culminating in the creation of the APERTURE EngineAstral Projection Extraction Recording and Transmission Unit for Remote Experience opened a new avenue into anomalous research, allowing Foundation researchers to revisit previously archived anomalies.

The APERTURE Engine is a device which allows baseline humans to visit and interact with other planes of existence via astral projection. The Engine also allows broadcasting and recording of these experiences, deeming it incredibly useful for exploration into unknown spaces and communication with unknown entities.

Prior to 20/04/2021, the research team assigned to SCP-9825 requested access to the APERTURE Engine for the purpose of communication with SCP-9825-1. Permission was granted shortly afterwards, and MTF Sigma-25 ("Ghostbusters") was requisitioned for assistance.

The unique Hume spectrum of SCP-9825-1, once keyed into the APERTURE Engine, allowed a member of MTF Sigma-25 (Agent Roy Ricard) to establish contact with the entity on 21/02/2021. The following interview took place shortly after.

<details>
<summary>▶ Interview 9825.1 (20/04/2021) ◀</summary>

= **INTERVIEW LOG**

---

**SUBJECT:** SCP-9825-1

**INTERVIEWER:** Agent Roy Ricard (MTF Sigma-25 "Ghostbusters")

**FOREWORD:** Conducted using the APERTURE Engine.

---

*The APERTURE Engine is engaged, and Agent Ricard begins astral projection. When a connection with Command is established, Agent Ricard is seen within a Victorian-era conservatory lined with plants. A humanoid figure dressed in a dark green suit and hat is busy watering the plants, presumed to be SCP-9825-1.*

**SCP-9825-1:** Why, hello there! It's not often I get visitors. Come, come! Have a seat!

*Agent Ricard makes no attempt to move.*

**Agent Ricard:** I'd prefer to stand, if you don't mind. 

**SCP-9825-1:** Very well then! Now... to what and whom do I owe the pleasure?

**Agent Ricard:** You can call me Agent Ricard. I'm currently employed by the SCP Foundation. I've come here today to ask you a few questions regarding your activities back in baseline reality.

**SCP-9825-1:** The notes? Ah, the coins... I'm surprised it took this long for a Child of the Sun to make my acquaintance!

**Agent Ricard:** Right. Well, I'd like to begin with the questions.

**SCP-9825-1:** Ask away!

*Agent Ricard pulls a small note-taking device from his pocket.*

**Agent Ricard:** Let us begin. What parameters decide whether a coin will produce a positive or negative effect? The Foundation's research thus far has indicated it was random.

**SCP-9825-1:** Oh, my dear boy, it is anything but random! However, I am detecting an underlying question here, so I hope you won't mind if I go ahead and answer it! Tell me, Agent Ricard, what does green mean to you? Why green?

**Agent Ricard:** I'm afraid I don't follow.

**SCP-9825-1:** Never fear, Agent Ricard! Green is growth. Growth, life! This *beautiful* gift given to us all by the heavens above! What is existence without growth?

*SCP-9825-1 stops watering the plants and turns to Agent Ricard. Small flames can be seen within its eye sockets.*

**SCP-9825-1:** Our lives only have meaning when we grow. The very purpose of life is to do so. However, you Children of the Sun often find yourselves in a bit of a pickle! Your world is hard. It is lonely, it is dark, it is cold. You find it is easy to dig yourself into a nice little hole where the darkness is more familiar, and the cold a little less bitter. 

**SCP-9825-1:** Growth stems from two catalysts; the first is hardship. This hardship is temporary. It is the hardship which causes you to break out from who you thought you were, who people told you you were, who you thought you had to be, and reach new, towering heights. The second is opportunity; a golden opportunity, one that arises and allows you to reach your truest potential. My goal is simply to *find* those who find themselves stuck in that damp, dark hole; those who, through flame, are ready to purge themselves of their past, those who are ready to set alight their burdens and illuminate the path forward. I decide what each one needs, and I haven't been wrong yet! The coins are my funny little way of doing so. I was *ever* so entranced by your little superstitions!

**Agent Ricard:** I... I see.

*Agent Ricard pauses for a moment.*

**Agent Ricard:** Who do you identify as? Where did yo-

**SCP-9825-1:** Ah! I'm afraid we've run out of time for today. No more questions!

*Agent Ricard attempts to protest but finds himself unable to speak.*

**SCP-9825-1:** Now, I'd say it's time for you to go. Wouldn't you?

*SCP-9825-1 makes a waving motion with its hand. Agent Ricard is forcefully removed back to baseline reality.*

**Addendum 9825.4 (Update 20/08/2026):** On 18/08/2026, Foundation researcher Spencer Miles was involved in testing of a pyrokinetic anomaly, resulting in an increase of Senior Researcher Miles' internal body temperature. Upon conclusion of their "purification", Senior Researcher Miles noticed an instance of SCP-9825-2 and quickly alerted the Site containment team. As this was the first occurrence of a Foundation staff member becoming an instance of SCP-9825-A, testing commenced shortly afterwards.

= **RESEARCHER NOTE**

---

I have to write this down, for the sake of anybody who hasn’t picked up on the implications of the recent SCP-9825 testing. I’ll throw it on the end of the file, just to make sure.

This *entire* time we thought that the coins were duds after the first use. I mean, can you blame us? The clover disappeared, and it was practically impossible to find them after an event. The dash-A’s usually lost them by the time we got around to questioning. But now, we're seeing that those coins are still active, and have been the entire time…

They could be anywhere. They could be *everywhere*. How the hell is the Foundation supposed to contain every penny on Earth?

Senior Researcher Miles
