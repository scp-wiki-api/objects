RESTRICTED

**Item #:** SCP-9827

**Object Class:** Euclid

# Special Containment Procedures

SCP-9827 is to be hosted on an isolated, air-gapped terminal at Site-19. Staff must not remain active on the page for longer than fifteen continuous minutes. Reading sessions require a second observer present at all times, stationed outside of visual range of the monitor, whose sole task is calling time. Under no circumstances is SCP-9827 to be printed, copied to external media, or forwarded via internal mail. Terminal access logs are to be reviewed nightly by RAISA staff for any discrepancy between reported reading duration and file growth, however minor.

# Description

SCP-9827 presents as a single .docx file, roughly forty kilobytes at first inspection, recovered from a decommissioned records server outside Cheyenne Mountain. It looks ordinary. Standard Foundation header, standard margins, a page count that sits quietly at six until somebody actually opens it, at which point the number begins climbing without any input from the reader, sometimes by three pages, sometimes by eleven, and no consistent rate has yet been established despite four separate attempts at measurement by Dr. Okonkwo-Reyes' team. It seems to know it's being watched. The growth tracks reading time, not clock time a researcher who stared at page one without scrolling for twenty minutes produced no new material, while a fast reader triggered expansion within ninety seconds.

The first new pages to appear are always testing logs, dated to the actual calendar date of the session, describing containment procedures that match whatever protocol Site-19 happens to be running that week down to the initial of the signing officer. Past roughly the fifteen-minute mark hence the containment threshold the document stops describing procedure and starts describing the reader. Small things first: a hand drifting toward a coffee mug, a decision to skip the elevator for the stairwell, an off hand remark to a coworker that hasn't been made yet. Every documented case shows the same pattern, and it is difficult to overstate how consistent this pattern is: whatever SCP-9827 writes down happens, in order, usually within minutes of being read. It does not predict so much as it assigns.

Beyond the fortieth minute a threshold no surviving subject has crossed voluntarily the document allegedly begins narrating the readers death. Three personnel have reached this section. None have lived past the paragraph describing it, and this suggests, though it cannot yet be proven, that the file's authority over its subject extends past simple foreshadowing into something closer to instruction.

09:14 — Page count: 6. Standard.

09:33 — Page count: 21. New section describes Petrov reaching for a water bottle on the left side of the terminal desk. Petrov, unprompted, reaches for a water bottle on the left side of the terminal desk. Halvorsen notes there was no water bottle there fifteen minutes prior.

09:39 — Petrov attempts to close the document. Cannot. File will not respond to standard close commands, force quit, or terminal reboot.

09:44 — Page count: 33. Section describes Petrov standing, walking toward the door, and saying the words "I need air" to Halvorsen. Petrov stands. Petrov says the words. Halvorsen begins recording verbally per emergency protocol.

09:47 — Petrov returns to the terminal against Halvorsen's direct order. Says he "has to see how it ends."

09:52 — Page count: 41. Document now describes cardiac arrest, onset within four minutes, no external cause identifiable. Petrov reports chest tightness at 09:52. Halvorsen calls for medical.

09:55 — Petrov unresponsive. CPR administered. Time of death recorded as 09:56, consistent with the document's stated timeline to within thirty seconds.

09:58 — Terminal auto-locks. Halvorsen reports the file, prior to lockout, displayed one additional line that had not been present during any prior review of the page.
