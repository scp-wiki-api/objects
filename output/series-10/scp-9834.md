**Item:** SCP-9834

**Object Class:** Leucippus

**Special Containment Procedures:** Survived subjects are monitored incrementally. Public outcry and conversation to be suppressed and downplayed through avenues of local jurisdictions. Misdirection and false dissemination of topics surrounding episode encouraged. Arresting of third party public groups encouraged. Internal review pending. Army supervisors retrieved nineteen bodies kept in [*https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/series-0-glossary Site-Blue] cold storage for current and future analysis.

**Description:** Discovered following public exposure as 2005 Cedar Park mass incident, initial coverup impossible due to circumstance of incident inciting scrutiny and interference from general populace, secondary and tertiary investigative journalistic bodies obscuring site of episode. Site cleanup resulted in loss of significant assets prior to Program acquisition. Paranormal nature unclear if at all present.

Designation is the 2005 mass fatality event that occurred on fourth night of five-day ArFIX Biannual Technical Conference, Cedar Park, TX. Following final presentations, attendees roamed premises before disbanding within main facility and lodging establishment, Open Seasons Recreation Center and Hotel, committing to personal hours before returning to individual accommodations. All 284 deaths subsequently occurred inside locked facilities within estimated 30-minute window at an undetermined time following 11 PM on Thursday 22 June.

All deaths were concluded as various forms of suicide ranging in methods and inflicted violence. Expirations followed patterns of sudden onset self-harm utilizing facilities immediately on-person and within subjects' rooms to induce passing as immediately as possible. Added presence of multiple blunt force injuries resulting in death. Majority of subjects lack documented history of suicidal ideation and similar symptoms, testimonies regarding behavioral patterns during conference appear as enthusiastic and eager, though displays may possibly have been anticipation towards future self-terminations. Reports of security footage depicting subjects’ rushing throughout building to reach personal lodging under audit due to missing recordings and ambiguous active status of Open Seasons' CCTV network (current lack of on-site witness statements to corroborate claims).
