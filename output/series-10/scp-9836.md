**Item #:** 9836

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9836 is to be in a 1 ft by 1 ft locked box at all times. Personnel are not permitted to wear the glasses. During testing, the room must be locked from the outside. The containment room (or storage bay) must have a lockdown button that shuts a metal shutter on both sides of the window and locks the room until Security has recontained SCP-9836. The anomaly requires level two clearance to enter.

**Description:** SCP-9836 appears to be a pair of red glasses with no markings that have not been tested to be destroyed because there is only one pair of them. The glasses are completely see-through. Whenever someone wears SCP-9836, the experience enhances strength and anger towards all living things. After someone takes off the glasses, the effects immediately disappear. The glasses were tested on animals in Tests 9836-2 to 9836-10. In conclusion, it was found that effects occurred only when the glasses were placed on a humanoid creature and the glasses fully fit on them, for instance, an orangutan. SCP-9836. SCP-9836 is currently at Site-██.

**Incident Report:** On January 19, 2018, Test 9836-1 was taking place. The D-Class who wore the glasses proceeded to destroy the window. The scientist was wounded before security arrived; when security did, the D-Class was terminated. Afterward, the lockdown procedure was introduced, with a metal shutter on both sides of the viewing glass.
