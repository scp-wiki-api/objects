**Item #:** SCP-9837

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9837 is to be put into a lead cage coated with paint that is excatly 21% Neptunium, 59% lapis lazuli and 20% walnut oil. The cage must be placed into a dimly lit dark blue room. Provide hot alkaline water and crushed up mix of volcanic rocks through an automatic and silent dispensing system.

**Description:** SCP-9837 extremely resembles a california condor in its shape and size with some differences in wing head proportions. SCP-9837's body is made from an obsidian-like material with a slight red glow. Along its body are sharp shards of obsidian, serving as feathers. SCP-9837 has featherless a head with small dark purple eyes and a large black beak with a purple crack-like pattern. 

SCP-9837 sings quite often but its song is comparable to deep howling mixed with wind, the sound is incredibly quiet and can only be heard when in the same room as SCP-9837.

SCP-9837 cannot be decumented in any visual form. Photographs, video footage and even art depicting SCP-9837 will automatically corrupt and display black and purple crystals instead of SCP-9837, this process takes anywhere from 2 to 8 minutes.

SCP-9837's internal body temperature is usually around 100 °C or 212 °F and its external body temperature sits around 45 °C or 113 °F. The body temperature of SCP-9837 is closely tied to its sleep cycle. When fully awake temperatures are its usual ones, when asleep a 30% decrease in body temperature can be observed and when waking up a 30% increase can be observed.

The behavior of SCP-9837 is fairly predictable. It is very calm but also cautious, but when SCP-9837 experiences high stress which can be caused through loud sounds and fast moving objects it thrashes around violently and its body temperature rises up to 60% depending on how stressed it is until the source is removed. 

SCP-9837 was first discovered in The Big Obsidian Flow, seeming observing the land for any food or threats. SCP-9837 was observed from a safe distance for 5 days. Thanks to this research, it was found out that SCP-9837 has a diet consisting of volcanic rocks and iron rich minerals. SCP-9837 was lured to a trap using these parts of its diet and breaking them down with a high power laser to release a strong odor. SCP-9837 was successfully captured and taken into a research facility for further testing.

After 5 weeks of testing different elements on SCP-9837 in order to see how its body reacts to them, it was found that SCP-9837 experiences number of effects when in the vicinity of pure Neptunium. Effects include: Body weakness, molting shards and its red glow dimming. This has been found to be extremely harmful to the bird and is never to be tested again. Interestingly, Neptunium mixed with lapis lazuli has a calming and healing effect on SCP-9837.

A few Class-D personnel have interacted with SCP-9837 within the same 5 weeks and it was found that just looking at SCP-9837 for a prolonged period of time can cause a few undesirable effects. D-[REDACTED] was instructed to look directly at SCP-9837 for as long as they could, after 2 minutes D-[REDACTED] noted how hard it was to look away and after 5 minutes and 31 seconds D-[REDACTED] lost all muscle strength and fell to the ground, seemingly dead. After paramedics confirmed D-[REDACTED] died, personnel with hazmat equipment moved D-[REDACTED] to an isolated laboratory for further testing. It was concluded that D-[REDACTED] extensive nerve damage, ultimately killing them. It seems that under 3 minutes or less of exposure to SCP-9837, slowly destroys nerves responsible for sensing pain. After more than 3 minutes of exposure the nerves responsible for muscle and organ control will rapidly start dying, leading to death of the subject in another 1 to 2 minutes. It has still not been found what exactly causes this effect, further testing required.

> +++++ **Leed researcher:** Dr. Normer

> **Dr. Normer:** "Good evening, D-9174. To begin with the test please step into the test chamber, use the key provided to open and enter SCP-9837's cage. Report any strange effects as you continue throughout the test."

> **D-9174:** "Hey, isn't this the new one?" *D-9174 enters the chamber, unlocks the cage, steps inside.* "What now?"

> **Dr. Normer:** "That would be correct. Please, let's continue. Take a step closer and keep looking at SCP-9837 for as long as possible."

> **D-9174:** "Yeah, sure... That's simple enough." *D-9174 steps closer to SCP-9873 and proceeds to look at it.* "Holy, look at this thing. It looks so gorgeous. It's like... my back hurts less the longer I look... actually I can't feel my back."

> **Dr. Normer:** "Your back pain is gone and you cannot feel your back? Noted. This is enough, please leave the cage and test chamber."

> **D-9174:** "I can't look away. I don't want to look away, I feel so peaceful." *D-9174's left arm goes limp but D-9174 has no reaction and continues staring at SCP-9837.*

> **Dr. Normer:** "You have been there for 4 minutes, we don't know what might happen! D-9174 leave the chamber immediately or face severe consequences!"

> **D-9174:** "No... I don't want to." *D-9174 continues staring at SCP-9837 with a lifeless smile on their face.* "Just a little... longer." *D-9174 suddenly collapses to the ground like a lifeless husk.*

> **Dr. Normer:** "God damn it! Send in the paramedics! D-9174 has collapsed!"

> ++++ **End of Test Log #239**
