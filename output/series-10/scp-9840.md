**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9840 cannot be contained. Personnel are to suppress any information about SCP-9840 before it causes panic. All other efforts are to be focused on Operation Overlook.

**Description:** SCP-9840 is a dead star. It is able to move. 

At irregular intervals, SCP-9840 stops and approaches a celestial object. It stays with the object anywhere from weeks to decades. Objects approached by SCP-9840 will eventually begin to lose color and luminosity for as long as it is with them. These objects rarely recover once it leaves. 

SCP-9840 is orbited by a lone planet, designated "Proserpina." It is, and has been, the only planet orbiting SCP-9840, predating its stellar death. Proserpina moves with SCP-9840.It is theorized Proserpina is not aware that SCP-9840 burned out.

Despite the proximity to SCP-9840, Proserpina has never lost color nor its luminosity. While Proserpina is largely ignored by SCP-9840, there have been four recorded instances of contact, during which the two bodies tidally lock. SCP-9840 has not been seen engaging in this behavior with any other celestial object. The reason for this is unknown.

**Discovery:** SCP-9840 was first catalogued in 1931 as an object of unusually high proper motion, unremarkable on its own. Follow-up tracking in 1937 found its rate of motion increasing between observations, consistent with an approaching object. It was flagged as anomalous that same year. By 1950, it had entered the outer solar system. By 1957, it had reached Venus.

On December 14, 1962, NASA's Mariner 2 recorded a brief, unattributed magnetic field reading during its flyby of Venus, in the same scan that confirmed the planet itself has no significant magnetic field.

SCP-9840 has since been linked to 9 additional dimming events predating its arrival at Venus, none of which were flagged as anomalous at the time.

**Operation Overlook:** On 05/25/2006 SCP-9840 left Venus. Its new trajectory suggests it has become attracted to Terra.And possibly even Luna. This discovery has prompted the Foundation to launch Operation Overlook, with the goal of making Terra as unappealing as possible before the arrival of SCP-9840.
