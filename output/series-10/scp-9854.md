Item #: SCP-9854

Object Class: Safe

Special Containment Procedures: SCP-9854 is to be stored in a hermetically sealed, vacuum-isolated containment vault within the Chemical Hazards Sector of Site-19. As the anomalous fluid undergoes rapid exothermic reactions and structural degradation when in contact with standard polymers, common metals, and organic matter, all experimental handling must be conducted using remote-operated mechanical manipulators or Class-A hazmat suits lined with reinforced titanium alloys.

Any unauthorized breach of the containment perimeter will be met with immediate lethal force by sector security personnel. Following any such event, responding guards are to be administered Class-A amnestics to mitigate severe psychological trauma secondary to visual exposure to the object's effects.

Description: SCP-9854 is a generic, unbranded 750 ml white plastic container. The object features no commercial labels, barcodes, or manufacturing marks, distinguished only by a bright red pump-dispenser cap. The interior contains a highly viscous, white emulsion that emits a faint scent identical to commercial body wash. The container itself (designated SCP-9854-2) is entirely immune to the corrosive properties of its contents (SCP-9854-1).

The anomalous properties of SCP-9854-1 activate immediately upon topical contact with living human dermal tissue:

Irreversible Liquefactive Necrosis: Upon application to a wet epidermis, SCP-9854-1 initiates a catastrophic chemical reaction that rapidly dissolves skin layers, adipose tissue, and underlying musculature. The compound binds to cellular structures at a molecular level, rendering it completely insoluble in water and immune to standard chemical neutralizing agents.

Artificial Neurological Maintenance: SCP-9854-1 alters the subject's nervous system, actively inhibiting the biological pathways responsible for vasovagal syncope, neurogenic shock, or loss of consciousness due to extreme trauma.

Clinical Progression: The subject is forced to remain fully conscious and receptive to nociceptive stimuli throughout a protracted degradation process lasting between 12 and 48 hours, during which the necrosis systematically advances toward internal organs. The survival rate is 0%. The only known method to prevent total systemic consumption is the complete amputation of the affected limb within three seconds of initial exposure.

Recovery Log: The object was recovered in [REDACTED] following the interception of a local emergency services dispatch. A civilian caller reported severe chemical burns sustained during routine showering, explicitly stating that the substance could not be washed off. Local emergency services initially suspected an industrial accident or a domestic attack involving military-grade incendiaries (such as Napalm-B). However, forensic analysis of the scene revealed only a single unbranded bottle on the shower floor. Operational assets belonging to the Global Occult Coalition (GOC) attempted to seize the item for immediate incineration under the assumption of a localized biological threat. Foundation field agents successfully intercepted the GOC transport detail, acquired the anomaly without casualties, and routed it to Site-19.

Addendum 9854-1: Audio Transcript (Emergency Dispatch Log)

Date: [REDACTED]

Source: [REDACTED] 

Emergency Communications Center Foreword: The following audio log was extracted from civilian telecom servers by Foundation Cyber-Security assets.

[BEGIN LOG]

Dispatcher: Emergency services, what is your location?

Victim: (High-pitched screaming. Heavy sound of pressurized water striking tile.) Help! Please, God, help me! It's burning! I'm burning alive!Dispatcher: Sir, calm down. What is your location? Is there a fire in the house?

Victim: No! No fire! I'm in the shower! I just put the soap on and it's—it's melting my legs! It's burning through me! (Sounds of violent retching and severe hyperventilation.)

Dispatcher: Sir, listen to me carefully. Step out of the shower immediately and rinse the area with cold water.

Victim: (Screaming intensifies) I am! I’ve had the water on full blast for three minutes! It won't come off! It’s sticking to me! I can see my bones, Jesus Christ, I can see the meat on my legs! Get it off me! Get it off! (A heavy, metallic thud is heard, consistent with the victim collapsing against the porcelain tub.)

Dispatcher: Medical units and fire rescue are en route to your position. Stay on the line. What kind of chemical is it? Did you mix bleach or ammonia?

Victim: (Voice drops significantly in volume, characterized by heavy wheezing, though vocalizations of pain remain constant.) No... it was... just a white bottle... red cap... normal... it won't wash off... please, just cut my leg— (Audio cuts to severe static interference due to moisture buildup. The victim continues to emit rhythmic, high-intensity groans but fails to respond to further prompting.)

Dispatcher: Sir? Sir, are you still there?

[END LOG]

Post-Recovery Note: When emergency personnel breached the residence fourteen minutes later, the victim was found alive, conscious, and experiencing massive involuntary muscle spasms despite suffering total liquefactive necrosis across approximately 40% of their lower body mass. Medical logs indicate the subject's nervous system remained artificially sustained until Foundation containment protocols were established and termination was authorized for clinical study.
