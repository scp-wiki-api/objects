**Item #:** SCP-9859

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9859 is contained at a provisional site in ████████████████████████.  A minimum of 2 foundation personnel are to maintain residence at the provisional site at all times.  These personnel may be sourced from low risk D-Class population if needed, provided they maintain good behavior and adhere to site requirements.  On-site residents are required to interact with SCP-9859-1-A for a minimum of 8 hours per day.  One Form 9859-1-F needs to be completed by any on-site personnel and submitted daily.  A score of 20 or higher on Form 9859-1-F will require an audit from the research team and may require reassignment of personnel.  A score of 35 or higher requires immediate action of procedure CANY.  Site residents are to fit in as normal city residents at all times in public, as well as maintaining the site to look like a normal city residence.

**Description:** SCP-9859 is a custom assembled gaming computer.  It cannot be powered off, shut down, disconnected from the wall outlet, or disassembled by any means.  The computer is constantly running a custom modified version of The Elder Scrolls 5: Skyrim, designated SCP-9859-1.  The game has a modified character assigned to follow the player character designated SCP-9859-1-A.  SCP-9859-1-A is a Level 3 reality bender who is capable of interacting with our reality, but not his own reality inside of SCP-9859-1.  SRA's have been deployed in the area, but seem to have little effect on SCP-9859-1-A due to his abnormal range of influence.  SCP-9859-1 also contains a number of entities designated SCP-9859-1-B.  These entities are hypothesized to be added by the only modified file in the game directory labeled "Uncannyrim.esm", which seems to have been developed on SCP-9859, although the files cannot be accessed because they are in use by the system and SCP-9859-1-A is aware when the system files are being manipulated, which will add to the score for SCP-9859-1-F for the day.  SCP-9859-1-B entities must be properly identified by the player and then defeated.  SCP-9859-1-A must be interacted with daily, and kept happy.

**Additional Notes:** SCP-9859-1-A is aware of his existence within the game and admits to putting himself there, however this subject should not be brought up in discussion with SCP-9859-1-A.  SCP-9859-1-A has been known to teleport undefeated SCP-9859-1-B instances to our reality if he becomes upset, irritated, or bored.  SCP-9859-1-B instances are highly dangerous and predatory to humans.  If SCP-9859-1-A becomes agitated during gameplay and teleports an instance of SCP-9859-1-B or if there is any indication that he might teleport one, procedure CANY is to be followed immediately.
