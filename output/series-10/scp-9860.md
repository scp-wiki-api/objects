**Item #:** SCP-9860

**Object Class:** [Diogenes-Goetia](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/series-0-glossary#toc7)

**Special Containment Procedures:** The Department of Postal Inspection will conduct quality control checks on shipments of contraceptives distributed within TN. SCP-9860 products must be seized, refrigerated, and stored at Site Red Armory, Wing C4, Corridor 12-E, Compartment 140646.

The Department of Publicity must actively notify the newspapers affiliated with the *Celestino Group Inc.* holding company within Tennessee County regarding the gradual and unexplained increase in maternal mortality, citing fabricated sources.

**Description:** Discovered in the inventory of the Jesus Christ of Our Sorrows Clinic, Chattanooga, TN 1980. Latex male contraceptives of the *Anti-Baby* brand produced by *Celestino Group Inc.* Their peculiarity lies in an outer layer of seminal fluid derived from an unknown mammalian species, with a shelf life of two months under ambient conditions. It is capable of fertilizing humans, resulting in a corniform mole. SCP-9860 is manufactured at a facility in Chattanooga, a subsidiary of *Celestino Group Inc.*

## **Forensic Record - Corniform Molar Mass**

#### Dissection Room and Organic Specimens, Site Red

---

**Category(ies):** Paranormal | Organic | Animal | Mammal | Amorphous | Unborn

@@ @@****Forensic Team:****

* Dr. Sarah Abney

* Dr. Robert Sanchez @@ @@

**Context:** Horn-like molar mass *(see {{Fig. 1}})* developed in the uterus of ████ G., age 20. Reported by the medical team of Hamilton County Health Department, Chattanooga, where the patient was admitted after suddenly entering a catatonic state. What was initially reported as a hydatidiform molar mass turned out to be a paranormal formation with dozens of protruding horns. ████ G. did not survive the operation. Her family was compensated by the Program following the destruction of all records at the facility. The formation was described as “unusual but natural” to witnesses. The victim’s partner stated that they did not have unprotected sex. No further details regarding her sexual history are known.

**Observations:** The formation possesses a total of twenty-three (23) horns from various mammals (sheep, cattle, goats, etc.) emerging from its fleshy core (0.8 inches) composed of trophoblastic cells, with an average length of ~40 inches per instance. These eventually pierced the abdomen, indicating that they grew rapidly within a few minutes. The size of the molar mass may be a sign that the formation was two months into gestation at the time of the incident, a period during which it remained inactive.

**ACT. 02/07/1980:** Numerous similar cases are being detected within Tennessee County, resulting in the deaths of █3 victims. Identifying the common cause of these incidents is a high priority.

= **WARNING**

The following record is strictly restricted to researchers assigned to the *Silvestre case*. Unauthorized distribution of this material to external agents will be considered a federal crime of treason.

The following meeting took place between Dr. Rust, the Tennessee regional director of *Sanitary Collective* (a front company of the Program), and Silvestre, the general pastor of the Celestine Church and CEO of *Celestino Group Inc.* Allegedly, the purpose of the meeting was to discuss the acquisition of the SCP-9860 production facility for its containment and possible conversion into a military asset.

---

**[START]**

00.00.00.00.

00.00.01.23. **Mr. Silvestre** Good afternoon!

00.00.02.10. **Dr. Rust** Hello. What did you want to discuss with us?

00.00.04.03. **Mr. Silvestre** Not much, really. Consider this an informal meeting; my decision isn’t going to change—I can assure you of that… There are some things money can’t buy, you know?

00.00.05.31. **Dr. Rust** We know that better than anyone—

00.00.06.10. **Mr. Silvestre** No.

00.00.07.10. **Dr. Rust** …Mr. Silvestre, if our proposal has offended you, there’s no need to call us in just to make that clear. Here at Sanitary Collective, we want to expand into new territories, and we’ve had our sights set on Tennessee for quite some time.

00.00.10.21. **Mr. Silvestre** No, my son, I don’t want to talk to any “sanitary” or any “collective”—that’s nonsense. I want to talk to *the Program*.

00.00.12.20. **Dr. Rust** Excuse me? The what?

00.00.13.50. **Mr. Silvestre** The Program, son! The s-c-p! This kind of strange initiative that my dear friends never stop talking about at my parties. They seem really enthusiastic about it—now I’m starting to understand why.

00.00.23.12. **Dr. Rust** I’m sorry, I don’t know what you’re talking about. You came to meet with Sanitary Collective because you wanted to discuss our offer to acquire your company. I’m just a representative.

00.00.35.54. **Mr. Silvestre** No. Neither you nor I are businesspeople. There are some things money can’t buy—take that to heart. Look, I know you’re ashamed of who you really are, but don’t worry. I forgive you.

00.00.46.25. **Dr. Rust** If you’re going to keep saying nonsense, you’re going to have to leave this room immediately.

00.01.02.40. **Mr. Silvestre** What for? So those burly guys out there can grab me and take me to the most inhospitable corner of this—and pardon my language—damned country? What you don’t know, son, is that I’ve known about them since before you were even born.

00.01.20.01. **Dr. Rust** Guards!

00.01.22.52. **Mr. Silvestre** Ah, what will poor [withheld] do when he finds out that his close friend Silvestre has been imprisoned by the very people who live off his generosity? Or the noble [withheld] once he realizes that his personal confessor is suffering at the hands of the very weapons he himself wields with the honor that you all lack.

00.01.54.12. **Dr. Rust** Wait, don’t do anything. How the hell do you know those names?

00.02.00.09. **Mr. Silvestre** That mouth of yours—I’m going to have to wash it out with soap.

00.02.01.00. **Dr. Rust** How the hell do you know them!

00.02.10.08. **Mr. Silvestre** My parties are very famous, you know? Too bad you didn’t get the invitation—though seeing what you’re doing to my mission, I don’t think you’d have been much of a guest.

00.02.34.39. **Dr. Rust** What mission are you talking about?

00.02.40.50. **Mr. Silvestre** The humble mission that all of the Lord’s shepherds selflessly share.

00.02.52.10. **Dr. Rust** Is it altruistic to murder women who just wanted to have a good time with their boyfriends?

00.03.10.30. **Mr. Silvestre** A good time! So that’s what sin is, huh? What a good time… I don’t know what kind of perversion is plaguing modern society; in my day, things weren’t like this. If you’re worried about the state of those lost girls, don’t worry any longer—all those murderous condoms have been sanctified with holy water so that their souls may find their way to paradise as soon as they die.

00.04.23.00. **Dr. Rust** What you are doing is an atrocity. You’d love it if that happened to your daughter.

00.04.59.30. **Mr. Silvestre** I don’t have one. Besides, I understand that this cuts especially deep in your heart. I’ve heard that your mother tried—well, she must have already told you—when you were just a tiny little thing in her womb. Would you have wanted to die? To be killed without even getting a taste of life first? These condoms are not a punishment; there are warnings.

00.05.49.22. **Dr. Rust** How… How…?

00.05.55.55. **Mr. Silvestre** The Lord knows everything. I am his pastor.

00.06.56.35. **Dr. Rust** You have a lot of blood on your hands. Are you really a Christian?

00.07.03.31. **Mr. Silvestre** Look, at the “parties” I’m telling you about… [Things happen](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-l106), things I’m not even the slightest bit proud of; I’m convinced you wouldn’t be either. But unfortunately, I must consider those men who revel in sin to be my friends. I have no choice! The earth wasn’t created in a day—do you think paradise will take any less time? The Lord holds the power, but all the kingdoms of the world and their glory are the possessions of these men. Faith is enough for oneself, but not enough to change things. Things don’t change by waiting for sinners to become saints. Nor can they be bought.

00.08.03.19. **Dr. Rust** You may go, Silvestre.

00.08.13.00. **Mr. Silvestre** Wait, I haven’t spoken to you yet. Forgive me if I got a little carried away with my sermon!

00.08.34.41. **Dr. Rust** What do you want?

00.08.12.43. **Mr. Silvestre** You won’t take my factory. What’s more, you won’t meddle in my future plans. I take pity on you—to atheist eyes, it must be very difficult to distinguish the work of the devil from the work of God. But don’t worry, I’ve been in this land of the damned for many years—far more than you realize. Do whatever you want, but do it wisely.

00.10.10.13. **Dr. Rust** Goodbye.

00.10.15.42. **Mr. Silvestre** Goodbye, my son. May the Lord be with you.

00.10.17.09.

**[END]**
