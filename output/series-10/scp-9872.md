**SCP-9872:** Satisfaction Guaranteed 

**Author:** 

**Content Warning:** This article contains sexually explicit themes.

**Item #:** SCP-9872

**Object Class:** ~~Safe~~ Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** All recovered instances of SCP-9872 are to be individually sealed in airtight evidence containers and stored within a secure anomalous-materials locker at Site-██. Access requires Level-3/9872 authorization. Under no circumstances is SCP-9872 to be introduced into the human body without authorization from the project director. Personnel handling SCP-9872 are to wear disposable gloves.

Foundation web-crawling systems are to monitor online marketplaces for listings matching known descriptions of SCP-9872. Particular attention is to be given to listings using the phrase “THREE COURSE DINNER GUM — AUTHENTIC” or displaying packaging bearing a stylized yellow W. Suspected instances are to be acquired and associated sellers and recipients identified.

The sole confirmed affected individual, designated SCP-9872-01, is housed in a modified medical containment suite at Site-██. Due to SCP-9872-01's mass and limited independent mobility, repositioning is to be performed using approved bariatric handling equipment under medical supervision. Invasive removal of anomalously generated tissue or fluid from SCP-9872-01 is prohibited without authorization from the project medical directorSee Addendum 9872.2.

SCP-9872-01 is sapient, cooperative, and does not require physical restraint. It is permitted personal electronic devices and monitored communication with designated individuals. Personnel are to accommodate reasonable requests from SCP-9872-01, provided they do not interfere with containment or ongoing medical observation.

**Description:** SCP-9872 is a stick of chewing gum, measuring approximately 7.0 cm in length, 1.4 width, and 0.15 cm in thickness. Recovered instances are individually enclosed in transparent polymer wrappers bearing a stylized yellow W. No manufacturer, ingredients, or other identifying information is present. Chemical analysis of recovered SCP-9872 material has identified conventional gum base, sweeteners, flavoring compounds, stabilizers, and food-grade additives. No component exhibiting independently anomalous properties has been identified.

When chewed by a human subject, SCP-9872 produces three sequential flavors:

* Tomato Soup

* Roast Beef and Baked Potato

* Blueberry Pie

Following manifestation of the third flavor, affected individuals undergo drastic physiological alteration. Only one confirmed exposure to SCP-9872 has been documented. The affected individual, designated SCP-9872-01, is an adult human female who voluntarily consumed one instance after purchasing it through an online marketplace.

**Discovery and Initial Containment:** SCP-9872-01 came to Foundation attention following automated review of emergency-service communications concerning an unidentified medical event. **Addendum 9872.1** — Recovered Digital Record

Examination of SCP-9872-01's mobile device following containment revealed that the subject had extensively documented her transformation through a private online messaging server.

The recovered material consists of ██ text messages, █ photographs, █ short video recordings, and five long-form written entries written between initial exposure to SCP-9872 and Foundation containment.

Timestamps associated with this material have allowed researchers to reconstruct the progression of SCP-9872-01's transformation with considerably greater precision than would otherwise have been possible. Of particular significance are five written entries composed by SCP-9872-01 throughout the event. These have been designated Documents 9872-1 through 9872-5. The entries were originally posted to Discord and ███████ during SCP-9872-01's transformation. Following Foundation intervention, all accessible copies were removed from both platforms. Archived versions, associated metadata, and relevant user interactions were subsequently transferred to Foundation custody.

| DOCUMENT | TIMESTAMP | SUBJECT-ASSIGNED TITLE | SUMMARY |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9872-1 | 07:58 | Disappointment | SCP-9872-01 expresses disappointment that SCP-9872 appears to be an ordinary novelty product. |
| 9872-2 | 09:06 | Okay, This Is Weird | Initial physical symptoms are documented. SCP-9872-01 expresses concern, but continues describing the experience to other users. |
| 9872-3 | 09:41 | Holy Shit | SCP-9872-01 confirms visible transformation and provides photographic evidence. Emotional response alternates between alarm and apparent excitement. |
| 9872-4 | 10:21 | Home | SCP-9872-01 documents increasing physical impairment while attempting to reach its residence. Despite worsening symptoms, it repeatedly refuses suggestions from other users to contact emergency services. |
| 9872-5 | 10:43–15:56 | SPHERICAL | SCP-9872-01 documents complete loss of independent mobility and progressive cognitive impairment. Portions of the document express apparent satisfaction with the transformation. |
The first four documents demonstrate no significant impairment in SCP-9872-01's written communication beyond informal language characteristic of the subject's normal online activity, until the last document. Analysis of 9872-5 indicates a progressive deterioration In spelling, sentence construction, temporal awareness, spatial reasoning, and sustained attention. beginning approximately 8 hours after exposure. Notably, SCP-9872-01 did not appear to recognize the severity of this deterioration.

The final message attributable to SCP-9872-01 was transmitted at 15:56.

> *berrybaka: blooob*

SCP-9872-01 has subsequently stated that it had no memory of composing this message.

**Addendum 9872.2** — Medical Examination

Following containment, SCP-9872-01 underwent comprehensive medical examination to determine the extent of its morphological and physiological alteration.Subject's heart, lungs, central nervous system, and portions of the gastrointestinal and reproductive systems remain anatomically recognizable and continue to function consistently with those of a healthy 28-year-old human female.  Tissue comprising the majority of SCP-9872-01's altered morphology has no recognizable analogue in human anatomy.

Biochemical analysis identified high concentrations of water, simple sugars, organic acids, anthocyanin-like pigments, pectin-associated polysaccharides, and volatile aromatic compounds. Comparative analysis found these tissues to closely resemble fruit of the Vaccinium genus.  “Like a blueberry.”- Dr. Mercer This similarity is systemic. All bodily fluids collected from SCP-9872-01 exhibit blue or blue-violet coloration and varying concentrations of the same compounds. Despite their anomalous composition, these fluids continue to perform their expected physiological functions.

Approximately 48 hours following containment, SCP-9872-01 developed spontaneous lactation. The resulting secretion chemically resembles other fluids produced by SCP-9872-01, with elevated concentrations of proteins and lipids. No conventional human milk has been detected.

SCP-9872-01's caloric and hydration requirements have not increased proportionally with its mass. Controlled caloric and fluid restriction produced conventional symptoms of hypoglycemia and dehydration without affecting anomalous tissue volume and were subsequently discontinued.

When informed of these findings, SCP-9872-01 asked whether this meant that it was “legally a fruit.”

No determination has been made.

**Addendum 9872.4** — Investigation of SCP-9872:

Investigation into the origin of SCP-9872 began following stabilization of SCP-9872-01. Foundation personnel recovered the instance of SCP-9872 from the waste-disposal trap beneath the kitchen sink. Attempts to reproduce the anomalous properties using chemically identical ingredients were unsuccessful.

SCP-9872-01 purchased the instance of SCP-9872 3 weeks prior to the incident through an online marketplace specializing primarily in secondhand merchandise. The listing was titled:

> THREE COURSE DINNER GUM — AUTHENTIC

The description consisted of a single sentence:

> *“You know what happens.”*

Four photographs accompanied the listing. Three depicted an unwrapped gray stick of chewing gum; the fourth depicted the wrapper later recovered from SCP-9872-01's residence. The product was listed for $30.00, excluding shipping. Investigators initially believed the listing to be a novelty product referencing the fictional confectionery featured in Charlie and the Chocolate Factory. SCP-9872-01 confirmed this was also its interpretation at the time of purchase.

Attempts to trace the seller through transaction and shipping records were unsuccessful. The package's return address corresponds with an undeveloped parcel of land, and no record of the package entering the postal system exists. Customs markings affixed to the package were determined to be authentic. No customs agency contacted by Foundation personnel could confirm having processed the package.

> Note: *Three stamps incorporate insignia belonging to agencies that do not exist. — Dr Mercer.*

SCP-9872-01 attempted to contact the seller seven times during the three-week shipping period. No responses were received. Investigators recovered one additional message that had not appeared in its inbox prior to containment. The message was timestamped approximately fourteen minutes after the affected individual's final Discord transmission.

> *“Satisfied?”*

It denies having seen the message prior to Foundation recovery of the device. Attempts to reply returned an error indicating that the recipient account did not exist.

Further investigation identified an irregularity in the original listing's visibility. Of thirty-one recorded impressions, twenty-seven originated from accounts associated with transformation-fetish communities, including SCP-9872-01. No advertising campaign associated with the listing could be identified. When questioned regarding how it initially encountered the listing, SCP-9872-01 was unable to provide a consistent answer.

**Addendum 9872.5 — Incident Log: 01**

Thirteen days after implementation of automated marketplace monitoring, Foundation systems identified a new listing matching SCP-9872's known characteristics. The listing appeared on a marketplace not previously associated with the investigation. The seller account had been created eleven minutes earlier. The listing read:

> THREE COURSE DINNER GUM — AUTHENTIC

> Price: $30.00

> Available quantity: 4

Foundation personnel attempted to acquire all 4 instances. Each purchase attempt returned an error despite the listing remaining active. Over the following twelve minutes, the available quantity decreased from four to zero. Marketplace administrators subsequently confirmed four completed transactions. None originated from Foundation-controlled accounts. Identification of the purchasers is ongoing.

SCP-9872 has been provisionally reclassified as Euclid due to the anomaly after being identified as a possible manifestation of a broader anomalous distribution phenomenon. Investigation is ongoing. SCP-9872-01 remains the only confirmed living recipient recovered by the Foundation.
