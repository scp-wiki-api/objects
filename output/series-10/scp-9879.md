**Special Containment Procedures:** Dr. Hart is to be denied interaction with SCP-9879 to prevent its usage for "satirical purposes". Under no circumstances should personnel reference Dr. Rayner's odor or Researcher Harris' mother while interacting with SCP-9879. Any attempts to "wake up" SCP-9879 through brusque means are to be reprimanded.

SCP-9879 is to be contained at the center of a 12 x 12 meter standard containment chamber at Site-138. Devices connected to external networks or the internet are prohibited from entering the containment chamber. Personnel are only authorized to interact with SCP-9879 for experimental purposes.

---

**Description:** SCP-9879 is an antique jack-in-the-box dated to the 17th century, measuring 76 centimeters squared whilst docile. Stapled to the lid is a sketch depicting SCP-9879 in its "activated" state, originating from 20th century Spain. The artist's relationship with SCP-9879 is unknown.

SCP-9879 naturally rests in a docile state until a minimum of two humans begin conversing within the object's proximity, assumed to be the extent of the chamber. Approximately 5 to 15 seconds after the conversation began, a piston will push SCP-9879's lid open, subsequently causing a skeleton to emerge. SCP-9879's skeleton is restricted from rising beyond pelvis height, and at its maximum, SCP-9879 can reach 193 centimeters (6 foot 4 inches) tall. SCP-9879's appearance features: 

* A black wide-brimmed hat, dressed with a purple plume feather. 

* A dirty tan cloak, draped across its upper torso and shoulders.

* A golden handbell held in its right hand, approximately 14 centimeters in diameter. 

* A tobacco pipe, which SCP-9879 smokes every two to three minutes using its left hand.

SCP-9879's skeleton appears to be organic and is fully animated, sentient and sapient, despite lacking a nervous system. SCP-9879 is also capable of speech, utilising a mix of both archaic and modern English, spoken with a perceivably nasal voice. SCP-9879 has also displayed knowledge of modern media and the internet.

Once SCP-9879 has activated, it will interject the conversation by ringing its handbell and subsequently announcing a secret held by one of the individuals in its proximity. As of 20██/04/12, SCP-9879 has only announced secrets deemed by Dr. Strauss to be "embarrassing and degrading". The validity of the claims made by SCP-9879 have been disputed by site personnel.

**Addendum 9879.1: Incident 1 — Video Transcript**

**Foreword:** Dr. Hart escorts Dr. Rayner to SCP-9879's containment chamber to conduct an unauthorized interrogation.

---

[RECORDING STARTED]

**Dr. Hart:** —newest on our wing!

**Dr. Rayner:** Right... So, are you actually gonna tell me what this thing does?

.

.

.

*The chamber door slides open and the two enter.*

**Dr. Hart:** Voila!

**Dr. Rayner:** *(suspicious)* You seem way too excited about this...

*The door closes.*

**Dr. Hart:** Oh, you'll see why in a second. Essentially, this SC—

*SCP-9879's lid violently flings open and a bang echoes through the room.*

**Dr. Rayner:** *(startled)* Oh my god!

*SCP-9879 begins to shake its handbell.*

**SCP-9879:** HEAR YE, HEAR YE! GOODNESS... CAN YOU SMELL THAT?

**Dr. Rayner:** *(hesitantly)* Uh, smell what... sir?

**SCP-9879:** OH, MY DEAR! IT APPEARS YOU'VE GONE NOSE BLIND!

**Dr. Rayner:** *(agitated)* Aaron, why did yo—

*SCP-9879 leans toward Dr. Rayner, fanning its hand in front of its nose,  then recoils backwards.*

**SCP-9879:** BY JOVE! IT IS MONSTROUS, AND IT APPEARS *YOU* ARE THE EPICENTER!

**Dr. Rayner:** I— I...

**SCP-9879:** IN MY PROFESSIONAL OPINION: YOU, MY DEAR, HAVE CRAPPED YOUR PANTS!

.

.

.

**Dr. Rayner:** *(stern)* Are you actually serio—

**SCP-9879:** *(snappy)* *CRAPPED,* DARLING! UNDENIABLE AND UNBEARABLE!

*SCP-9879 rattles its bones in condemnation. Dr. Rayner turns to notice Dr. Hart's smirk.*

**Dr. Rayner:** *(agitated)* Is this seriously what you brought me here for!? That was hours ago! The smell's not even on me anymore! How did he ev—

**Dr. Hart:** *(mockingly)* Hours of Hell, and guess what. Turns out it *was* you after all.

.

.

.

**Dr. Rayner:** A— Are you fucking recording this!?

*Dr. Hart turns and immediately heads for the door.*

[RECORDING TERMINATED]

@@ @@

**Aftermath:** Dr. Hart was promptly reprimanded for recording Foundation personnel without consent, as well as for documenting confidential information without prior authorization. As such, Dr. Hart is strictly prohibited from interacting with SCP-9879 for non-essential work. Dr. Rayner has declined the offer to open an investigation onto Dr. Hart and has granted permission for the incident to be logged.

**Addendum 9879.2: Incident 2 — CCTV Video Transcript**

**Foreword:** During a Chaos Insurgency orchestrated containment breach at Site-138, a unit of Insurgents attempt to retrieve SCP-9879.

---

[CCTV LOG STARTED]

*The chamber door slides open. Four Insurgency members file into the chamber.*

.

.

.

*The door closes. The Insurgents surround SCP-9879. One Insurgency member slides a crowbar under SCP-9879's lid and attempts to pry it open.*

.

.

.

*The lid suddenly gives way. SCP-9879 rapidly emerges and bludgeons Insurgent 1's head with its handbell.*

**Insurgent 1:** Oh shit!

*Insurgent 1 falls to the floor. SCP-9879 starts to ring its handbell.*

**SCP-9879:** HEAR YE, HEAR YE! A BUNCH OF BITCHES BE SNATCHIN' ME!

*SCP-9879 leans down; an inch away from Insurgent 1's face.*

**Insurgent 2:** Hey! Get away from him right now!

**SCP-9879:** BUT WHAT ARE YOU GONNA DO TO ME, *BIG BOY?*

*The Insurgents aims their rifles at SCP-9879. SCP-9879 leans its hand on the fallen Insurgent's face.*

**Insurgent 2:** We won't hesitate to shoot if we need to! Get away from him now!

*SCP-9879 rattles its bones condescendingly.*

**SCP-9879:** SHIVER ME TIMBERS! MY DEMISE... AT THE HANDS OF SUCH *TOUGH-GUYS!*

*SCP-9879 rattles its bones mockingly.*

**Insurgent 3:** Ten seconds!

**SCP-9879:** SUCH *HUNKS...* TWO OF WHICH HAVE NEVER KISSED A LADY IN THEIR LIFE!

*The Insurgents fire. A flag labelled 'BANG!' pops out of each rifle's barrel.*

**SCP-9879:** *(dramatically)* OH, THE PAIN, *THE PAIN!*

*SCP-9879 recoils backwards and places its hand on its ribcage.*

**Insurgent 3:** Get him!

*Insurgents 2 and 4 hoist Insurgent 1 up. SCP-9879 begins to smoke.*

.

.

.

**Insurgent 4:** *(irritated)* Please don't tell me the guns are fucked...

*Commotion is heard as MTF Upsilon-11 “Avalon's Wake” approach the containment chamber.*

**Insurgent 3:** *(disbelief)* There's no fucking way... They heard him!?

*SCP-9879 sets its handbell on the floor, resting its skull in its hands while silently observing. Loud crashes reverberate from the chamber's door.*

**U-11 Carter:** *(muffled)* WE KNOW YOU'RE IN THERE! PUT YOUR HANDS UP AND FACE THE WALL!

*The Insurgents frantically glance at one another. One member complies, then the rest.*

.

.

.

*The chamber door opens. MTF agents file into the chamber and restrain the Insurgents. SCP-9879 observes while smoking.*

**U-11 Bale:** *(over radio)* Four Insurgency raiders have been detained. SCP-9879 appears to be cooperative. We're bringing them to you now.

*The MTF agents escort the Insurgents out of the containment chamber. SCP-9879 waves as they leave.*

**SCP-9879:** BEGONE! AND REMEMBER: NEVER FIGHT SOMEONE YOU JUST WOKE UP; IT'LL BE A FIGHT YOU'LL *NEVER* WIN!

*The final MTF agent and Insurgent head for the door. SCP-9879 blows smoke into the Insurgent's face before exiting.*

[END CCTV LOG]

@@ @@

**Aftermath:** After the incident, Dr. Strauss requested to the Site Director to promote SCP-9879 to a risk level of 'Caution', given its capability to harm individuals when provoked. This request has been accepted and no experiments attempting to "wake up" SCP-9879 are to be authorized. After examining the Insurgent's weapons, further investigations have been greenlit to examine SCP-9879's potential ontokinetic abilities.

**Addendum 9879.3: Incident 3 — Video Transcript**

**Foreword:** Dr. Strauss, Researcher Harris and Researcher Brooks enter SCP-9879's containment chamber, tasked to query SCP-9879 on events linked to Incident 2 and how the anomaly retrieves personal information.

---

[RECORDING STARTED]

**Dr. Strauss:** Recording has begun.

**Researcher Brooks:** SCP-9879, are you able to hear us?

.

.

.

**Researcher Brooks:** SCP-9879, if you don't mind, we have some questions for you. It would be our pleasure to talk to you.

.

.

.

**Researcher Harris:** Well, how long is this supposed to ta—

*SCP-9879 springs open and begins to ring its handbell.*

**SCP-9879:** HEAR YE, HEAR YE! A QUESTION FOR ME!?

**Researcher Brooks:** Good afternoon, SCP-9879. Is it permissible to conduct an interview?

**SCP-9879:** OH, SUCH LOVE FOR YOU AND I!

.

.

.

**Researcher Brooks:** I'll interpret that as confirmation... To start, would you be willing to tell us how yo—

**SCP-9879:** SUCH OVERWHELMING INFATUATION FOR ME, YOU COULDN'T *BEAR* BUT TO CALL FOR ME!

.

.

.

**Researcher Brooks:** If you could tell us how you're able to—

**SCP-9879:** FRET NOT! THE LOVE FOR YOU GROWS STRONG, TOO. A SPECIAL SOMEONE, ITCHING TO CALL *YOU...*

*Researcher Harris' phone starts playing George Michael's "Careless Whisper".*

**Researcher Harris:** What the hell?

**Dr. Strauss:** Could you turn that off, please?

**Researcher Harris:** *(flustered)* I... can't. It's opened on YouTube, but I can't pause the vi—

**SCP-9879:** DESIRES SO ALLURING, ONLY A *FOOL* WOULD WANT THEM TO PAUSE! THE THOUGHT OF YOUR OH, SO, PRECIOUS *BETHANY* HAS ENSLAVED YOUR MIND! LOOKS LIKE I WON'T BE THE ONLY ONE RECEIVING A QUESTION TONIGHT...

*Researcher Harris' phone volume increases to the maximum. SCP-9879 begins to dance sensually.*

**Researcher Harris:** *(quietly)* Can I... leave?

*Dr. Strauss nods. Researcher Harris exits, face flushing pink.*

.

.

.

**Dr. Strauss:** Right, so... What was your question, Bethany?

**Researcher Brooks:** *(processing)* I... think we should recuperate first.

*Dr. Strauss sighs.*

**Dr. Strauss:** Very well.

[RECORDING TERMINATED]
