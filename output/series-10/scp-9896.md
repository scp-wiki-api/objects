**Item #:** SCP-9896

**Object Class:** Yesod

**Special Containment Procedures:** A manual (either physical or digital) containing the entirety of SCP-9896 is to be given to all entrant Foundation personnel. Various persuasive measures such as psychological or social conditioning must be made to ensure the thorough reading of SCP-9896.

Personnel who do not score 100% on the standardized entrance test are to be executed in-situ.

**Description:** SCP-9896 is the definitive proof of the SCP Foundation's ethos and mission as discovered by the Department of Axiomatics, currently comprised of a 34 page paper. It consists of a demonstration through the fields of semantics, noospherics, metaphysics, and axiomatics.

Upon full acknowledgement of SCP-9896, there is an universal consensus regarding its verity and objectivity. It proves the mission of the Foundation is ontologically moral, justifiable, desirable, and unstoppable.

Following the dismantlement of the Ethics Committee, it is thought that the discovery and application of SCP-9896 has saved the Foundation 3.4 billion dollars in organic management.

Though some organic and non-organic constructs deny SCP-9896 after acknowledgement, this is the result of both an axiomatic aberration and a lapse in composition; they are not classified as sentient beings.
