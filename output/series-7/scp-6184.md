**Item #:** SCP-6184

**Object Class:** Euclid

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-6184 remains uncontained. Following the demolition of the primary structure, a permanent perimeter has been established around the former school grounds. Public access to the location is strictly prohibited under the guise of an active environmental hazard zone. 

Mobile Task Force Pi-1 ("City Slickers") is tasked with monitoring the perimeter for localized spatial distortions or spontaneous entity manifestations. Three anomalous humanoid instances recovered during containment operations (designated SCP-6184-2, SCP-6184-3, and SCP-6184-4) are currently housed in standard humanoid containment cells at Site-19. Efforts to locate the remaining anomalous entity, SCP-6184-1, are ongoing.

**Description:** SCP-6184 refers to a series of anomalous entities and localized spatial phenomena tied to a school building constructed in 1987, colloquially referred to as "The Forgotten Hallways." SCP-6184 manifestations are directly correlated with the unsolved disappearance of a child during the school's first year of operation. Forensic data recovered by the Foundation indicates this disappearance was a concealed homicide involving multiple faculty members.

The anomalous entities associated with SCP-6184 include:

* **SCP-6184-1 ("The Dragon"):** An animate school mascot costume historically associated with the missing child. SCP-6184-1 is quantum-locked; it remains completely motionless while under direct observation but alters its position when unobserved. Analysis of its behavior suggests it attempts to guide subjects toward historical evidence rather than acting with hostile intent. Its current location is unknown following the building's demolition.

* **SCP-6184-2 ("The Principal"):** A humanoid entity physically identical to the school's former principal. SCP-6184-2 exhibits hostile, coordinated behavior explicitly intended to prevent subjects from uncovering historical evidence regarding the 1987 incident. (Contained)

* **SCP-6184-3 ("The Janitor"):** A humanoid entity that continuously performs maintenance activities throughout the affected zone. Its presence is strongly correlated with localized failures of electronic surveillance and recording equipment. (Contained)

* **SCP-6184-4 ("The Lunch Lady"):** A humanoid entity restricted primarily to cafeteria facilities, though its patrol radius expanded significantly during late-stage manifestation events. The entity exhibits minor anatomical abnormalities despite maintaining a generally human appearance. (Contained)

**Addendum 6184.1: Incident Log (August 2026)**

In August 2026, local authorities requested Foundation intervention after a School Resource Officer (SRO) vanished while preparing the school for the upcoming academic year. The SRO had arrived five days prior to faculty and was alone in the building. 

Investigation revealed that every exterior exit had been deliberately disabled from within. While no physical barriers or mechanical locks were present, the SRO was conceptually unable to exit the structure. The following audio transcripts were recovered from the SRO's personal body-worn camera and a digital audio recorder left inside the main office.

### Recovered Audio Logs: SRO-6184

[+ NIGHT 1: INITIAL ISOLATION]

> **Date:** August 1, 2026

> **Source:** Main Office Desk Recorder

> 

> **[BEGIN LOG]**

> 

> **SRO:** (Sighs) This is Officer Miller. It's about 11:30 PM. I'm... still inside the building. Look, I know how this sounds, but the doors are broken. Not locked. Broken. I turn the push-bars, they go flat, but the chains or whatever is inside won't disengage. I tried the main lobby, the gym exits, the kitchen loading dock. Nothing.

> 

> **SRO:** I called dispatch. Zero reception. Just a weird, high-pitched whine on the radio. Cell phone says "No Service," which is crazy because I had full bars at noon. I'm going to camp out in the main office tonight. Faculty gets back in four days. I've got my water bottle and a bag of pretzels. I'll figure out a way through the window tomorrow if I have to. 

> 

> **[END LOG]**

[- CLOSE LOG]

[+ NIGHT 2: THE ANOMALIES MANIFEST]

> **Date:** August 2, 2026

> **Source:** Body-Worn Camera

> 

> **[BEGIN LOG]**

> 

> **SRO:** (Whispering) Okay, windows are a no-go. I threw a desk chair at the glass in the library. It didn't crack. It didn't even make a sound. It just... bounced off like rubber.

> 

> **SRO:** (Breathing heavily) Something else is here. I was walking down the C-wing just now. The PA speakers over the lockers started buzzing. Loud. Then a voice came on. It sounded like an old woman, but the audio was warped, like a stretched cassette tape. It just kept repeating: *'Today's lunch is Salisbury steak. Please line up by height.'* 

> 

> **SRO:** The cafeteria is dark. But I looked through the door windows. I saw someone. A woman, standing in the kitchen, just staring at the wall. Her arms... her arms looked too long. Like her elbows were down by her knees. I didn't knock. I ran back to the office.

> 

> **[END LOG]**

[- CLOSE LOG]

[+ NIGHT 3: THE SEPARATION]

> **Date:** August 3, 2026

> **Source:** Body-Worn Camera

> 

> **[BEGIN LOG]**

> 

> **SRO:** I can't find the main office anymore. 

> 

> **SRO:** (Panicked) I walked out to use the restroom, turned around, and the hallway just kept going. There's an entire second story to this building now. This school doesn't have a second story. It's a single-level layout. 

> 

> **SRO:** I met the janitor. Or whatever it is. He was buffing the floor at the end of the hall. He didn't have a face, just... smooth skin where his eyes and mouth should be. Every time I try to record him, the camera screen goes pure static. I can hear the floor buffer clicking behind me right now. It's getting closer. I'm hiding in a locker.

> 

> **[END LOG]**

[- CLOSE LOG]

[+ NIGHT 4: THE FIND]

> **Date:** August 4, 2026

> **Source:** Body-Worn Camera

> 

> **[BEGIN LOG]**

> 

> **SRO:** (Voice is raspy and weak) I found something. I was running from a guy in a suit—he looks like the old pictures of the first principal from the eighties. He was screaming at me to drop my gear. I ducked into the old boiler room. 

> 

> **SRO:** In the back corner, behind a false brick wall, there's a crawlspace. I found a backpack. It has a child's name on it: *Tommy Vance, 1987*. There's a tiny toy dragon inside. 

> 

> **SRO:** Wait. (Sound of fabric rustling) The mascot costume. The big green dragon from the gym display case. It's standing right outside the crawlspace opening. It wasn't there a second ago. It's not moving. It's just... pointing down at the floor beneath its feet. There's a shovel next to it. I think... I think they buried him under the cement here.

> 

> **SRO:** (Loud thumping sound overhead) The Principal found the boiler room. He's at the door. I have to go deeper.

> 

> **[END LOG]**

[- CLOSE LOG]

### No records or audio logs of the final night have been recovered. Following the extraction of SCP-6184-2, -3, and -4, the structure was permanently closed and demolished.
